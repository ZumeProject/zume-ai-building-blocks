## Core Deity Terms

### Jesus
**German:** Jesus
**Genitive:** Jesu (used with following genitive noun)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "How to Follow Jesus" → "Wie man Jesus nachfolgt"
2. "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "Was ist ein Nachfolger Jesu?"
3. "Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus" → "Mit Leuten, DIE DU KENNST, über Jesus reden"
4. "Becoming more like Jesus... Together" → "Jesus ähnlicher werden... Gemeinsam"
5. "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "Lernt, wie wir Jesu Auftrag ausführen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Jesus (nominative/accusative): "Jesus nachfolgen" (to follow Jesus)
- Jesu (genitive): "Nachfolger Jesu" (follower of Jesus), "Jesu Auftrag" (Jesus' command)
- The genitive form "Jesu" is used in traditional/biblical contexts rather than the modern "Jesus'"

### Christ / Messiah
**German:** Christus (Christ), Messias (Messiah)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Body of Christ" → "Leib Christi"
2. "through the death, burial and resurrection of Christ" → "durch den Tod, das Begräbnis und die Auferstehung Christi"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Christus (nominative), Christi (genitive)
- Used in formal theological contexts
- Often appears in compound phrases like "Leib Christi"

### God
**German:** Gott
**Article:** der (masculine)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "God Uses Ordinary People" → "Gott gebraucht ganz normale Menschen"
2. "Hearing and Obeying God" → "Gott hören und ihm gehorchen"
3. "What do you learn about God?" → "Was lernt ihr über Gott?"
4. "God's Kingdom" → "Gottes Königreich" / "Reich Gottes"
5. "God makes plans" → "Gott macht Pläne"
6. "God's Holy Spirit" → "Gottes Heiliger Geist"
7. "Sharing God's Story" → "Gottes Geschichte weitergeben"
8. "God's voice" → "Gottes Stimme"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Gott (nominative/accusative)
- Gottes (genitive): "Gottes Wort" (God's Word), "Gottes Königreich" (God's Kingdom)
- Gott (dative): "von Gott" (from God), "zu Gott" (to God)
- The dative "ihm" (to him) is used when referring back to God: "Gott hören und ihm gehorchen"

### Holy Spirit
**German:** Heiliger Geist / Heiligen Geist (declined form)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together" → "Bitte Gottes Heiligen Geist, eure gemeinsame Zeit zu leiten"
2. "without the Holy Spirit, spiritual understanding and transformation is not possible" → "Ohne den Heiligen Geist ist geistliche Erkenntnis und Transformation nicht möglich"
3. "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" → "im Namen des Vaters und des Sohnes und des Heiligen Geistes"
4. "ask His Holy Spirit to lead your time, together" → "Bittet Gottes Heiligen Geist, eure gemeinsame Zeit zu leiten"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Nominative: "der Heilige Geist"
- Accusative: "den Heiligen Geist" (direct object)
- Genitive: "des Heiligen Geistes"
- Dative: "dem Heiligen Geist"
- Full declension of adjective "heilig" + noun "Geist"

### Spirit / Spiritual / Spirituality
**German:** Geist (spirit), geistlich (spiritual), Geistlichkeit (spirituality - rarely used)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Spiritual Breathing" → "Geistliches Atmen"
2. "spiritual economy" → "Die Weltordnung Gottes" / "Gottes Weltordnung"
3. "Keys to Spiritual Growth" → "Schlüssel zu geistlichem Wachstum"
4. "spiritual family" → "geistliche Familie"
5. "Spiritual Practices" → "Geistliche Übungen"
6. "spiritual generations" → "geistliche Generationen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "geistlich" = spiritual (adjective, declined according to gender/case)
- "Geist" alone can mean spirit/mind; needs context for "Holy Spirit"
- Common compounds: "geistliche Familie" (spiritual family), "geistliches Wachstum" (spiritual growth)

### Lord
**German:** Herr (der Herr)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "The Lord's Supper" → "Das Abendmahl"
2. "the Lord leads you" → "der Herr dich führt" / "der Herr dir eingibt"
3. "Ask the Lord" → "Bitte den Herrn"
4. "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "Feiert das Abendmahl"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Herr (nominative), den Herrn (accusative), des Herrn (genitive), dem Herrn (dative)
- "The Lord's Supper" is idiomatically "Das Abendmahl" (the evening meal), not a literal translation
- Used both for Jesus and God the Father in different contexts

### Father (God the Father)
**German:** Vater

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" → "im Namen des Vaters und des Sohnes und des Heiligen Geistes"
2. "God the Father" → "Gott der Vater"
3. "focused on the work of the Father" → "immer auf das Werk des Vaters fokussiert"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Vater (nominative/accusative), des Vaters (genitive), dem Vater (dative)
- Capitalized when referring to God the Father
- Also used for human fathers (not capitalized)

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
**German:** Jünger (masculine)
**Plural:** Jünger (same form)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "What's a Disciple?" → "Was ist ein Jünger?"
2. "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "Einfache Definition von Jünger und Gemeinde"
3. "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "Jünger zu sein bedeutet, dass wir Gott hören und ihm gehorchen"
4. "Making Disciples" → "Jünger machen"
5. "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" → "Trainingszyklus um Jünger in die Reife zu führen"
6. "trained multiplying disciple" → "geschulter, sich vervielfältigender Jünger"
7. "Empowering Disciples" → "Jünger befähigen"

#### Variations:
- "der Jünger" (singular)
- "die Jünger" (plural - same spelling)
- No separate feminine form; women disciples are also "Jünger"

### Disciple-making / Discipleship
**German:** Jüngermachen (disciple-making), Jüngerschaft (discipleship)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Duckling Discipleship" → "Entenküken-Jüngerschaft"
2. "what ducklings have to do with disciple-making" → "was Entenküken mit Jüngermachen zu tun haben"
3. "disciple-making doesn't have to be linear" → "Jüngermachen muss nicht linear ablaufen"
4. "help with disciple-making" → "beim Jüngermachen helfen"
5. "Discipleship Concepts" → "Jüngerschaftskonzepte"
6. "multiplying discipleship" → "multiplizierende Jüngerschaft"
7. "the fundamentals and principles of multiplying discipleship" → "die Grundlagen und Prinzipien von sich multiplizierender Jüngerschaft"
8. "practical training in starting simple churches and disciple-making" → "praktisches Training im Gründen einfacher Gemeinden und Jüngermachen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "Jüngermachen" is a compound noun (infinitive as noun)
- "Jüngerschaft" is an abstract noun denoting the state/practice of discipleship
- Both are neuter gender (das Jüngermachen, die Jüngerschaft)

### Follower (of Jesus)
**German:** Nachfolger (masculine)
**Verb:** nachfolgen (to follow)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "Was ist ein Nachfolger Jesu?"
2. "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" → "Was tut ein Nachfolger Jesu?"
3. "help others become followers with me" → "anderen dabei helfen, gemeinsam mit mir zu Nachfolgern zu werden"
4. "every follower of Jesus" → "jeder Nachfolger Jesu"
5. "followers of Jesus meet, pray, learn, grow" → "Nachfolger Jesu treffen sich, beten, lernen, wachsen"
6. "How to Follow Jesus" → "Wie man Jesus nachfolgt"
7. "Follow/Following Jesus" → "Jesus nachfolgen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Nachfolger (singular), Nachfolger (plural - same form)
- Genitive: "Nachfolger Jesu" (follower of Jesus)
- Verb: "nachfolgen" (separable prefix verb): "ich folge Jesus nach"
- Can have feminine form "Nachfolgerin" but often generic masculine is used

### Believer
**German:** Gläubiger (masculine), Gläubige (as adjective/noun)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Share the Training Cycle with a believer on your list of 100" → "Teile den Trainingszyklus mit einem Gläubigen auf deiner 100er-Liste"
2. "with another believer you know" → "mit einem anderen Gläubigen, den du kennst"
3. "ordinary believers" → "gewöhnliche Gläubige" / "einfache Gläubige"
4. "if your family are believers" → "wenn deine Familie gläubig ist"
5. "equip ordinary believers" → "gewöhnliche Gläubige ausrüsten"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Can be used as adjective: "gläubig" (believing)
- As noun: "der/die Gläubige" (declined as adjective)
- "ein Gläubiger" (a male believer), "eine Gläubige" (a female believer)
- Plural: "Gläubige"

### Obey / Obedience
**German:** gehorchen (verb - to obey), Gehorsam (noun - obedience)
**Past Participle:** gehorcht

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Hearing and Obeying God" → "Gott hören und ihm gehorchen"
2. "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "Jünger zu sein bedeutet, dass wir Gott hören und ihm gehorchen"
3. "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "Lernt, wie wir Jesu Auftrag ausführen" (more dynamic: "carry out Jesus' commission")
4. "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus" → "Anderen beibringen, Jesus zu gehorchen und ihm zu folgen"
5. "Obey" → "Gehorche" (imperative)
6. "Obeyed" → "Gehorcht" (past participle used in evaluation)
7. "Heard, Obeyed, Shared, Trained" → "Gehört, Gehorcht, Geteilt, Trainiert"
8. "The Power of Obedient Faith" → "Die Kraft des gehorsamen Glaubens"
9. "practice obeying and passing on what they learn" → "üben, zu gehorchen und das Gelernte weiterzugeben"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "gehorchen" takes dative object: "Gott gehorchen" (to obey God), "ihm gehorchen" (to obey him)
- Noun: "der Gehorsam" (obedience)
- Adjective: "gehorsam" (obedient)
- Alternative verb: "befolgen" (to follow/comply with rules/commands)

### Follow / Following
**German:** folgen (to follow), nachfolgen (to follow after/be a disciple)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Follow Jesus" → "Jesus nachfolgen"
2. "How to Follow Jesus" → "Wie man Jesus nachfolgt"
3. "lead and follow simultaneously" → "führen an und folgen gleichzeitig nach"
4. "obey and follow Jesus" → "gehorchen und Jesus folgen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "folgen" + dative: "Jesus folgen" (general following)
- "nachfolgen" + dative: "Jesus nachfolgen" (discipleship following, more committed)
- Both are intransitive verbs requiring dative object

## Church Terms

### Church
**German:** Gemeinde (f) [preferred in evangelical contexts], Kirche (f) [institutional/building]

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "What's a Church?" → "Was ist eine Gemeinde?"
2. "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "Einfache Definition von Jünger und Gemeinde"
3. "Disciples and the Church" → "Jünger und die Gemeinde"
4. "Leading a Simple Church" → "Eine einfache Gemeinde leiten"
5. "My Churches" → "Meine Gemeinden"
6. "Church Name" → "Name der Gemeinde"
7. "Church Location" → "Standort der Gemeinde"
8. "Parent Church" → "Muttergemeinde"
9. "Always Part of Two Churches" → "Immer Teil zweier Gemeinden sein"

#### Terminology Notes:
**Gemeinde vs. Kirche:**
- **Gemeinde** = congregation, community of believers, local church (preferred in evangelical/charismatic circles)
- **Kirche** = church institution, church building, denominational church
- In the PO file, "Gemeinde" is consistently used for simple churches and church planting contexts
- "Kirche" appears in statistics/reports: "Benötigte Kirchen" (Churches Needed), "Gemeldete Kirchen" (Churches Reported)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Churches Needed" → "Benötigte Kirchen"
2. "Churches Reported" → "Gemeldete Kirchen"
3. "2 Simple Churches" → "2 Einfache Hausgemeinden"
4. "simple churches" → "einfache Gemeinden" / "einfache Hausgemeinden"
5. "When you think of a church, what comes to mind?" → "Wenn ihr an eine Gemeinde denkt, was geht euch dann durch den Kopf?"

### Simple Church
**German:** einfache Gemeinde, einfache Hausgemeinde

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Leading a Simple Church" → "Eine einfache Gemeinde leiten"
2. "Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church?" → "Ist eine 3/3-Gruppe eine einfache Gemeinde?"
3. "In this way, a 3/3 Group is not just a small group, but a simple church" → "Auf diese Weise ist eine 3/3-Gruppe nicht nur eine Kleingruppe, sondern eine einfache Gemeinde"
4. "2 Simple Churches" → "2. Einfache Hausgemeinden"
5. "multiplying simple churches" → "sich vervielfältigende einfache Gemeinden"
6. "start simple churches" → "einfache Gemeinden gründen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "einfach" = simple (adjective, declined)
- "Hausgemeinde" = house church (compound: Haus + Gemeinde)
- Plural: "einfache Gemeinden"

### Congregation
**German:** Gemeinde, Versammlung

#### Notes:
- "Gemeinde" serves double duty as both "church" and "congregation"
- "Versammlung" = assembly/gathering (more formal, less common in evangelical contexts)
- The concept is typically expressed as "Gemeinde" in this translation

### Assembly
**German:** Versammlung (f)

#### Notes:
- Not commonly used in the PO file
- "Gemeinde" is preferred for church gatherings
- "Versammlung" is more formal/institutional

### Body of Christ
**German:** Leib Christi

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Pray and thank God for the Body of Christ" → "Betet und dankt Gott für den Leib Christi"
2. "united through the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ" → "durch den Tod, das Begräbnis und die Auferstehung Christi vereint zu sein"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "Leib" = body (masculine noun)
- Genitive: "des Leibes Christi" or "Leib Christi"
- Traditional theological term used across denominations

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
**German:** Evangelium (n)
**Article:** das

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "The Gospel" → "Das Evangelium"
2. "The Gospel and How to Share It" → "Das Evangelium weitergeben"
3. "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" → "3-Kreise-Evangeliumspräsentation"
4. "Share the Gospel" → "Gib das Evangelium weiter"
5. "Take turns telling the Gospel to each other" → "Erzählt einander abwechselnd das Evangelium"
6. "Sharing the Gospel" → "Das Evangelium weitergeben"
7. "Practice Sharing the Gospel" → "Übe, das Evangelium weiterzugeben"
8. "How could a person of peace accelerate the spread of the gospel?" → "Wie könnte eine Person des Friedens die Ausbreitung des Evangeliums beschleunigen?"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Genitive: "des Evangeliums"
- Dative: "dem Evangelium"
- From Greek εὐαγγέλιον (euangelion = good news)
- Compound words: "Evangeliumspräsentation" (gospel presentation)

### Good News
**German:** gute Nachricht (f)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "share the good news of the Kingdom of God" → "teilt die gute Nachricht vom Reich Gottes"
2. Often expressed as "Evangelium" rather than literal "gute Nachricht"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "gut" (good) + "Nachricht" (news/message)
- While literally accurate, "Evangelium" is more common in religious contexts
- Declined: "die gute Nachricht", "der guten Nachricht" (dative)

### Bible
**German:** Bibel (f)
**Article:** die

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" → "S.O.A.P.S. Bibelstudium"
2. "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading" → "S.O.A.P.S.-Bibelstudium"
3. "Bible Reading" → "Bibellesen"
4. "Personal Bible Study" → "Persönliches Bibelstudium"
5. "read the Bible / understand the Bible" → "Bibel lesen/verstehen"
6. "The Bible tells us" → "Die Bibel sagt uns"
7. "faithful attendance at Bible studies and church services" → "treu an Bibelstunden und Gottesdiensten teilgenommen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- No article needed in compounds: "Bibelstudium" (not "Bibel-Studium")
- "Bibellesen" = Bible reading (compound noun)
- "Bibelstunden" = Bible study sessions (plural)

### Scripture / Word (Word of God)
**German:** Schrift (f), Bibel (f), Wort Gottes (n)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Scripture" → "Die Bibel"
2. "according to Scripture" → "der Schrift entspricht"
3. "God's Word" → "Gottes Wort"
4. "understand, apply and pass on God's Word" → "Gottes Wort zu verstehen, umzusetzen und weiterzugeben"
5. "promises in His Word" → "in seinem Wort verspricht"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "Schrift" = Scripture (traditional term, often used in theological contexts)
- "die Heilige Schrift" = Holy Scripture
- "Gottes Wort" = God's Word (neuter noun)
- Compounds: "Bibelstudium" (Bible study)

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism / Baptize
**German:** Taufe (f) [noun], taufen [verb]

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "Baptism and How To Do It" → "Taufe und wie das geht"
2. "Baptism" → "Taufe"
3. "go and make disciples... BAPTIZING them" → "Geht und macht alle Völker zu Jüngern und TAUFT sie"
4. "baptize them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" → "tauft sie im Namen des Vaters und des Sohnes und des Heiligen Geistes"
5. "If the Great Commission applies to every follower of Jesus, does that mean every follower is allowed to baptize others?" → "Wenn der Missionsbefehl für jeden Nachfolger Jesu gilt, bedeutet das, dass jeder Nachfolger andere taufen darf?"
6. "IMPORTANT REMINDER – Have you been baptized?" → "WICHTIGE FRAGE – Hast du dich taufen lassen?"
7. "If you know someone who follows Jesus but has not yet been baptized, challenge them to do so and offer to baptize them" → "Wenn du jemanden kennst, der Jesus nachfolgt, aber noch nicht getauft ist, fordere ihn heraus dies zu tun, und biete an, ihn zu taufen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Noun: "die Taufe" (baptism)
- Verb: "taufen" (to baptize)
- Passive construction: "getauft werden" (to be baptized)
- Reflexive: "sich taufen lassen" (to have oneself baptized, more common)

### Communion / Lord's Supper
**German:** Abendmahl (n), Herrenmahl (n - rare)

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "The Lord's Supper" → "Das Abendmahl"
2. "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" → "Das Abendmahl leiten"
3. "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "Feiert das Abendmahl"
4. "Spend the next 10 minutes celebrating the Lord's Supper as a group" → "Verbringt die nächsten 10 Minuten damit, in eurer Gruppe das Abendmahl zu feiern"

#### Grammar Notes:
- "das Abendmahl" = the Lord's Supper (lit. "evening meal")
- This is the standard Protestant German term for communion
- Capitalized as it refers to the specific sacrament
- Catholics might use "Eucharistie" (Eucharist) or "Kommunion" (Communion)
- Lutheran tradition strongly influenced this terminology

### Worship
**German:** Anbetung (f), Gottesdienst (m - church service)

#### Notes:
- "Anbetung" = worship (act of worshiping)
- "Gottesdienst" = worship service, church service
- Not heavily featured in the PO file; the concept appears more in practice than in direct terminology

#### Grammar Notes:
- "das Zeugnis" (testimony, witness)
- Plural: "die Zeugnisse"
- Related verb: "bezeugen" (to testify, to witness)
- Compounds: "Drei-Minuten-Zeugnis" (three-minute testimony)

### Witness (verb and noun)
**German:** Zeuge (m) [noun - witness], bezeugen [verb - to witness/testify]

#### Contextual Examples:
1. "you will be my witnesses" → "ihr werdet meine Zeugen sein"
2. "we testify about the impact Jesus has had" → "wir bezeugen, welchen Einfluss Jesus auf unser Leben hatte"
3. "God's call to be His 'witnesses' and tell others about Him" → "Gottes Auftrag, seine 'Zeugen' zu sein und von Ihm zu erzählen"

#### Grammar Notes:
- Noun: "der Zeuge" (witness), die Zeugen (plural)
- Feminine: "die Zeugin" (female witness)
- Verb: "bezeugen" (to testify, to witness)
- Related: "Zeugnis" (testimony)

## Salvation Terms

### Salvation / Save / Saved
**German:** Rettung (f) [salvation], retten [save], gerettet [saved]

#### Notes:
- "Rettung" = salvation, rescue
- "retten" = to save, to rescue
- "gerettet" = saved (past participle)
- These terms are less prominent in the PO file, which focuses more on discipleship than conversion terminology
- "Save" appears mainly in UI context: "Save" → "Speichern" (save data)

### Repent / Repentance
**German:** umkehren (verb), Umkehr (noun), Buße tun (traditional)

#### Notes:
- "umkehren" = to turn around, to repent
- "Umkehr" = repentance, turning around

## Cultural and Linguistic Notes

### German Language Characteristics in Religious Translation

#### 1. Compound Words
German freely creates compound nouns, which are common in this translation:
- **Jüngermachen** (disciple-making)
- **Entenküken-Jüngerschaft** (duckling discipleship)
- **Trainingszyklus** (training cycle)
- **Leiterschaftszellen** (leadership cells)
- **Gebetsspaziergang** (prayer walking)
- **Generationenkarte** (generational mapping)
- **Hausgemeinde** (house church)

These compounds are written as single words without hyphens (except for clarity or when one element is a foreign word or acronym).

#### 2. Case System
German has four cases (nominative, accusative, dative, genitive), affecting articles and adjectives:
- **Nominative:** "der Heilige Geist" (the Holy Spirit - subject)
- **Accusative:** "den Heiligen Geist" (the Holy Spirit - direct object)
- **Genitive:** "des Heiligen Geistes" (of the Holy Spirit)
- **Dative:** "dem Heiligen Geist" (to the Holy Spirit)

#### 3. Genitive Construction
German commonly uses genitive for possession, but in religious contexts, the older/biblical genitive form is often preferred:
- **Modern:** "Jesus' Nachfolger" (Jesus' followers)
- **Biblical/Traditional:** "Nachfolger Jesu" (followers of Jesus)
- **Modern:** "Gottes Königreich" (God's kingdom)
- **Alternative:** "das Königreich Gottes" (the kingdom of God)

#### 4. Formal vs. Informal Address
German distinguishes between formal "Sie" and informal "du":
- The Zúme translation consistently uses **informal "du/ihr"** (you singular/plural)
- This creates a warm, personal, inclusive tone appropriate for discipleship training
- Reflects modern evangelical practice of addressing believers informally
- Commands use informal imperative: "Gehorche" (obey), "Teile" (share), "Betet" (pray)

### Lutheran Reformation Influence

#### 1. Martin Luther's Bible Translation Legacy
Many terms in this translation reflect Luther's 1545/1984 Bible:
- **"Abendmahl"** (Lord's Supper) - Luther's term, emphasizing the evening meal aspect
- **"Reich Gottes"** (Kingdom of God) - Luther's translation
- **"Heiliger Geist"** (Holy Spirit) - Luther's formulation
- **"Nachfolger"** (follower) - from "nachfolgen" (to follow after)

#### 2. Protestant Vocabulary Choices
The translation shows clear Protestant/evangelical preferences:
- **"Gemeinde"** (congregation/church) preferred over "Kirche" (institutional church)
- **"Gläubige"** (believers) emphasized over "Christen" (Christians)
- **"Jünger"** (disciples) central rather than "Mitglieder" (members)
- Emphasis on **personal relationship** language over institutional language

### Catholic vs. Protestant Terminology

#### Protestant (Used in This Translation):
- **Gemeinde** (local congregation)
- **Abendmahl** (Lord's Supper)
- **Bibel** (Bible)
- **Gläubige** (believers)
- **taufen** (to baptize - simple verb)

#### Catholic (Not Used Here):
- **Kirche** (church institution)
- **Eucharistie** (Eucharist)
- **Heilige Schrift** (Holy Scripture - more formal)
- **Gläubigen** (the faithful)
- **die Taufe spenden** (to administer baptism - more formal)

### Modern Evangelical vs. Traditional Church Language

#### Modern Evangelical (Used in Translation):
- **Jüngermachen** (making disciples - active, missional)
- **einfache Gemeinde** (simple church)
- **geistliche Familie** (spiritual family)
- **Nachfolger Jesu** (followers of Jesus - personal relationship)
- **multiplizieren / sich vervielfältigen** (to multiply - growth emphasis)
- **Coach** (coach - English loanword)
- **Training** (training - English loanword)

#### Traditional Church Language (Less Common):
- **Gemeindeaufbau** (church building/development)
- **Kirchengemeinde** (parish)
- **Gemeinde Christi** (Church of Christ - more formal)
- **Christen** (Christians - institutional identity)
- **Wachstum** alone (growth - more static)
- **Seelsorger** (pastor/spiritual counselor - traditional term)
- **Schulung** (training - traditional German)

### East/West Germany Post-Reunification Vocabulary

The translation uses **West German evangelical vocabulary**, which became standard after reunification:
- Modern English loanwords freely used: **Training, Coach, Mentor, Team**
- Emphasis on **individual agency and choice** (characteristic of Western evangelicalism)
- **Informal, relational language** rather than institutional structures
- **"Gläubige"** (believers) preferred over East German secular alternatives

East Germany had limited active Christian vocabulary development during the DDR period (1949-1990), so modern German evangelical language is predominantly Western in origin.

### Distinctive Zúme Translation Choices

#### 1. "Spiritual Economy"
- English: "Spiritual Economy"
- German: **"Die Weltordnung Gottes"** (God's world order) or **"Gottes Weltordnung"**
- This is a creative cultural adaptation rather than literal translation

#### 2. "Simple Church"
- English: "Simple Church"
- German: **"einfache Gemeinde"** or **"einfache Hausgemeinde"**
- "Hausgemeinde" (house church) adds clarification
- Sometimes appears as **"2 Einfache Hausgemeinden"** (2 Simple House Churches)

#### 3. "Duckling Discipleship"
- English: "Duckling Discipleship"
- German: **"Entenküken-Jüngerschaft"**
- Playful compound maintains the metaphor
- Shows German's flexibility with creative compounds

#### 4. "Person of Peace"
- English: "Person of Peace"
- German: **"Person des Friedens"**
- Technical term in church planting, translated literally
- Maintains theological concept from Luke 10

#### 5. Multiplication Language
The translation uses **two main terms** for multiplication:
- **"multiplizieren"** - more mathematical/abstract
- **"sich vervielfältigen"** - more organic/reproductive (preferred in spiritual contexts)
- Both appear, with "sich vervielfältigen" more common for disciples and churches

### Grammar Patterns in Religious Translation

#### 1. Infinitive as Noun (Substantivierter Infinitiv)
German readily turns infinitives into neuter nouns:
- **Jüngermachen** (disciple-making) from "Jünger machen"
- **Bibellesen** (Bible reading) from "Bibel lesen"
- **Vermitteln** (conveying/teaching) from "vermitteln"

#### 2. Present Participle as Adjective
Used for ongoing action descriptions:
- **sich vervielfältigender Jünger** (multiplying disciple)
- **sich vervielfältigende Gemeinden** (multiplying churches)
- Fully declined: "geschulter, sich vervielfältigender Jünger"

#### 3. Separable Prefix Verbs
Common in German, these split in conjugation:
- **nachfolgen** (to follow after): "ich folge Jesus nach"
- **weitergeben** (to pass on): "das Evangelium weitergeben"
- **durchführen** (to carry out): "Jesu Auftrag ausführen"

#### 4. Reflexive Constructions
More common in German than English:
- **sich vervielfältigen** (to multiply itself)
- **sich treffen** (to meet - lit. "to meet oneself")
- **sich fortpflanzen** (to reproduce)

### Practical Translation Patterns

#### 1. "To Share" Has Multiple Translations
Context determines the German verb:
- **teilen** (to share/divide): "Teile dein Zeugnis" (Share your testimony)
- **weitergeben** (to pass on): "das Evangelium weitergeben" (share the gospel)
- **erzählen** (to tell): "Gottes Geschichte erzählen" (share God's story)
- **mitteilen** (to communicate): less common in this context

#### 2. "To Make Disciples"
- **Jünger machen** (to make disciples - simple, active)
- **zu Jüngern machen** (to make into disciples): "macht alle Völker zu Jüngern"
- **Jüngermachen** (disciple-making - noun form)

#### 3. "To Lead"
Multiple verbs depending on context:
- **leiten** (to lead/guide): "eine Gemeinde leiten" (lead a church)
- **führen** (to lead/conduct): "in die Reife führen" (lead to maturity)
- **anführen** (to lead/command): "führen an" (lead - ducklings)

#### 4. Commands and Instructions
German uses various forms for instructions:
- **Imperative:** "Gehorche!" (Obey!), "Teile!" (Share!)
- **Infinitive:** "Gottes Geschichte weitergeben" (To share God's story)
- **Passive with modal:** "sollte geteilt werden" (should be shared)
- **First person plural:** "Lernt" (Learn - you all)

### Theological Nuances

#### 1. "Obedience" Language
German has strong obedience vocabulary:
- **gehorchen** (to obey - with dative object)
- **Gehorsam** (obedience - noun)
- **gehorsam** (obedient - adjective)
- **befolgen** (to comply with/follow - for rules/commands)
- "Jesu Auftrag ausführen" = to carry out Jesus' commission (more active than "obey")

#### 2. "Church" Terminology Strategy
The translation carefully distinguishes:
- **Gemeinde** = local congregation, church community (spiritual emphasis)
- **Kirche** = church building, church institution (structural emphasis)
- **Hausgemeinde** = house church (specific model)
- **einfache Gemeinde** = simple church (Zúme model)

#### 3. "Kingdom" Language
Two main forms with subtle differences:
- **Reich Gottes** = Kingdom of God (biblical, traditional)
- **Königreich Gottes** = Kingdom of God (emphasizes royal rule)
- **Himmelreich** = Kingdom of Heaven (lit. "heaven's realm")

#### 4. "Spirit" vs. "Holy Spirit"
- **Geist** alone = spirit (general)
- **Heiliger Geist** = Holy Spirit (always with adjective)
- **Gottes Heiliger Geist** = God's Holy Spirit (full form)
- **geistlich** = spiritual (adjective)

### Stylistic Observations

#### 1. Warmth and Accessibility
The translation maintains a **warm, accessible tone**:
- Consistent use of informal **"du/ihr"** (you)
- Active, encouraging verbs: "Entdeckt" (Discover), "Lernt" (Learn)
- Personal pronouns: "dein Zeugnis" (your testimony)
- Direct address: "Was tut ein Nachfolger Jesu?" (What does a follower of Jesus do?)

#### 2. Action Orientation
Emphasis on **doing** and **practice**:
- **"Übe"** (Practice) - frequently used
- **"trainieren"** (to train)
- **"umsetzen"** (to implement, to put into practice)
- **"weitergeben"** (to pass on)
- **"ausführen"** (to carry out)

#### 3. Relational Language
Strong emphasis on **relationships** and **community**:
- **"geistliche Familie"** (spiritual family)
- **"gemeinsam"** (together)
- **"miteinander"** (with one another)
- **"einander"** (one another) - frequently used
- **"Gemeinschaft"** (community, fellowship)

#### 4. Movement Language
Dynamic vocabulary of **multiplication** and **movement**:
- **"sich vervielfältigen"** (to multiply)
- **"multiplizieren"** (to multiply)
- **"wachsen"** (to grow)
- **"ausbreiten"** (to spread)
- **"durchsäuern"** (to leaven, to permeate)
- **"überfluten"** (to flood)
- **"sättigen"** (to saturate)
