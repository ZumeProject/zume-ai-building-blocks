# Korean (ko_KR) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference Guide

## Overview

This guide documents how key spiritual and ministry terms are translated from English to Korean in the Zume training materials. It serves as a reference for translators and coaches to maintain consistency and understand the theological and cultural choices made in the Korean translation.

**Language:** Korean (한국어)
**Language Code:** ko_KR
**Script:** Hangul (한글) with some Sino-Korean vocabulary
**Source File:** zume-ko_KR.po

---

## Translation Philosophy

The Korean translation demonstrates a consistent approach that:

1. **Uses standard Korean Christian terminology** - The translation employs well-established Korean Protestant vocabulary that Korean Christians will immediately recognize
2. **Balances formality and accessibility** - Uses polite/formal register (하십시오 form) appropriate for teaching materials while remaining accessible
3. **Prefers Sino-Korean for theological terms** - Core theological concepts use Sino-Korean vocabulary (Chinese-derived words) which is standard in Korean Christianity
4. **Uses native Korean for practical actions** - Action-oriented ministry concepts often use native Korean words
5. **Maintains honorific language for deity** - Consistently uses respectful forms when referring to God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit

## Core Deity Terms

### God

**Korean:** 하나님 (Hananim)

**Romanization:** Ha-na-nim

**Literal Meaning:** "Great One" or "Heaven Lord" (honorific form)

**Register:** Respectful/honorific

**Notes:**
- This is the standard Protestant Christian term for God in Korean
- More respectful than the Catholic term 천주 (Cheonju)
- The suffix -님 (-nim) is an honorific marker showing respect
- Always capitalized in Korean text as 하나님

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| God Uses Ordinary People | 하나님은 평범한 사람들을 사용하십니다 | Title - God as active agent using people |
| Hearing and Obeying God | 하나님의 말씀을 듣고 순종함 | Possessive form with "Word" |
| What do you learn about God? | 하나님에 대해 무엇을 배웠나요? | About/concerning God |
| God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact | 하나님은 일반적이고 평범한 사람들을 사용하여 큰 변화를 만들어내십니다 | Full sentence showing verb agreement |
| Sharing God's Story | 하나님의 이야기를 나누기 | Possessive genitive |
| God's Kingdom | 하나님의 나라 / 하나님 나라 | Kingdom concept |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Uses honorific verb ending -십니다 when God is the subject
- Possessive form: 하나님의 (Hananim-ui) = "of God"

### Jesus

**Korean:** 예수님 (Yesunim)

**Romanization:** Ye-su-nim

**Literal Meaning:** "Jesus" (transliteration) + honorific suffix

**Register:** Respectful/honorific

**Notes:**
- 예수 is a phonetic transliteration of "Jesus"
- The honorific suffix -님 (-nim) is always added to show respect
- Sometimes appears as 예수 그리스도 (Yesu Geuriseudo = Jesus Christ)

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| How to Follow Jesus | 예수를 따르는 방법 | Infinitive/action form |
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 예수님을 따르는 자란 누구인가요? | Follower of Jesus |
| Learn how to obey Jesus' commands | 예수님의 명령에 순종하는 방법을 배우십시오 | Jesus' commands (possessive) |
| Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus | 지인에게 예수님을 전하기 | Evangelism context |
| Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you | 가르쳐 지키게 하고 당신과 함께 예수님을 따르게 하세요 | Following Jesus |
| Becoming more like Jesus... Together | 함께 더욱 예수님을 닮아가는 중... | Becoming like Jesus |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Object marker: 예수님을 (Yesunim-eul) = "Jesus" as direct object
- Possessive: 예수님의 (Yesunim-ui) = "Jesus'" or "of Jesus"
- Sometimes appears without -님 in compound phrases

### Holy Spirit

**Korean:** 성령 (Seongnyeong)

**Romanization:** Seong-nyeong

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy Spirit" (Sino-Korean: 성 = holy/sacred, 령/영 = spirit)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Notes:**
- Pure Sino-Korean theological term
- Standard across all Korean Christian denominations
- Sometimes appears as 성령님 (with honorific) in very devotional contexts, but typically used without -님 in teaching materials
- Can also be written 하나님의 성령 (God's Holy Spirit)

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together | 성령이 함께 하는 시간 동안 인도해주시도록 기도하십시오 | Prayer for guidance |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Subject marker: 성령이 (Seongnyeong-i) = "Holy Spirit" as subject
- Uses honorific verb forms (인도해주시도록) when Holy Spirit is the subject

### Lord

**Korean:** 주 (Ju) / 주님 (Junim)

**Romanization:** Ju / Ju-nim

**Literal Meaning:** "Lord" or "Master"

**Register:** Formal/respectful

**Notes:**
- 주 is Sino-Korean for "lord/master"
- Often appears with honorific -님 as 주님
- Used in compound phrases like "Lord's Supper"

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| The Lord's Supper | 성찬 / 주의 만찬 | Communion/Eucharist |
| Celebrate the Lord's Supper | 주의 만찬을 기념하십시다 | Celebration context |

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple

**Korean:** 제자 (Jeja)

**Romanization:** Je-ja

**Literal Meaning:** "Disciple" or "pupil" (Sino-Korean: 제 = receive, 자 = person)

**Register:** Formal

**Notes:**
- Standard Sino-Korean term used in all Christian contexts
- Same term used in secular educational contexts (student/pupil)
- Very stable term with clear biblical associations

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 제자와 교회의 간단한 정의 | Basic definition |
| What's a Disciple? | 제자란 무엇입니까? | Question form |
| Being A Disciple | 제자 되기 | Becoming/being a disciple |
| 1 trained multiplying disciple per 5,000 | 미국 내 5,000명당 1명의 훈련된 증식 제자 수 | Statistical description |
| Ask yourself, what does my disciple need | 당신의 제자들에게 무엇이 필요할지 스스로에게 물어보기 | Personal relationship |
| To saturate the world with multiplying disciples | 제자 배가를 통해 온 세상에 충만하기 | Vision statement |

**Compound Forms:**
- 제자 되기 = being/becoming a disciple
- 제자 삼기 = making disciples (disciple-making)
- 제자 양육 = disciple nurturing/cultivation
- 제자 배가 = disciple multiplication
- 제자 훈련 = disciple training
- 제자화 = discipleship (noun form)

### Discipleship

**Korean:** 제자화 (Jejahwa) / 제자 훈련 (Jeja Hunryeon)

**Romanization:** Je-ja-hwa / Je-ja Hun-ryeon

**Literal Meaning:** "Disciple-ization" / "Disciple training"

**Register:** Formal

**Notes:**
- 제자화 is the abstract noun form (adding -화 makes it "the process of")
- 제자 훈련 is more concrete and action-oriented (training disciples)
- Both terms are used in Korean Christianity

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Discipleship Concepts | 제자화의 개념 | Abstract concept |
| Duckling Discipleship | 새끼오리 제자훈련 | Specific training method |

### Disciple-Making / Making Disciples

**Korean:** 제자 삼기 (Jeja Samgi)

**Romanization:** Je-ja Sam-gi

**Literal Meaning:** "Making disciples" (삼기 = making/causing to become)

**Register:** Active/practical

**Notes:**
- Action-oriented phrase
- 삼다 (samda) = to make, to cause to become
- This is the standard translation of the Great Commission phrase "make disciples"

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making | 새끼오리와 제자 삼기에 어떤 연관이 있는지 알아보세요 | Metaphor explanation |
| Making Disciples | 제자 삼기 | Section title |
| Train disciples how to make disciples | 20시간의 코스 콘텐츠로 제자 양육 방법 교육하기 | Training purpose |

## Church Terms

### Church

**Korean:** 교회 (Gyohoe)

**Romanization:** Gyo-hoe

**Literal Meaning:** "Teaching gathering" (Sino-Korean: 교 = teaching, 회 = gathering/meeting)

**Register:** Formal/standard

**Notes:**
- Standard term for church in all Korean Christian contexts
- Can refer to both the building and the congregation
- Same term used for both local churches and the universal Church

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 제자와 교회의 간단한 정의 | Basic definition |
| When you think of a church, what comes to mind? | 교회를 생각하면 무엇이 떠오르는가요? | Reflection question |
| What's a Church? | 교회란 무엇입니까? | Definition question |
| Leading a Simple Church | 단순한 교회를 이끌기 | House church context |
| My Churches | 나의 교회 | Personal churches planted |
| Church Name | 교회 이름 | Administrative |
| Church Location | 교회 위치 | Geographical |
| Parent Church | 모교회 | Church planting terminology |

**Compound Forms:**
- 간이 교회 / 간단한 교회 = simple church
- 단순한 교회 = simple church (variant)
- 모교회 = mother/parent church

### Simple Church

**Korean:** 간단한 교회 (Gandanhan Gyohoe) / 간이 교회 (Gani Gyohoe)

**Romanization:** Gan-dan-han Gyo-hoe / Ga-ni Gyo-hoe

**Literal Meaning:** "Simple/easy church"

**Register:** Descriptive

**Notes:**
- 간단한 = simple, easy, straightforward
- 간이 = simple, temporary, makeshift (slightly different nuance)
- Both forms appear in the translation for "simple church"

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 2 simple churches per 5,000 people | 미국 인구 5,000명당 2개의 간이 교회 수 | Statistics |
| Always report new simple churches that you are starting | 새로 시작하는 간단한 교회는 항상 보고하세요 | Reporting |
| Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church? | 3/3 그룹을 단순한 교회로 보아도 될까요? | Discussion question |

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel

**Korean:** 복음 (Bogeum)

**Romanization:** Bo-geum

**Literal Meaning:** "Good news" or "blessed sound" (Sino-Korean: 복 = blessing/good fortune, 음 = sound/news)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Notes:**
- Standard biblical term for gospel in Korean
- Well-established translation with over 100 years of usage in Korean Christianity
- Same term used across all Korean Bible translations

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| The Gospel and How to Share It | 복음 및 전하는 방법 | Training title |
| The Gospel | 복음 | Simple reference |
| Share the Gospel | 복음을 전하기 | Evangelism action |
| Take turns telling the Gospel to each other | 돌아가며 서로에게 복음을 전해봅니다 | Practice activity |
| Sharing the Gospel | 복음 전하기 | Action noun |
| Practice Sharing the Gospel | 복음 전하기 연습 | Training exercise |
| 3-Circles Gospel Presentation | 세 개의 원으로 제시하는 복음 / 3 서클 복음 프레젠테이션 | Method name |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Object marker: 복음을 (bogeum-eul) = "the gospel" as direct object
- 복음 전하기 = gospel sharing/proclamation
- 복음을 나누다 = to share the gospel

### Bible / Scripture

**Korean:** 성경 (Seonggyeong)

**Romanization:** Seong-gyeong

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy scriptures" (Sino-Korean: 성 = holy, 경 = classic texts/scriptures)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Notes:**
- Universal term for the Bible in Korean
- Used across all denominations
- Very stable and well-established term

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study | S.O.A.P.S. 성경 읽기 | Study method |
| S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading | S.O.A.P.S. 성경 읽기 | Reading method |
| Scripture | 성경 | General reference |
| Bible Reading | 성경 일기 | Regular practice |
| Personal Bible Study | 개인 성경 공부 | Individual study |

**Compound Forms:**
- 성경 읽기 = Bible reading
- 성경 일기 = Bible diary/journal
- 성경 공부 = Bible study

### Word (of God)

**Korean:** 말씀 (Malsseum)

**Romanization:** Mal-sseum

**Literal Meaning:** "Word" (honorific form)

**Register:** Respectful/honorific

**Notes:**
- Honorific form of 말 (mal = word/speech)
- Used specifically for God's Word or respected persons' words
- Different from regular word (말) due to honorific nature
- Can mean both Scripture and direct revelation from God

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Hearing and Obeying God | 하나님의 말씀을 듣고 순종함 | God's Word (direct) |
| Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God | 영적 호흡은 하나님의 말씀을 듣고 순종하는 것입니다 | God's communication |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 하나님의 말씀 = God's Word (possessive)
- Always uses honorific verb forms when the Word is the subject

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism

**Korean:** 세례 (Serye)

**Romanization:** Se-rye

**Literal Meaning:** "Washing ceremony" (Sino-Korean: 세 = wash, 례 = ceremony/rite)

**Register:** Formal/liturgical

**Notes:**
- Standard Protestant term for baptism
- Catholics sometimes use 영세 (yeongse) but 세례 is more common in Protestant contexts
- Well-established liturgical term

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Baptism and How To Do It | 세례 및 진행 방법 | Training title |
| Baptism | 세례 | Simple reference |
| Have you ever baptized someone? | 다른 사람에게 세례를 준 적이 있습니까? | Question about practice |
| IMPORTANT REMINDER – Have you been baptized? | 중요 알림 – 세례를 받았습니까? | Personal status |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 세례를 주다 = to baptize (give baptism)
- 세례를 받다 = to be baptized (receive baptism)

### Lord's Supper / Communion

**Korean:** 성찬 (Seongchan) / 주의 만찬 (Jui Manchan)

**Romanization:** Seong-chan / Ju-ui Man-chan

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy meal" / "Lord's dinner"

**Register:** Formal/liturgical

**Notes:**
- 성찬 is the shorter, more common Protestant term (holy meal)
- 주의 만찬 is more literal (Lord's dinner/supper)
- Both terms are understood and used in Korean churches
- Catholics use 성체성사 (holy sacrament) but Protestants prefer 성찬

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It | 성찬과 진행하는 방법 | Training title |
| The Lord's Supper | 성찬 | Simple reference |
| Celebrate the Lord's Supper | 주의 만찬을 기념하십시다 | Action/practice |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 성찬을 가지다 / 성찬식을 가지다 = to have/celebrate communion
- 주의 만찬을 기념하다 = to commemorate the Lord's Supper

### Prayer

**Korean:** 기도 (Gido)

**Romanization:** Gi-do

**Literal Meaning:** "Prayer" (Sino-Korean: 기 = pray/request, 도 = way/path)

**Register:** Formal/standard

**Notes:**
- Universal term for prayer in Korean
- Used in religious and secular contexts (though more common in religious)
- Very stable and well-established term

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| How to Spend an Hour in Prayer | 기도로 시간을 보내는 방법 | Training on prayer |
| See how easy it is to spend an hour in prayer | 한 시간 동안 기도하는 것이 얼마나 쉬운지요 | Encouragement |
| Prayer Walking and How To Do It | 걷기 기도 및 진행 방법 | Prayer method |
| Prayer Walking | 걷기 기도 | Practice name |
| The BLESS Prayer Pattern | BLESS 기도 패턴 | Specific method |
| Prayer Cycle | 기도 사이클 | Prayer tool |
| Prayer | 기도 | General term |
| What is your reaction to spending an hour in prayer? | 기도로 한 시간을 보내는 것에 대한 당신의 반응은 무엇입니까? | Reflection |
| Strong Prayer Life | 강력한 기도 생활 | Spiritual discipline |

**Compound Forms:**
- 기도 생활 = prayer life
- 기도 사이클 = prayer cycle
- 기도 패턴 = prayer pattern
- 걷기 기도 = prayer walking (literally "walking prayer")

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 기도하다 = to pray (verb)
- 기도로 = through/by prayer (instrumental)
- 기도를 하다 = to do prayer

### Worship

**Korean:** 예배 (Yebae)

**Romanization:** Ye-bae

**Literal Meaning:** "Worship service" or "ceremonial respect" (Sino-Korean: 예 = ceremony/respect, 배 = bow/worship)

**Register:** Formal/liturgical

**Notes:**
- Standard term for worship service in Korean Christianity
- Can refer to both corporate worship and private devotion
- Not heavily featured in the translation sample but standard term

## Salvation Terms

### Faith / Faithfulness

**Korean:** 믿음 (Mideum) [faith] / 신실함 (Sinsilham) [faithfulness]

**Romanization:** Mi-deum / Sin-sil-ham

**Literal Meaning:** "Believing" / "Trustworthiness-quality"

**Register:** Formal

**Notes:**
- 믿음 is native Korean for "faith/belief" (from verb 믿다 = to believe)
- 신실함 is Sino-Korean for "faithfulness/trustworthiness" (신실 = faithful + -함 = abstract noun suffix)
- The translation makes a clear distinction between faith as belief and faithfulness as character quality

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Faithfulness is Better Than Knowledge | 신실함이 지식보다 낫습니다 | Teaching title |
| Faithfulness | 신실함 | Character quality |
| Faithfulness is Better than Knowledge | 신실함이 지식보다 낫습니다 | Comparative statement |
| The Power of Obedient Faith | 순종하는 믿음의 힘 | Faith in action |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 믿음 = faith/belief (noun from verb)
- 신실함 = faithfulness (abstract quality)
- Different terms used for different theological concepts

### Believer / Unbeliever

**Korean:** 신자 (Sinja) / 비신자 (Bisinja) / 그리스도인 (Geuriseudoin)

**Romanization:** Sin-ja / Bi-sin-ja / Geu-ri-seu-do-in

**Literal Meaning:** "Believing person" / "Non-believing person" / "Christian person"

**Register:** Formal

**Notes:**
- 신자 (believer) is Sino-Korean (신 = faith + 자 = person)
- 비신자 (unbeliever) adds prefix 비- (non-/un-)
- 그리스도인 means "Christian" (literally "Christ person")
- These are standard terms in Korean Christianity

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Unbeliever | 비신자 | Non-Christian |
| Share the "Training Cycle" with a believer on your list of 100 | "훈련 사이클"을 100인의 명단 중 그리스도인들에게 나눠보십시오 | Targeting believers |

### Follower (of Jesus)

**Korean:** 따르는 자 (Ttareuneun Ja)

**Romanization:** Tta-reu-neun Ja

**Literal Meaning:** "One who follows" (따르다 = to follow + -는 자 = person who)

**Register:** Formal/descriptive

**Notes:**
- Descriptive phrase rather than fixed theological term
- 따르다 (ttareuda) = to follow
- -는 자 creates a noun meaning "one who [verbs]"
- More action-oriented than "believer"

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| How to Follow Jesus | 예수를 따르는 방법 | Action/practice |
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 예수님을 따르는 자란 누구인가요? | Identity question |
| What are the activities of a follower of Jesus? | 예수님을 따르는 자들의 활동에는 어떤 것이 있나요? | Behavioral description |
| Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you | 가르쳐 지키게 하고 당신과 함께 예수님을 따르게 하세요 | Discipleship action |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 예수를 따르다 = to follow Jesus
- 예수님을 따르는 자 = one who follows Jesus (person)
- 따르게 하다 = to cause to follow

## Spiritual Qualities and Actions

### Obey / Obedience

**Korean:** 순종 (Sunjong) / 순종하다 (Sunjong-hada) / 지키다 (Jikida)

**Romanization:** Sun-jong / Sun-jong-ha-da / Ji-ki-da

**Literal Meaning:** "Obedience/submission" / "to obey" / "to keep/guard"

**Register:** Formal

**Notes:**
- 순종 is Sino-Korean (순 = follow/comply + 종 = follow) - noun for obedience
- 순종하다 is the verb form (to obey)
- 지키다 means "to keep/observe/guard" and is used for keeping commands
- Both terms appear in obedience contexts with slightly different nuances

**Examples:**

| English | Korean | Context |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God | 영적 호흡은 하나님의 말씀을 듣고 순종하는 것입니다 | Core practice |
| Hearing and Obeying God | 하나님의 말씀을 듣고 순종함 | Action pair |
| Learn how to obey Jesus' commands | 가서 머물면서 예수님의 명령에 순종하는 방법을 배우십시오 | Learning obedience |
| Obeyed | 순종함 | Self-assessment category |
| Obey | 순종 | Action word |
| Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus | 가르쳐 지키게 하고 당신과 함께 예수님을 따르게 하세요 | Using 지키다 form |
| OBEY | 순종 | Section header |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 순종하다 = to obey (verb)
- 순종하는 = obeying (present participle)
- 지키다 = to keep/observe (commands)
- 지키게 하다 = to cause to keep/observe

### Hear / Listen

**Korean:** 듣다 (Deutda) / 들음 (Deureum)

**Romanization:** Deut-da / Deu-reum

**Literal Meaning:** "To hear/listen"

**Register:** Native Korean (not Sino-Korean)

**Notes:**
- Pure native Korean verb
- 듣다 = to hear/listen (verb)
- 들음 = hearing (noun form)
- Key part of "hearing and obeying" couplet

**Examples:**

## Translation Patterns and Principles

### 1. Honorific Usage

The Korean translation consistently demonstrates proper use of honorifics:

**For Deity (God, Jesus, Holy Spirit):**
- Uses honorific verb endings: -십니다, -세요, -시도록
- Example: 하나님은 평범한 사람들을 사용하십니다 ("God uses..." with honorific)
- Adds honorific suffix -님 to Jesus (예수님)
- Uses honorific word 말씀 instead of regular 말 for God's Word

**For Teaching/Instruction:**
- Uses polite imperative forms: -하십시오, -세요
- Example: 기도하십시오 ("Please pray" - formal polite)
- Appropriate for training and instructional materials

**For Questions:**
- Uses polite question endings: -습니까?, -나요?, -까요?
- Shows respect to the reader/learner

### 2. Sino-Korean vs. Native Korean Vocabulary

The translation strategically chooses between Sino-Korean (Chinese-derived) and native Korean words:

**Sino-Korean for:**
- Theological concepts: 제자 (disciple), 교회 (church), 복음 (gospel), 성경 (Bible)
- Formal/abstract concepts: 훈련 (training), 책임 (accountability), 신실함 (faithfulness)
- Institutional terms: 세례 (baptism), 성찬 (communion)

**Native Korean for:**
- Action verbs: 듣다 (hear), 나누다 (share), 따르다 (follow)
- Practical activities: 걷기 (walking), 모임 (gathering)
- Common objects: 사람 (person), 나라 (country)

**English Loanwords for:**
- Contemporary ministry concepts: 코치 (coach), 멘토 (mentor), 리더 (leader)
- Modern tools: 그룹 (group), 비전 (vision), 커뮤니티 (community)
- Shows adaptation to contemporary church culture

### 3. Compound Formation

Korean readily forms compounds for ministry concepts:

**Pattern 1: Noun + Noun**
- 제자 + 훈련 = 제자 훈련 (disciple training)
- 기도 + 생활 = 기도 생활 (prayer life)
- 책임 + 그룹 = 책임 그룹 (accountability group)

**Pattern 2: Adjective + Noun**
- 영적 + 호흡 = 영적 호흡 (spiritual breathing)
- 간단한 + 교회 = 간단한 교회 (simple church)

**Pattern 3: Verb Noun + Noun**
- 걷기 + 기도 = 걷기 기도 (prayer walking)
- 들음 + 순종 = 들음과 순종 (hearing and obeying)

### 4. Verb Forms and Nominalizations

Korean flexibly converts between verbs and nouns:

**Verb → Noun Patterns:**
- 듣다 (to hear) → 들음 (hearing)
- 순종하다 (to obey) → 순종함 (obeying)
- 나누다 (to share) → 나눔 (sharing)
- 훈련하다 (to train) → 훈련함 (training/being trained)

**Action Nouns with -기:**
- 제자 삼기 = disciple-making (making disciples)
- 나누기 = sharing
- 세우기 = establishing

### 5. Respectful Distance and Formality

The overall register maintains:
- Professional distance appropriate for training materials
- Respect for spiritual topics
- Accessible but not casual tone
- Formal enough for church use, practical enough for application

### 6. Theological Vocabulary Consistency

The translation uses standard Korean Protestant terminology that will be:
- Immediately recognizable to Korean Christians
- Consistent with Korean Bible translations
- Compatible with existing Korean church vocabulary
- Appropriate across denominational lines (within Protestantism)

## Special Notes for Translators

### 1. Particle Usage

Korean requires particles (조사, josa) that English doesn't have. Key particles in spiritual contexts:

- **Subject markers:** 이/가
  - 하나님이 (God as subject)
  - 성령이 (Holy Spirit as subject)

- **Object markers:** 을/를
  - 예수님을 (Jesus as object)
  - 복음을 (gospel as object)

- **Possessive marker:** 의
  - 하나님의 말씀 (God's Word)
  - 예수님의 명령 (Jesus' commands)
  - Often omitted in compounds: 하나님 나라 instead of 하나님의 나라

- **Topic marker:** 은/는
  - 하나님은 (God as topic)

### 2. Verb Endings Matter

The formality level is conveyed through verb endings:

**Formal Polite (used in Zume):**
- -습니다/-ㅂ니다 (statement)
- -십시오/-세요 (command)
- -습니까/-ㅂ니까 (question)

**Examples:**
- 기도하십시오 = Please pray (formal)
- 배웠나요? = Did you learn? (polite informal)

### 3. Honorific Consistency

When translating:
- Always use -님 with Jesus (예수님) unless in compound technical terms
- Always use honorific verbs when God/Jesus/Holy Spirit is subject
- Use 말씀 (not 말) for God's Word
- Maintain consistent formality level throughout materials

### 4. Loanword Integration

Modern Korean ministry terms often use English loanwords. Don't be afraid to use:
- 코치 (coach) rather than trying to find Korean equivalent
- 리더 (leader) in contemporary contexts
- 그룹 (group) for small groups
- 비전 (vision) for vision-casting

These are standard in Korean church contexts and more natural than forcing Korean equivalents.

### 5. Compound Flexibility

Korean allows flexible compounding. Both patterns are acceptable:
- With particle: 하나님의 나라 (God's kingdom)
- Without particle: 하나님 나라 (God-kingdom)

The version without particle is often more concise and equally natural.

### 6. Context Determines Word Choice

Some terms have multiple Korean equivalents. Choose based on context:

**"Share":**
- 나누다 for sharing stories, testimony, personal experience
- 전하다 for proclaiming gospel, conveying message
- 공유하다 for sharing resources, information

**"Church":**
- 교회 for church as institution or building
- 공동체 for church as community/fellowship (less common)

**"Kingdom":**
- 하나님 나라 for God's kingdom (preferred modern usage)
- 천국 for heaven/kingdom of heaven (traditional)
