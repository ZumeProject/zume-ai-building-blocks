## Introduction

This reference document provides a comprehensive analysis of spiritual and theological terminology in Bengali (India) as used in the Zúme training materials. Bengali, an Indo-Aryan language with over 230 million speakers worldwide, has a rich literary tradition and incorporates extensive Sanskrit loanwords, particularly in religious contexts.

### Key Linguistic Features

1. **Sanskrit Influence:** Heavy use of Sanskritized terms for theological concepts
2. **Script:** Uses Bengali script (Bangla lipi), derived from Brahmi script
3. **Cultural Context:** Bengali speakers include Hindus, Muslims, and Christians, requiring careful terminology choices
4. **Formality:** Mix of formal Sanskrit-based terms and colloquial Bengali expressions

## CORE DEITY TERMS

### Jesus - যীশু (Yīśu)

**Bengali:** যীশু
**Transliteration:** Yīśu
**Pronunciation:** YEE-shu
**Etymology:** Direct transliteration from Greek Ἰησοῦς (Iēsoûs)

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Learn how to obey Jesus' commands by going AND staying."**
   - EN: Learn how to obey Jesus' commands
   - BN: যীশুর আদেশ মেনে চলতে শিখুন
   - TR: Yīśur ādeś mene calte śikhun
   - Note: Possessive form "যীশুর" (Yīśur = Jesus's)

2. **"How to Follow Jesus"**
   - EN: How to Follow Jesus
   - BN: কিভাবে যীশুকে অনুসরণ করবেন
   - TR: Kibhābe yīśuke anuśaraṇ karben
   - Note: Accusative case "যীশুকে" (Yīśuke = Jesus [object])

3. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - EN: follower of Jesus
   - BN: যীশুর অনুসারী
   - TR: Yīśur anuśārī
   - Note: Possessive construction

4. **"Jesus said, 'Whoever wishes to become great among you shall be your servant.'"**
   - EN: Jesus said
   - BN: যীশু বলেছেন
   - TR: Yīśu balechen
   - Note: Perfect tense "বলেছেন" (has said) - shows respect

5. **"Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus"**
   - EN: about Jesus
   - BN: যীশু সম্পর্কে
   - TR: Yīśu samparke
   - Note: Postposition construction "সম্পর্কে" (about)

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Direct nominative: যীশু (Yīśu)
- Possessive: যীশুর (Yīśur)
- Accusative/Object: যীশুকে (Yīśuke)
- About/Concerning: যীশু সম্পর্কে (Yīśu samparke)

### God - ঈশ্বর (Īśvar)

**Bengali:** ঈশ্বর
**Transliteration:** Īśvar
**Pronunciation:** EE-shwar
**Etymology:** Sanskrit ईश्वर (Īśvara) meaning "lord, master, supreme being"

**Cultural Note:** ঈশ্বর is the Sanskritized, Hindu-origin term for God, used by Bengali Christians to bridge cultural contexts. Muslims typically use আল্লাহ (Āllāh), but Christian materials predominantly use ঈশ্বর to connect with the broader Bengali-speaking population.

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"God Uses Ordinary People"**
   - EN: God Uses Ordinary People
   - BN: ঈশ্বর সাধারণ মানুষকে ব্যবহার করেন
   - TR: Īśvar sādhāraṇ mānuṣake byabahār karen
   - Note: "Uses" = ব্যবহার করেন (formal/respectful)

2. **"Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - EN: Hearing and Obeying God
   - BN: ঈশ্বরের কথা শোনা এবং তার বাধ্য হওয়া
   - TR: Īśvarer kathā śonā ebam tār bādhya hoẏā
   - Note: Possessive "ঈশ্বরের" + "কথা" (word/voice)

3. **"Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God."**
   - EN: we hear from God and we obey God
   - BN: আমরা ঈশ্বরের কাছ থেকে শুনি এবং ঈশ্বরের কথা মেনে চলি
   - TR: Āmrā īśvarer kāch theke śuni ebam īśvarer kathā mene cali
   - Note: "From God" = ঈশ্বরের কাছ থেকে (ablative construction)

4. **"God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact."**
   - EN: God uses ordinary people
   - BN: ঈশ্বর সাধারণ মানুষকে সাধারণ কাজ করে বড় প্রভাব ফেলতে ব্যবহার করেন
   - TR: Īśvar sādhāraṇ mānuṣake sādhāraṇ kāj kare baṛa prabhāb phelate byabahār karen

5. **"Have you ever stopped to think about where God's Kingdom... isn't?"**
   - EN: God's Kingdom
   - BN: ঈশ্বরের রাজ্য
   - TR: Īśvarer rājya
   - Note: Possessive construction

**Grammatical Forms:**
- Nominative: ঈশ্বর (Īśvar)
- Possessive: ঈশ্বরের (Īśvarer)
- From God: ঈশ্বরের কাছ থেকে (Īśvarer kāch theke)
- God's voice/word: ঈশ্বরের কথা (Īśvarer kathā)

### Love (agape) - ভালোবাসা / প্রেম (Bhālobāsā / Prem)

**Bengali (Love - general):** ভালোবাসা
**Transliteration:** Bhālobāsā
**Bengali (Love - romantic/spiritual):** প্রেম
**Transliteration:** Prem
**Etymology:** ভালোবাসা = compound of ভালো (good) + বাসা (dwelling/loving); প্রেম from Sanskrit प्रेम

**Note:** No direct examples found in extracted content. Bengali has multiple words for love:
- ভালোবাসা (Bhālobāsā) = general love, affection
- প্রেম (Prem) = deep love, romantic love, divine love
- স্নেহ (Sneha) = tender affection
- প্রীতি (Prīti) = love, kindness

For agape love in Christian contexts:
- ঈশ্বরের ভালোবাসা (Īśvarer bhālobāsā) = God's love
- নিঃস্বার্থ প্রেম (Niḥsvārtha prem) = selfless love

## LINGUISTIC PATTERNS AND OBSERVATIONS

### 1. **Sanskrit Dominance**
The translation heavily relies on Sanskrit-derived vocabulary for theological and spiritual concepts:
- **Deity terms:** ঈশ্বর (Īśvar), পবিত্র আত্মা (Pabitra Ātmā), প্রভু (Prabhu)
- **Spiritual qualities:** আধ্যাত্মিক (Ādhyātmik), প্রার্থনা (Prārthanā), আশীর্বাদ (Āśīrbād)
- **Discipleship:** শিষ্য (Śiṣya), গুরু (Guru - implied)

This creates theological continuity with Hindu religious vocabulary, making Christian concepts accessible to Bengali speakers from Hindu backgrounds.

### 2. **Portuguese and English Loanwords**
- **Portuguese:** গির্জা (girjā = church) from "igreja"
- **English:** বাইবেল (bāibel), বাপ্তিস্ম (bāptisma), নেটওয়ার্ক (neṭaoyārk), কোচ (koc)

These loanwords reflect historical Christian missionary influence and modern globalization.

### 3. **Calque Translations**
Beautiful native formations:
- সুসমাচার (susamācār) = good news (সু = good + সমাচার = news)
- পবিত্র আত্মা (pabitra ātmā) = holy spirit (পবিত্র = holy + আত্মা = spirit)
- আধ্যাত্মিক শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস (ādhyātmik śvās-praśvās) = spiritual breathing

### 4. **Compound Verbs**
Bengali frequently uses compound verbs with করা (karā = to do):
- প্রার্থনা করা (prārthanā karā) = to pray
- বিশ্বাস করা (biśvās karā) = to believe
- অনুসরণ করা (anuśaraṇ karā) = to follow
- গুণ করা (guṇ karā) = to multiply

### 5. **Case System**
Bengali retains a simplified case system:
- **Nominative:** যীশু (Yīśu = Jesus)
- **Possessive:** যীশুর (Yīśur = Jesus's)
- **Accusative:** যীশুকে (Yīśuke = Jesus [object])
- **Locative:** প্রার্থনায় (prārthanāẏa = in prayer)

### 6. **Respectful Language**
The translation uses honorific verb forms consistently:
- করেন (karen) instead of করে (kare) = formal "does"
- বলেছেন (balechen) = "has said" (respectful)
- This shows cultural respect for Jesus and God

### 7. **Reduplication**
Bengali uses reduplication for emphasis:
- ভাগাভাগি (bhāgābhāgi) = sharing (from ভাগ = share)
- শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস (śvās-praśvās) = breathing (inhale-exhale)

### 8. **Formal vs. Colloquial Balance**
The translation strikes a balance:
- **Formal/Sanskritized:** ঈশ্বর, আধ্যাত্মিক, প্রার্থনা
- **Colloquial:** কথা (kathā = word), শোনা (śonā = hearing), মানুষ (mānuṣ = people)

This makes content accessible while maintaining theological dignity.

### 1. **Hindu-Christian Terminology Bridge**
The use of Hindu-derived Sanskrit terms creates a bridge for Hindu-background Bengalis:
- ঈশ্বর (Īśvar) is acceptable to both Hindus and Christians
- শিষ্য (Śiṣya) resonates with guru-śiṣya tradition

While Bengali Muslims use আল্লাহ (Allāh) for God, this Christian material uses ঈশ্বর, clearly positioning it within a Hindu-Christian dialogue rather than Islamic.

### 3. **Church vs. Sangha**
The use of গির্জা (girjā) rather than সংঘ (saṅgha - Buddhist assembly) clearly marks Christian community identity.

### 4. **Simple Church Terminology**
সাধারণ গির্জা (sādhāraṇ girjā) and সহজ গির্জা (sahaj girjā) both mean "simple church," emphasizing accessibility and avoiding institutional complexity - key to the Zúme approach.

### 5. **Multiplication Language**
Heavy use of গুণ (guṇ = multiplication) connects to mathematical literacy while carrying spiritual significance of exponential growth.

### 6. **Relationship-Focused Language**
- শিষ্য (disciple) emphasizes learning relationship
- অনুসারী (follower) emphasizes allegiance
- বিশ্বাসী (believer) emphasizes faith stance

This trinity of terms provides nuanced understanding of Christian identity.
