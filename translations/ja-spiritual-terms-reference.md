## Core Deity Terms

### God
**Japanese**: 神 (kami)
**Romaji**: kami
**Register**: Formal

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "God Uses Ordinary People"
   **Japanese**: 神は普通の人たちを用いられる
   **Romaji**: Kami wa futsuu no hitotachi wo mochiirareru
   **Literal**: God uses ordinary people

2. **English**: "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God."
   **Japanese**: 弟子であるとは、神の声を聞き、従うことです。
   **Romaji**: Deshi de aru to wa, kami no koe wo kiki, shitagau koto desu
   **Literal**: Being a disciple is hearing God's voice and obeying

3. **English**: "God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact."
   **Japanese**: 神は、素朴なことをしている普通の人たちを用いて、大きな変化を起こされる。
   **Romaji**: Kami wa, soboku na koto wo shite iru futsuu no hitotachi wo mochiite, ookina henka wo okosareru
   **Literal**: God uses ordinary people doing simple things to cause big change

4. **English**: "Why is it essential to learn to hear and recognize God's voice?"
   **Japanese**: 神の御声を聞き分けられるようになることは、なぜ必要不可欠なのでしょう？
   **Romaji**: Kami no mi-koe wo kikiwakerareru you ni naru koto wa, naze hitsuyou-fukaketsu na no deshou?
   **Literal**: Why is it essential to be able to discern God's voice?

5. **English**: "Have you ever stopped to think about where God's Kingdom... isn't?"
   **Japanese**: 神の御国がない場所について、考えてみたことはありますか？
   **Romaji**: Kami no mikuni ga nai basho ni tsuite, kangaete mita koto wa arimasu ka?
   **Literal**: Have you ever thought about places where God's Kingdom isn't?

6. **English**: "God makes plans, and He expects us to make plans, too."
   **Japanese**: 神は計画を立てられ、私たちにも計画を立てるように期待されています。
   **Romaji**: Kami wa keikaku wo taterareru, watashitachi ni mo keikaku wo tateru you ni kitai sarete imasu
   **Literal**: God makes plans and expects us also to make plans

7. **English**: "Sharing God's Story"
   **Japanese**: 神のストーリーを分かち合う
   **Romaji**: Kami no sutoorii wo wakachiau
   **Literal**: Sharing God's story

8. **English**: "Go as God leads, and plan on spending 60-90 minutes on this activity."
   **Japanese**: 神が導かれるままに出かけ、60から90分かけてこのアクティビティを行います。
   **Romaji**: Kami ga michibikareru mama ni dekake, 60 kara 90-pun kakete kono akutibiti wo okonaimasu
   **Literal**: Go out as God leads, spending 60-90 minutes on this activity

9. **English**: "What do you learn about God?"
   **Japanese**: 神について何を学びますか？
   **Romaji**: Kami ni tsuite nani wo manabimasu ka?
   **Literal**: What do you learn about God?

**Notes**:
- Uses 神 (kami) consistently throughout
- When referring to "God's voice" uses 御声 (mi-koe) which is an honorific form
- When referring to "God's Kingdom" uses 御国 (mikuni) which is also honorific
- The honorific prefix 御 (mi-/o-) is consistently used for God's attributes (御声 mi-koe = voice, 御国 mikuni = kingdom)
- Verb forms for God's actions often use the respectful 〜られる (rareru) suffix

**Japanese**: イエス (Iesu)
**Romaji**: Iesu
**Register**: Formal
**Form**: Katakana transliteration

**Usage Examples**:

   **Romaji**: Iesu ni shitagau houhou
   **Literal**: Method to follow Jesus

2. **English**: "What is a follower of Jesus?"
   **Japanese**: イエスに従う者とは何でしょうか？
   **Romaji**: Iesu ni shitagau mono to wa nan deshou ka?
   **Literal**: What is a person who follows Jesus?

3. **English**: "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?"
   **Japanese**: イエスに従う者の活動とは何でしょうか？
   **Romaji**: Iesu ni shitagau mono no katsudou to wa nan deshou ka?
   **Literal**: What are the activities of a person who follows Jesus?

4. **English**: "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands by going AND staying."
   **Japanese**: 去ることと留まることによってイエスの命令に従う方法を学びます。
   **Romaji**: Saru koto to todomaru koto ni yotte Iesu no meirei ni shitagau houhou wo manabimasu
   **Literal**: Learn the method to obey Jesus' commands by going and staying

5. **English**: "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you"
   **Japanese**: 他の人たちに、あなたと一緒にイエスに従うよう教える
   **Romaji**: Hoka no hitotachi ni, anata to issho ni Iesu ni shitagau you oshieru
   **Literal**: Teach others to follow Jesus together with you

   **Japanese**: 自分が知っている人たちにイエスについて話す
   **Romaji**: Jibun ga shitte iru hitotachi ni Iesu ni tsuite hanasu
   **Literal**: Talk about Jesus to people you know

7. **English**: "Speaking to people YOU DON'T KNOW about Jesus"
   **Japanese**: 自分が知らない人たちにイエスについて話す
   **Romaji**: Jibun ga shiranai hitotachi ni Iesu ni tsuite hanasu
   **Literal**: Talk about Jesus to people you don't know

8. **English**: "What if many friends, family and others start following Jesus with me?"
   **Japanese**: 多くの友人、家族などが、私と一緒にイエスに従い始めたらどうでしょうか？
   **Romaji**: Ooku no yuujin, kazoku nado ga, watashi to issho ni Iesu ni shitagaihajimetara dou deshou ka?
   **Literal**: What if many friends, family etc. start following Jesus together with me?

9. **English**: "Becoming more like Jesus... Together"
   **Japanese**: ともに、イエスに似た者となる
   **Romaji**: Tomoni, Iesu ni nita mono to naru
   **Literal**: Together, becoming one who resembles Jesus

**Notes**:
- Uses katakana イエス (Iesu) consistently - a phonetic transliteration
- Does not use kanji for Jesus' name
- "Follower of Jesus" is rendered as イエスに従う者 (Iesu ni shitagau mono) - literally "person who follows Jesus"
- Pattern イエスに従う (Iesu ni shitagau) means "to follow Jesus"

### Holy Spirit
**Japanese**: 聖霊 (seirei)
**Romaji**: seirei
**Literal Meaning**: Holy/Sacred (聖 sei) + Spirit/Soul (霊 rei)
**Register**: Formal

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together."
   **Japanese**: 神の聖霊に、グループの時間を導いてくださるようにお願いしましょう。
   **Romaji**: Kami no seirei ni, guruupu no jikan wo michibiite kudasaru you ni onegai shimashou
   **Literal**: Let's ask God's Holy Spirit to lead the group's time

2. **English**: "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time, together."
   **Japanese**: 神の聖霊に、グループの時間を導いて頂きましょう。
   **Romaji**: Kami no seirei ni, guruupu no jikan wo michibiite itadakimashou
   **Literal**: Let's have God's Holy Spirit lead the group's time

**Notes**:
- Uses 聖霊 (seirei) - compound of "holy" + "spirit"
- Always paired as 神の聖霊 (kami no seirei) = "God's Holy Spirit"
- Uses respectful/humble language (ください kudasai, 頂く itadaku) when asking the Holy Spirit to act

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
**Japanese**: 弟子 (deshi)
**Romaji**: deshi
**Literal Meaning**: Younger brother (弟 dei/ototo) + Child (子 shi/ko) = apprentice, student, follower
**Register**: Formal/Neutral

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God."
   **Japanese**: 弟子であるとは、神の声を聞き、従うことです。
   **Romaji**: Deshi de aru to wa, kami no koe wo kiki, shitagau koto desu
   **Literal**: To be a disciple is to hear God's voice and obey

2. **English**: "1 trained multiplying disciple per 5,000 in the United States"
   **Japanese**: アメリカ合衆国で5000人につき、増員の訓練を受けた弟子が1人
   **Romaji**: Amerika gasshukoku de 5000-nin ni tsuki, zouin no kunren wo uketa deshi ga 1-ri
   **Literal**: In the United States, 1 disciple who received multiplication training per 5000 people

3. **English**: "1 trained multiplying disciple per 50,000 globally"
   **Japanese**: 地球上で50000人につき、増員の訓練を受けた弟子が1人
   **Romaji**: Chikyuujou de 50000-nin ni tsuki, zouin no kunren wo uketa deshi ga 1-ri
   **Literal**: On earth, 1 disciple who received multiplication training per 50000 people

4. **English**: "Ask yourself, what does my disciple need"
   **Japanese**: 自分の弟子に必要なことを自問自答してください。
   **Romaji**: Jibun no deshi ni hitsuyou na koto wo jimon-jitou shite kudasai
   **Literal**: Self-question yourself about what your disciple needs

5. **English**: "To saturate the world with multiplying disciples in our generation."
   **Japanese**: 私たちの時代に、弟子を増やして世を満たしましょう。
   **Romaji**: Watashitachi no jidai ni, deshi wo fuyashite yo wo mitashimashou
   **Literal**: Let's fill the world by multiplying disciples in our generation

6. **English**: "Train disciples how to make disciples with 20 hours of course content"
   **Japanese**: 20時間のコースで、弟子作りの方法を弟子たちに訓練する
   **Romaji**: 20-jikan no koosu de, deshi-zukuri no houhou wo deshitachi ni kunren suru
   **Literal**: Train disciples in the method of disciple-making with a 20-hour course

**Notes**:
- Uses 弟子 (deshi) consistently - a traditional Japanese term for student/apprentice/disciple
- Has historical Buddhist and martial arts connotations but widely understood in Christian context
- "Multiplying disciples" translated as 弟子を増やす (deshi wo fuyasu) - literally "multiply/increase disciples"
- Plural form 弟子たち (deshitachi) adds the plural suffix たち (tachi)

   **Japanese**: Duckling Discipleship (appears to remain in English in heading)
   **Notes**: Used アヒルの子が弟子作りと (ahiru no ko ga deshi-zukuri to) when explaining the concept

### Follower
**Japanese**: 従う者 (shitagau mono)
**Romaji**: shitagau mono
**Literal Meaning**: To follow/obey (従う) + person (者)
**Register**: Formal

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "What is a follower of Jesus?"
   **Japanese**: イエスに従う者とは何でしょうか？
   **Romaji**: Iesu ni shitagau mono to wa nan deshou ka?
   **Literal**: What is a person who follows Jesus?

2. **English**: "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?"
   **Japanese**: イエスに従う者の活動とは何でしょうか？

### Believer
**Japanese**: 信者 (shinja) or 未信者 (mi-shinja) for "unbeliever"
**Romaji**: shinja / mi-shinja
**Literal Meaning**: Faith/belief (信 shin) + person (者 ja)
**Register**: Formal/Neutral

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "Unbeliever"
   **Japanese**: 未信者
   **Romaji**: mi-shinja
   **Literal**: Not-yet-believer (未 mi = not yet)

**Notes**:
- 信者 (shinja) = believer
- 未信者 (mi-shinja) = unbeliever (literally "not-yet believer")
- The prefix 未 (mi) suggests "not yet" rather than "never," carrying a hopeful connotation
- Used in contexts discussing evangelism targets

### Maturity / Maturing
**Japanese**: 成長 (seichou)
**Romaji**: seichou
**Literal Meaning**: Form/achieve (成 sei) + grow (長 chou)
**Register**: Formal/Neutral

**Usage Examples**:
1. **English**: "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples"
   **Japanese**: 弟子の成長のためのトレーニングのサイクル
   **Romaji**: Deshi no seichou no tame no toreeningu no saikuru
   **Literal**: Training cycle for the growth of disciples

**Notes**:
- 成長 (seichou) means "growth" or "maturation"
- More commonly uses "growth" language rather than "maturity" as a state

## Church Terms

### Church
**Japanese**: 教会 (kyoukai) or チャーチ (chaachi)
**Romaji**: kyoukai / chaachi
**Register**: Formal / Casual-Modern

**Usage Examples**:

**Traditional term 教会 (kyoukai)**:
1. **English**: "When you think of a church, what comes to mind?"
   **Japanese**: 教会と聞いて、何を思いますか？
   **Romaji**: Kyoukai to kiite, nani wo omoimasu ka?
   **Literal**: When you hear "church," what do you think?

2. **English**: "Add your churches in the 'My Churches' section of the dashboard."
   **Japanese**: ダッシュボードの「マイ・チャーチ」に自分の教会を追加してください。
   **Romaji**: Dasshubōdo no "mai chaachi" ni jibun no kyoukai wo tsuika shite kudasai
   **Literal**: Please add your churches to the "My Churches" section of the dashboard

**Modern term チャーチ (chaachi)** - Used for "Simple Church" concept:
3. **English**: "Always report new simple churches that you are starting."
   **Japanese**: 新しく始めたシンプル・チャーチは常に報告してください。
   **Romaji**: Atarashiku hajimeta shinpuru chaachi wa tsuneni houkoku shite kudasai
   **Literal**: Please always report simple churches you have newly started

4. **English**: "2 simple churches per 5,000 people in the United States"
   **Japanese**: アメリカ合衆国で、5000人につき2つのシンプル・チャーチ
   **Romaji**: Amerika gasshukoku de, 5000-nin ni tsuki futatsu no shinpuru chaachi
   **Literal**: 2 simple churches per 5000 people in the United States

5. **English**: "2 simple churches per 50,000 people globally"
   **Japanese**: 地球上で50000人につき2つのシンプル・チャーチ
   **Romaji**: Chikyuujou de 50000-nin ni tsuki futatsu no shinpuru chaachi
   **Literal**: 2 simple churches per 50000 people on earth

6. **English**: "Simple church vision map"
   **Japanese**: シンプル・チャーチのビジョンマップ
   **Romaji**: Shinpuru chaachi no bijon mappu
   **Literal**: Simple church vision map

7. **English**: "Click to add your first church"
   **Japanese**: クリックして、最初のチャーチを加える
   **Romaji**: Kurikku shite, saisho no chaachi wo kuwaeru
   **Literal**: Click to add your first church

8. **English**: "2 Simple Churches"
   **Japanese**: 2つのシンプル・チャーチ
   **Romaji**: Futatsu no shinpuru chaachi
   **Literal**: 2 simple churches

9. **English**: "Leading a Simple Church"
   **Japanese**: シンプル・チャーチをリードする
   **Romaji**: Shinpuru chaachi wo riido suru
   **Literal**: Leading a simple church

**Mixed usage**:
10. **English**: "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"
    **Japanese**: Uses both terms depending on context

**Notes**:
- **教会 (kyoukai)**: Traditional Japanese Christian term for "church"
  - Literally 教 (kyou = teaching) + 会 (kai = gathering/meeting)
  - Used for institutional/traditional church contexts

- **チャーチ (chaachi)**: Katakana transliteration of English "church"
  - Used specifically for "Simple Church" (シンプル・チャーチ)
  - Creates linguistic distinction between traditional and new church-planting models
  - Modern, casual feel compared to traditional 教会

- **Strategic distinction**: The use of チャーチ for "Simple Church" may be intentional to:
  - Differentiate from traditional church (教会) concepts
  - Emphasize this is a new/different model
  - Make it feel more accessible and less institutionally bound

   **Japanese**: 福音

   **Literal**: The Gospel

## Translation Patterns and Insights

### 1. Honorific Language for God
The translation consistently uses honorific language when referring to God and His attributes:
- **御声** (mi-koe) instead of just 声 (koe) for "God's voice"
- **御国** (mikuni) instead of just 国 (kuni) for "God's Kingdom"
- Respectful verb forms with **〜られる** (rareru) suffix for God's actions

**Example**:
- 神が導かれる (kami ga michibikareru) = "God leads" (with respectful form)

### 2. Katakana for Modern Concepts
Modern ministry concepts are frequently transliterated into katakana rather than translated:
- **リーダー** (riidaa) = leader
- **コーチ** (koochi) = coach
- **メンター** (mentaa) = mentor
- **トレーニング** (toreeningu) = training
- **アカウンタビリティ** (akauntabiriti) = accountability
- **ビジョン** (bijon) = vision
- **グループ** (guruupu) = group

**Rationale**: This creates a modern, accessible feel and avoids confusion with traditional religious terminology.

### 3. Strategic Use of Traditional vs. Modern Terms

**Traditional Japanese Christian terms** (kanji):
- 神 (kami) = God
- 聖霊 (seirei) = Holy Spirit
- 弟子 (deshi) = disciple
- 福音 (fukuin) = gospel
- 聖書 (seisho) = Bible
- 洗礼 (senrei) = baptism
- 主の晩餐 (shu no bansan) = Lord's Supper
- 教会 (kyoukai) = church (traditional)

**Modern transliterations** (katakana):
- イエス (Iesu) = Jesus
- チャーチ (chaachi) = church (for "Simple Church")
- バイブル (baiburu) = Bible (in casual contexts)
- バプテスマ (baputesuma) = baptism (parenthetical)

### 4. Church Terminology Distinction
The translation makes a strategic linguistic distinction:
- **教会 (kyoukai)**: Used for traditional/institutional church
- **チャーチ (chaachi)**: Used specifically for "Simple Church" concept

This differentiation helps convey that "Simple Church" is a different model from traditional church structures.

### 5. Compound Verb Patterns

**分かち合う** (wakachiau) = "to share"
- Compound of 分かつ (wakatsu = divide) + 合う (au = mutual)
- Beautiful expression suggesting mutual participation
- Used for sharing gospel, testimony, stories

**従う** (shitagau) = "to follow/obey"
- Single verb carries both "follow" and "obey" meanings
- Core disciple concept integrated into one word
- Often paired with 聞く (kiku = hear): "hear and obey"

### 6. Heard, Obeyed, Shared, Trained Framework
The discipleship assessment framework keeps English terms with Japanese explanations:
- **Heard**（聞いた kiita）
- **Obeyed**（従った shitagatta）
- **Shared**（分かち合った wakachaiatta）
- **Trained**（訓練した kunren shita）

**Pattern**: English term + Japanese translation in parentheses using full-width parentheses （）

### 7. Model, Assist, Watch, Leave Framework
Similar pattern for coaching framework:
- **Model**（模範を示す mohan wo shimesu）
- **Assist**（援助する enjo suru）
- **Watch**（見守る mimamoru）
- **Leave**（任せる makaseru）

### 8. Practical Action-Oriented Language
The translation emphasizes practical action:
- 実践 (jissen) = practice/implementation (frequent use)
- 練習 (renshuu) = practice/rehearsal
- 訓練 (kunren) = training
- 方法 (houhou) = method/way

**Example**:
- "How to..." = 〜の方法 (no houhou) or 〜方 (kata)
- "Practice..." = 〜を実践する (wo jissen suru)

### 9. Respectful Language Levels
The translation maintains appropriate formal/respectful register:
- Uses **です/ます** (desu/masu) forms throughout
- Employs **ください** (kudasai) for polite requests
- Uses **ましょう** (mashou) for inclusive suggestions ("let's...")

**Example**:
- お願いしましょう (onegai shimashou) = "let's ask/request"
- 考えてみたことはありますか (kangaete mita koto wa arimasu ka) = "have you ever thought about"

### 10. Multiplication Language
**増員** (zouin) and **増やす** (fuyasu) are consistently used for "multiplication":
- 増員 = noun form (increase in personnel/members)
- 増やす = verb form (to multiply/increase)
- Pragmatic choice focusing on numerical increase rather than abstract "multiplication"

### 11. Cultural Adaptation: Prayer Walking
**祈りの散歩** (inori no sanpo) = "prayer walking"
- 散歩 (sanpo) = walk, stroll
- Natural Japanese expression that maintains meaning
- Not a direct transliteration but culturally adapted phrase

### 12. Mixed Script Usage
The translation fluidly combines three Japanese scripts:
- **Kanji** (漢字): For core concepts (神, 弟子, 福音, 聖書)
- **Hiragana** (ひらがな): For grammatical particles and native Japanese words
- **Katakana** (カタカナ): For foreign loanwords and modern concepts

**Example sentence**:
神の聖霊に、グループの時間を導いてくださるようにお願いしましょう。
- 神 (kanji) - God
- の (hiragana) - possessive particle
- 聖霊 (kanji) - Holy Spirit
- に (hiragana) - particle
- グループ (katakana) - group
- の (hiragana) - possessive
- 時間 (kanji) - time
- を (hiragana) - object marker
- 導いて (kanji+hiragana) - lead
- くださる (hiragana) - honorific giving verb
- ように (hiragana) - so that
- お願いしましょう (kanji+hiragana) - let's request

### 13. "3/3 Group" Translation
**3／3グループ** (san-bun-no-san guruupu)
- Uses full-width slash ／
- Keeps "group" in katakana
- Pattern maintains recognition while being pronounceable in Japanese

### 14. Acronym Handling
English acronyms are preserved with explanations when first introduced:
- **S.O.A.P.S.** stays in English, described as S.O.A.P.S.式 (shiki = style)
- **B.L.E.S.S.** stays in English, described as BLESS式祈り (shiki inori = style prayer)

### 15. "Simple" Concept Translation
**シンプル** (shinpuru) = "simple"
- Uses katakana rather than 簡単 (kantan = simple/easy)
- シンプル carries modern, minimalist connotation
- Different nuance from traditional 簡単 (which means "easy")

## Usage Recommendations for Translators

### 1. **Maintain Core Term Consistency**
Always use these established translations:
- 神 (kami) for God
- イエス (Iesu) for Jesus
- 聖霊 (seirei) for Holy Spirit
- 弟子 (deshi) for disciple
- 福音 (fukuin) for gospel
- 従う (shitagau) for follow/obey

### 2. **Use Honorifics for God**
- 御声 (mi-koe) for God's voice
- 御国 (mikuni) for God's Kingdom
- Respectful verb forms with 〜られる (rareru)

### 3. **Distinguish Church Terms**
- 教会 (kyoukai) for traditional church
- チャーチ (chaachi) for Simple Church
- Maintain this distinction to signal paradigm difference

### 4. **Keep Framework Terms in English**
For key teaching frameworks, retain English with Japanese explanations:
- Heard（聞いた）, Obeyed（従った）, Shared（分かち合った）, Trained（訓練した）
- Model（模範を示す）, Assist（援助する）, Watch（見守る）, Leave（任せる）

### 5. **Use Modern Katakana for Contemporary Concepts**
- リーダー (leader), コーチ (coach), メンター (mentor)
- トレーニング (training), グループ (group), ビジョン (vision)
- アカウンタビリティ (accountability)

### 6. **Maintain Formal-Polite Register**
- Use です/ます (desu/masu) forms
- Use ましょう (mashou) for inclusive suggestions
- Use ください (kudasai) for polite requests

### 7. **Emphasize Action and Practice**
- 実践する (jissen suru) = to practice/implement
- 練習する (renshuu suru) = to practice/rehearse
- 〜の方法 (no houhou) = method/way to...

### 8. **Use "Share" as 分かち合う**
This beautiful compound verb (wakachiau) should be consistently used for sharing gospel, testimony, stories - maintains the sense of mutual participation.

### 9. **Multiplication Language**
- 増員 (zouin) for multiplication (noun)
- 増やす (fuyasu) for multiply (verb)
- Don't use literal translation of "multiplication" mathematics term

### 10. **Natural Japanese Expressions**
Create natural phrases rather than word-for-word translations:
- "Prayer walking" = 祈りの散歩 (inori no sanpo)
- "God leads" = 神が導かれる (kami ga michibikareru)
