## Core Spiritual Concepts

### Spiritual / روحانی
**Punjabi:** روحانی
**Transliteration:** rūḥānī
**Literal Meaning:** "spiritual, of the spirit"
**Etymology:** From Arabic/Persian رُوح (rūḥ) meaning "spirit, soul"
**Usage Examples:**
- "Spiritual Breathing" = روحانی ساہ (rūḥānī sāh)
- "Spiritual Economy" = رُوحانی معیشت (rūḥānī ma'īshat)
- "Spiritual Growth" = رُوحانی ترقی (rūḥānī taraqqī)
- "Spiritual Practices" = رُوحانی مشقاں (rūḥānī mashqāṅ)

**Notes:** This is a Perso-Arabic loanword universally used in Pakistani Punjabi Islamic contexts. The term carries Islamic connotations but is appropriately used for Christian spiritual concepts as well.

## Divine Beings and Trinity

### God / خُدا
**Punjabi:** خُدا
**Transliteration:** Khudā
**Literal Meaning:** "God, the Divine"
**Etymology:** Persian خدا (Khudā), widely used in Islamic contexts
**Usage Examples:**
- "God uses ordinary people" = خُدا عام لوکاں نُوں ورتدا اے (Khudā 'ām lokāṅ nūṅ vartdā e)
- "Hearing and Obeying God" = خُدا دی گل سُننا تے عمل کرنا (Khudā dī gal sunnā te 'amal karnā)
- "God makes plans" = خُدا منصوبے بناندا اے (Khudā manṣūbe banāndā e)
- "What do you learn about God?" = خُدا دے بارے کیہ سِکھدے او؟ (Khudā de bāre keh sikhdē ō?)
- "God's voice" = خُدا دی آواز (Khudā dī āvāz)
- "God's Kingdom" = خُدا دی بادشاہی (Khudā dī bādshāhī)
- "God's Story" = خُدا دی کہانی (Khudā dī kahānī)

**Alternative:** رب (Rabb) - "Lord" (also used)
- "God Uses Ordinary People" = رب عام لوکاں نُوں ورتدا اے (Rabb 'ām lokāṅ nūṅ vartdā e)

**Notes:** Both خُدا (Khudā) and رب (Rabb) are used interchangeably. Khudā is Persian-derived and widely used across South Asian Muslim contexts. Rabb is Arabic (الرب) meaning "Lord" and carries connotations of sovereignty and lordship.

### Jesus / یسوع
**Punjabi:** یسوع
**Transliteration:** Yasū' / Yesū'
**Literal Meaning:** "Jesus" (transliteration)
**Etymology:** Arabic عيسى ('Īsā) adapted to یسوع (Yasū') for Christian contexts
**Usage Examples:**
- "How to Follow Jesus" = یسوع دی پیروی کیویں کرنی اے (Yasū' dī pairvī kiveṅ karnī e)
- "What is a follower of Jesus?" = یسوع دا پیروکار کیہ اے؟ (Yasū' dā pairvkār keh e?)
- "Activities of a follower of Jesus" = یسوع دے پیروکار دِیاں سرگرمیاں (Yasū' de pairvkār dīāṅ sargarmiāṅ)
- "Obey Jesus' commands" = یسوع دے حکماں تے عمل کرن (Yasū' de ḥukmāṅ te 'amal karan)
- "Becoming more like Jesus" = یسوع ورگے بننا (Yasū' varge bannā)
- "Speaking about Jesus" = یسوع دے بارے دسدے ہوئے (Yasū' de bāre dasde hōe)
- "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus" = یسوع دی پیروی تے حکماں تے عمل کرنا سکھانا

**Notes:** The name یسوع (Yasū') is the Christian transliteration, distinguishing it from the Quranic عيسى ('Īsā). This is an important contextual marker identifying Christian usage.

### Holy Spirit / خُدا دے رُوح / رُوح القدس
**Punjabi:** خُدا دے رُوح
**Transliteration:** Khudā de Rūḥ
**Literal Meaning:** "God's Spirit"
**Alternative:** رُوح القدس (Rūḥ al-Quds) - "Holy Spirit" (Arabic)
**Usage Examples:**
- "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time" = خُدا دے رُوح اگے درخواست کرو کہ تہاڈے کٹھ ویلے تہاڈی راہنمائی کرے (Khudā de Rūḥ age darkhvāst karō ki tuhāḍe kaṭh vēle tuhāḍī rāhnumāī kare)

**Notes:** The phrase uses a descriptive construction rather than a single compound word, emphasizing the Spirit's relationship to God.

### Lord / رب / عشائے ربانی
**Punjabi:** رب
**Transliteration:** Rabb
**Literal Meaning:** "Lord, Master, Sustainer"
**Etymology:** Arabic الرب (ar-Rabb)
**Usage Examples:**
- "The Lord's Supper" = عشائے ربانی ('ishā'e rabbānī)
- "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" = عشائے ربانی دا جشن مناؤ ('ishā'e rabbānī dā jashn manā'ō)

**Notes:** Used both as a general title for God and specifically in compound religious terms. عشائے ربانی ('ishā'e rabbānī) for "Lord's Supper" uses classical Arabic-derived terminology.

## Gospel and Salvation

### Gospel / خوشخبری / انجیل
**Punjabi:** خوشخبری (primary), انجیل (alternative)
**Transliteration:** khushkhabrī, injīl
**Literal Meaning:** خوشخبری = "good news" (Persian), انجیل = "Gospel" (Arabic, from Greek εὐαγγέλιον)
**Etymology:**
- خوشخبری: Persian خوش (khush - "good, happy") + خبری (khabrī - "news")
- انجیل: Arabic إنجيل (injīl), Islamic term for the Gospel

**Usage Examples:**
- "The Gospel" = خوشخبری (khushkhabrī)
- "The Gospel and How to Share It" = خُوشخبری نُوں سُناؤن دا طریقہ (khūshkhabrī nūṅ sunā'ūn dā ṭarīqah)
- "Share the Gospel" = خوشخبری سُناؤ (khushkhabrī sunā'ō)
- "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" = 3-دائرے خوشخبری دی پیشکش (3-dā'ire khushkhabrī dī pēshkash)
- "3 Circles Gospel Presentation" = 3 حلقے انجیل پریزنٹیشن (3 ḥalqe injīl prezinṭēshan)
- "Sharing the Gospel" = خوشخبری سُنانا (khushkhabrī sunānā)
- "Practice Sharing the Gospel" = خوشخبری سُنان دی مشق کرو (khushkhabrī sunān dī mashq karō)
- "Take turns telling the Gospel to each other" = اِک دُوجے نُوںواری واری کہانی سُناؤ (ik dūje nūṅ vārī vārī kahānī sunā'ō)

**Notes:** Both terms are used, with خوشخبری (khushkhabrī) being more descriptive and انجیل (injīl) being the formal religious term familiar from Islamic contexts.

### Baptism / بپتسمہ
**Punjabi:** بپتسمہ
**Transliteration:** baptismah
**Literal Meaning:** "baptism" (transliteration from Greek βάπτισμα via Urdu)
**Usage Examples:**
- "Baptism" = بپتسمہ (baptismah)
- "Baptism and How To Do It" = بپتسمہ دین دا طریقہ (baptismah dēn dā ṭarīqah)
- "Have you ever baptized someone?" = کیہ تُسی کدی کِسے نُوں بپتسمہ دِتا اے؟ (keh tusī kadī kise nūṅ baptismah ditā e?)
- "IMPORTANT REMINDER – Have you been baptized?" = اہم یاددہانی - کیہ تُسی بپتسمہ لیا اے؟ (aham yāddahānī - keh tusī baptismah laiyā e?)

**Notes:** Direct transliteration of the Christian sacramental term, clearly marking Christian religious practice distinct from Islamic ritual washing.

### Believer / اِیماندار
**Punjabi:** اِیماندار
**Transliteration:** īmāndār
**Literal Meaning:** "faithful one, believer"
**Etymology:** Persian/Urdu ایمان (īmān - "faith") + دار (dār - suffix meaning "having, possessing")
**Usage Examples:**
- "Share the 'Training Cycle' with a believer on your list of 100" = اپنی 100 دی لِسٹ چوں اِک اِیماندار نُوں "تربیتی چکر" بارے دسو (apnī 100 dī lisṭ chōṅ ik īmāndār nūṅ "tarbiyatī chakkar" bāre dasō)

**Opposite:** بے دِین (bē dīn) - "unbeliever, without religion"

**Notes:** Uses the Islamic-derived concept of faith (ایمان - īmān) but applies it to Christian belief. The term is religiously neutral and used across faith traditions in South Asia.

### Unbeliever / بے دِین
**Punjabi:** بے دِین
**Transliteration:** bē dīn
**Literal Meaning:** "without religion/faith"
**Etymology:** Persian/Urdu بے (bē - "without") + دین (dīn - "religion, faith")
**Notes:** General term for someone without faith, used across religious contexts in Pakistan.

### Salvation (implied through contextual usage)
**Notes:** While "salvation" doesn't appear as a standalone term in the extracted data, the concept is embedded in gospel terminology and redemptive language.

## Church and Community

### Church / کلیسیا / گرجیاں
**Punjabi:** کلیسیا (singular), کلیسیاواں (plural)
**Alternative plural:** گرجیاں (girjiyāṅ)
**Transliteration:** kalīsiyā, kalīsiyāvāṅ, girjiyāṅ
**Literal Meaning:** "church, assembly"
**Etymology:**
- کلیسیا: From Greek ἐκκλησία (ekklesia) via Urdu
- گرجا: From Portuguese igreja, used in Indian subcontinent

**Usage Examples:**
- "Church" = کلیسیا (kalīsiyā)
- "Churches" = کلیسیاواں (kalīsiyāvāṅ) / گرجیاں (girjiyāṅ)
- "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" = شاگرد تے کلیسیا تی سادہ تعریف (shāgird te kalīsiyā tī sādah ta'rīf)
- "Disciples and the Church" = شاگرد تے کلیسیا (shāgird te kalīsiyā)
- "When you think of a church, what comes to mind?" = جدوں تُسی کلیسیا دا سوچدے او تے ذہن وِچ کیہ آؤندا اے؟ (jadōṅ tusī kalīsiyā dā sōchde ō te zehan vich keh ā'ūndā e?)
- "Always Part of Two Churches" = ہمیشہ دو کلیسیاواں دا حصہ (hamēshah dō kalīsiyāvāṅ dā ḥiṣṣah)
- "Churches Needed" = گرجیاں دی لوڑ اے (girjiyāṅ dī lōṛ e)
- "Churches Reported" = کلیسیاواں دی رپوٹ (kalīsiyāvāṅ dī rapōṭ)
- "Map of Zúme Churches" = زُومی کلیسیاواں دا نقشہ (Zūmī kalīsiyāvāṅ dā naqshah)
- "My Churches" = میریاں کلیسیاواں (mērīāṅ kalīsiyāvāṅ)
- "Church Name" = کلیسیا دا ناں (kalīsiyā dā nāṅ)
- "Church Location" = کلیسیا دا مقام (kalīsiyā dā maqām)
- "Parent Church" = مائی باپ کلیسیا (māī bāp kalīsiyā)
- "2 Simple Churches" = 2 سادہ کلیسیاواں (2 sādah kalīsiyāvāṅ)
- "What's a Church?" = کلیسیا کیہ اے؟ (kalīsiyā keh e?)
- "Leading a Simple Church" = سادہ کلیسیا دی راہنمائی کرنا (sādah kalīsiyā dī rāhnumāī karnā)
- "Simple church vision map" = سادہ کلیسیا دی رویا دا نقشہ (sādah kalīsiyā dī rōyā dā naqshah)
- "Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church?" = کیہ 3/3 دے گروپ نُوں سادہ کلیسیا سمجھیا جا سکدا اے؟ (keh 3/3 de grōp nūṅ sādah kalīsiyā samjhiyā jā sakdā e?)

**Notes:** کلیسیا (kalīsiyā) is the standard Christian term. The alternative گرجا (girjā) is Portuguese-derived and commonly used in India/Pakistan. The translation emphasizes "simple church" (سادہ کلیسیا - sādah kalīsiyā) as a key concept.

### Community / کمیونٹی / برادری
**Punjabi:** کمیونٹی, برادری
**Transliteration:** kamyūniṭī, barādrī
**Literal Meaning:**
- کمیونٹی: "community" (English loanword)
- برادری: "brotherhood, community" (Persian/Urdu)

**Usage Examples:**
- "Join the Zúme community" = زُومی برادری وِچ شا مل ہو جاؤ (Zūmī barādrī vich shāmil hō jā'ō)
- "Join Zume community using the QR code" = کیو آر کوڈ ورتدے ہوئے زُومی کمیونٹی چےشامل ہو جاؤ (QR kōḍ vartde hōe Zūmī kamyūniṭī che shāmil hō jā'ō)
- "You have joined the community" = تُسی کمیونٹی چے شامل ہوگئے او (tusī kamyūniṭī che shāmil hō gae ō)
- "Join the community" = کمیونٹی چے شمولیت کرو (kamyūniṭī che shumūliyat karō)

**Notes:** Both English loanword (کمیونٹی) and native Urdu/Persian term (برادری - "brotherhood") are used interchangeably.

### Body Life / جسم دی زندگی
**Punjabi:** جسم دی زندگی
**Transliteration:** jism dī zindagī
**Literal Meaning:** "body's life" - referring to the body of Christ, the church community
**Etymology:** جسم (jism - "body") + زندگی (zindagī - "life")
**Notes:** Metaphorical usage referring to corporate church life and community.

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple / شاگرد
**Punjabi:** شاگرد
**Transliteration:** shāgird
**Literal Meaning:** "student, learner, disciple"
**Etymology:** Persian شاگرد (shāgird) - traditional term for student/disciple in Islamic educational contexts
**Usage Examples:**
- "Disciple" = شاگرد (shāgird)
- "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" = شاگرد ہون دا مطلب ہے اسی خُدا دی گل سُنندے تےاوہدے حکماں تے عمل کرنے آں (shāgird hōn dā maṭlab he asī Khudā dī gal sunnde te ōhde ḥukmāṅ te 'amal karne āṅ)
- "What does my disciple need" = میرے شاگرد نُوں کس چَے دی لوڑ اے (mēre shāgird nūṅ kis chae dī lōṛ e)
- "Being A Disciple" = شاگرد بن کے (shāgird ban ke)
- "What's a Disciple?" = شاگرد کیہ اے؟ (shāgird keh e?)
- "Making Disciples" = شاگرد بنانا (shāgird banānā)
- "Empowering Disciples" = شاگرداں نُوں اِختیار دینا (shāgirdāṅ nūṅ ikhtiyār dēnā)

**Compound Terms:**
- "Trained multiplying disciple" = سِکھیا ہویا وادھا کرن والا شاگرد (sikhiyā hōyā vādhā karan vālā shāgird)
- "To saturate the world with multiplying disciples in our generation" = اپنی نسل وِچ واداھا کرنے والے شاگرداں نال دُنیا نُوں بھر دیو (apnī nasal vich vādhā karne vāle shāgirdāṅ nāl dunyā nūṅ bhar dēō)
- "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" = شاگرداں نُوں بالغ کرن لئی تربیتی چکر (shāgirdāṅ nūṅ bāligh karan la'ī tarbiyatī chakkar)

**Notes:** The term شاگرد (shāgird) is deeply embedded in South Asian Islamic educational terminology (teacher-student relationship), making it naturally comprehensible for the concept of discipleship.

### Discipleship / شاگردی
**Punjabi:** شاگردی
**Transliteration:** shāgirdī
**Literal Meaning:** "the state/practice of being a disciple"
**Usage Examples:**
- "Duckling Discipleship" = بطخ دے چُوچیاں دی شاگردی (baṭakh de chūchiyāṅ dī shāgirdī)
- "Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately" = بطخ دے چُوچے دی شاگردی - پہلے ہتھ قیادت (baṭakh de chūche dī shāgirdī - pahle hath qiyādat)
- "Discipleship Concepts" = شاگردی دے تصورات (shāgirdī de taṣavvurāt)

**Notes:** The suffix ی (-ī) creates an abstract noun indicating the state or practice of discipleship.

### Follower / پیروکار
**Punjabi:** پیروکار
**Transliteration:** pairvkār
**Literal Meaning:** "follower, adherent"
**Etymology:** Persian/Urdu پیرو (pairv - "following") + کار (kār - "doer")
**Usage Examples:**
- "What is a follower of Jesus?" = یسوع دا پیروکار کیہ اے؟ (Yasū' dā pairvkār keh e?)
- "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" = یسوع دے پیروکار دِیاں سرگرمیاں کیہ نیں؟ (Yasū' de pairvkār dīāṅ sargarmiāṅ keh nēṅ?)

**Related verb:** پیروی (pairvī) - "following"
- "How to Follow Jesus" = یسوع دی پیروی کیویں کرنی اے (Yasū' dī pairvī kiveṅ karnī e)

**Notes:** Standard term for follower/adherent used across religious contexts in Urdu/Punjabi.

### Trainee / سِکھن والا
**Punjabi:** سِکھن والا
**Transliteration:** sikhan vālā
**Literal Meaning:** "one who learns, learner"
**Etymology:** Punjabi سِکھنا (sikhnā - "to learn") + والا (vālā - agentive suffix)
**Usage Examples:**
- "trainee" = سِکھن والا (sikhan vālā)
- "A new trainee from... has registered!" = توں اِک نواں سِکھن والا داخل ہوگیا اے (tōṅ ik navāṅ sikhan vālā dākhil hō gayā e)
- "A coach is modeling for a trainee" = اک کوچ اک سِکھنوالے نُوں نمونہ وکھا رہیا اے (ik kōch ik sikhanvāle nūṅ namūnah vikhā rahiyā e)
- "Trainee vision map" = سکھن والیاں دی رویا دا نقشہ (sikhan vāliyāṅ dī rōyā dā naqshah)

**Notes:** Descriptive construction using the native Punjabi verb "to learn" with agentive suffix.

## Prayer and Spiritual Practices

### Prayer / دُعا
**Punjabi:** دُعا
**Transliteration:** du'ā
**Literal Meaning:** "prayer, supplication"
**Etymology:** Arabic دعاء (du'ā')
**Usage Examples:**
- "Prayer" = دُعا (du'ā)
- "How to Spend an Hour in Prayer" = دُعا چے اِک گھنٹہ گزارن دا طریقہ (du'ā che ik ghanṭah guzāran dā ṭarīqah)
- "See how easy it is to spend an hour in prayer" = ویکھو کہ دُعا چے اِک گھنٹہ گزارنا کِڈا سوکھا اے (vēkhō ki du'ā che ik ghanṭah guzārnā kiḍā sōkhā e)
- "What is your reaction to spending an hour in prayer?" = دُعا وِچ اِک گھنٹہ گزارن تے تہاڈا ردِ عمل کیہ اے؟ (du'ā vich ik ghanṭah guzāran te tuhāḍā rad-i-'amal keh e?)
- "Prayer Walking" = دُعائیہ سَیر (du'ā'iyah sair)
- "Prayer Walking and How To Do It" = دُعائیہ سَیر تے اوہدا طریقہ (du'ā'iyah sair te ōhdā ṭarīqah)
- "This session ends with the prayer walking activity" = ایس سیشن دا خاتمہ دُعائیہ سَیردی سرگرمی نال ہوندا اے (is sēshan dā khātimah du'ā'iyah sair dī sargaramī nāl hōndā e)
- "Prayer Cycle" = دُعائیہ چکر (du'ā'iyah chakkar)
- "The Prayer Cycle" = دُعائیہ چکر (du'ā'iyah chakkar)
- "Pray the Prayer Cycle for an hour individually" = اک گھنٹہ کلیاں دُعائیہ چکر دی دُعا کرو (ik ghanṭah kaliyāṅ du'ā'iyah chakkar dī du'ā karō)
- "The BLESS Prayer Pattern" = برکت دی دُعا دا نمُونہ (barkat dī du'ā dā namūnah)
- "The B.L.E.S.S. Prayer" = بی۔ ایل۔ ای۔ ایس۔ ایس۔ دُعا (B.L.E.S.S. du'ā)
- "B.L.E.S.S. Prayer" = بی ایل ای ایس ایس۔ دُعا (B.L.E.S.S. du'ā)

### Bible / بائبل / پاک کلام
**Punjabi:** بائبل, پاک کلام
**Transliteration:** bā'ibil, pāk kalām
**Literal Meaning:**
- بائبل: "Bible" (transliteration)
- پاک کلام: "Holy Word" (Persian/Urdu: پاک "holy" + کلام "word/speech")

**Usage Examples:**
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" = ایس اہ اے پی ایس بائبل دا مطالعہ (S.O.A.P.S. bā'ibil dā muṭāli'ah)
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading" = ایس او اے پی ایس بائبل دی پڑھائی (S.O.A.P.S. bā'ibil dī paṛhā'ī)
- "Bible Reading" = بائبل دی پڑھائی (bā'ibil dī paṛhā'ī)
- "Personal Bible Study" = بائبل دا شخصی مطالعہ (bā'ibil dā shakhṣī muṭāli'ah)
- "Scripture" = پاک کلام (pāk kalām)

**Notes:** بائبل (bā'ibil) is the standard transliteration. پاک کلام (pāk kalām - "Holy Word") is a respectful alternative emphasizing sacredness, parallel to Quranic terminology.

### Kingdom / بادشاہی
**Punjabi:** بادشاہی
**Transliteration:** bādshāhī
**Literal Meaning:** "kingdom, sovereignty, reign"
**Etymology:** Persian/Urdu بادشاہ (bādshāh - "king, emperor") + ی (-ī suffix)
**Usage Examples:**
- "Eyes to See Where the Kingdom Isn't" = ویکھ آلیاں اکھاں کہ بادشاہی کِتھے نئیں ہیگی (vēkh āliyāṅ akhāṅ ki bādshāhī kithe na'īṅ hēgī)
- "Have you ever stopped to think about where God's Kingdom... isn't?" = context implies بادشاہی (bādshāhī)

**Notes:** Standard Persian-derived term for kingdom/sovereignty, appropriate for "Kingdom of God" concept.

### Lord's Supper / Communion / عشائے ربانی
**Punjabi:** عشائے ربانی
**Transliteration:** 'ishā'e rabbānī
**Literal Meaning:** "Lord's Supper" (Arabic: عشاء 'ishā' "evening meal/supper" + ربانی rabbānī "of the Lord")
**Usage Examples:**
- "The Lord's Supper" = عشائے ربانی ('ishā'e rabbānī)
- "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" = عشائے ربانی تے اوہنُوں کرن دا طریقہ ('ishā'e rabbānی te ōhnūṅ karan dā ṭarīqah)
- "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" = عشائے ربانی دا جشن مناؤ ('ishā'e rabbānی dā jashn manā'ō)

**Notes:** Classical Arabic-derived terminology for this Christian sacrament, using ربانی (rabbānī - "of the Lord") to emphasize its divine institution.

### Person of Peace / سلامتی دا فرزند
**Punjabi:** سلامتی دا فرزند
**Transliteration:** salāmatī dā farzand
**Literal Meaning:** "child/person of peace"
**Etymology:** Arabic سلامة (salāmah - "peace, safety") + فرزند (farzand - "child, offspring" - Persian)
**Usage Examples:**
- "A Person of Peace and How To Find One" = سلامتی دا فرزند تے اوہنُوں لبھن دا طریقہ (salāmatī dā farzand te ōhnūṅ labhan dā ṭarīqah)
- "Person of Peace" = سلامتی دا فرزند (salāmatī dā farzand)

**Notes:** Beautiful fusion of Arabic (سلامتی - "peace") and Persian (فرزند - "child/person"), conveying the biblical concept of a receptive person who brings peace.

## Linguistic Notes

### Script and Writing System
- **Script:** Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی) - Perso-Arabic script adapted for Punjabi
- **Direction:** Right-to-left (RTL)
- **Character Set:** Arabic alphabet with additional diacritics and letters for Punjabi-specific sounds
- **Vowel Markers:** Uses Arabic vowel diacritics (zabar, zer, pesh) though often omitted in standard writing
- **Regional Context:** Used primarily in Pakistan, contrasting with Gurmukhi script used in Indian Punjab

### Linguistic Characteristics

#### Translation Strategy

2. **Native Punjabi for Actions:** Core verbs and actions use native Punjabi roots (سُننا - "hear," سکھنا - "learn," ویکھنا - "see").

5. **Contextual Adaptation:** Terms adapt to Islamic cultural familiarity while maintaining Christian theological distinctiveness (e.g., یسوع Yasū' for Jesus, distinguishing from Quranic عيسى 'Īsā).

### Phonological Notes
- **Aspiration:** Punjabi maintains aspiration contrasts (پ p vs. پھ ph, ت t vs. تھ th)
- **Retroflex Consonants:** Uses retroflex series (ٹ ṭ, ڈ ḍ, ڑ ṛ)
- **Nasalization:** Employs nasalization marker (ں - nūn ghunnah)
- **Gemination:** Double consonants indicated by tashdīd (ّ )

### Grammar Notes
- **Postpositions:** Uses postpositions rather than prepositions (نُوں nūṅ - "to," نال nāl - "with," وِچ vich - "in")
- **Ergativity:** Shows ergative-absolutive alignment in past tense constructions
- **Agentive Suffix:** والا (vālā) creates agent nouns (سکھن والا sikhan vālā - "learner," تربیت دین والے tarbiyat dēn vāle - "trainer")
- **Gender:** Maintains grammatical gender (masculine/feminine)
- **Honorifics:** Uses plural forms for respect (تُسی tusī - "you" honorific)

Pakistani Punjabi (Shahmukhi) shows linguistic distinctiveness:
