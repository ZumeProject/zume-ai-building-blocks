## Core Deity Terms

### God
**Marathi**: देव (deva)
**Transliteration**: dev
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanword, primary term for deity
**Usage**: Consistently used throughout the translation

**Examples**:
- "God uses ordinary people" → "देव सामान्य लोकांचा उपयोग करितो"
- "Hearing and Obeying God" → "देवाचे ऐकणे आणि त्याचे पालन करणे"
- "God's story" → "देवाची गोष्ट" / "देवाची कहाणी"
- "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "शिष्य बनण्याचा अर्थ आहे कि आपण देवाकडून ऐकतो आणि आपण देवाची आज्ञा पाळितो"
- "What do you learn about God?" → "तुम्ही देवाबद्दल काय शिकलात?"
- "God makes plans" → "देव योजना बनवतो"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Genitive form: देवाचे (devache) - "of God"
- Oblique form: देवाकडून (devakadun) - "from God"
- Possessive: देवाची (devachi) - "God's" (feminine), देवाचा (devacha) - "God's" (masculine)

### Jesus
**Marathi**: येशू (yeshu)
**Transliteration**: yeshu
**Etymology**: Transliteration from Greek/English via Portuguese influence
**Usage**: Standard Christian terminology in Marathi

**Examples**:
- "How to Follow Jesus" → "येशू अनुसरण कसे करावे"
- "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "येशूचा अनुयायी काय आहे?"
- "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" → "येशूच्या अनुयायाचे कार्य काय आहेत?"
- "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "येशूच्या आज्ञा कशा पाळाव्यात ते शिका"
- "Speaking about Jesus" → "येशूबद्दल बोलणे"
- "Becoming more like Jesus... Together" → "येशूसारखे बनणे... एकत्र"
- "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus" → "इतरांना तुमच्या बरोबर येशूचे आज्ञापालन आणि अनुसरण करणे शिकविणे"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Genitive: येशूचा/येशूचे/येशूची (yeshucha/yesuche/yeshuchi) - "Jesus'" or "of Jesus"
- No variations observed; consistently येशू throughout

### Christ
**Status**: NOT FOUND as standalone term
**Note**: The translation appears to use येशू (Jesus) rather than a separate term for "Christ" (which would typically be ख्रिस्त/Khrista in formal Marathi Christian contexts)

### Holy Spirit
**Marathi**: पवित्र आत्मा (pavitra atma)
**Transliteration**: pavitra atma
**Etymology**: पवित्र (pavitra) = holy/sacred (Sanskrit), आत्मा (atma) = spirit/soul (Sanskrit)
**Usage**: Compound term combining two Sanskrit loanwords

**Examples**:
- "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together" → "देवाच्या पवित्र आत्म्यास तुमच्या वेळेचे एकत्रितपणे नेतृत्व करण्यास सांगा"
- "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time, together" → "देवाच्या पवित्र आत्म्याला तुमच्या वेळेचे एकत्रितपणे नेतृत्व करण्यास सांगा"
- "When the Holy Spirit comes upon you" → "परंतु पवित्र आत्मा तुमच्यावर येईल"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Oblique case: पवित्र आत्म्यास/पवित्र आत्म्याला (pavitra atmyas/atmyala) - "to the Holy Spirit"
- Possessive construct: देवाच्या पवित्र आत्म्यास - "God's Holy Spirit"

### Lord
**Marathi**: प्रभू (prabhu)
**Transliteration**: prabhu
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanword meaning "master, lord, sovereign"
**Usage**: Used in formal contexts, particularly in "Lord's Supper"

**Examples**:
- "The Lord's Supper" → "प्रभू भोजन"
- "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" → "प्रभु भोजन आणि त्याचे नेतृत्व कसे करावे"
- "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "प्रभू भोजनाचा सराव करा"
- "Hearing and responding to the Lord" → "परमेश्वराचे ऐकणे आणि त्याला प्रतिसाद देणे"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Also परमेश्वर (parmeshwar) used in some contexts meaning "supreme Lord/God"
- परमेश्वराचे (parmeshwarache) - "of the Lord"

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
**Marathi**: शिष्य (shishya)
**Transliteration**: shishya
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanword, traditional term for student/pupil, carries deep spiritual connotation
**Usage**: Core term used extensively throughout the translation

**Examples**:
- "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "शिष्य आणि चर्चची साधी व्याख्या"
- "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "शिष्य बनण्याचा अर्थ आहे कि आपण देवाकडून ऐकतो आणि आपण देवाची आज्ञा पाळितो"
- "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" → "परिपक्व होणाऱ्या शिष्यांसाठी प्रशिक्षण चक्र"
- "What's a Disciple?" → "शिष्य म्हणजे काय?"
- "Being A Disciple" → "शिष्य असणे"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Plural: शिष्यांसाठी (shishyansathi) - "for disciples"
- Possessive: शिष्याचे/शिष्याला (shishyache/shishyala) - "of/to the disciple"
- The term carries traditional guru-shishya (teacher-student) relationship connotations

### Disciple-making / Making Disciples
**Marathi**: शिष्य बनवणे (shishya banvane)
**Transliteration**: shishya banvane
**Construction**: शिष्य (disciple) + बनवणे (to make/create)
**Usage**: Verbal noun form indicating the action of making disciples

**Examples**:
- "Making Disciples" → "शिष्य बनवणे"
- "Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making" → "बदकाच्या पिल्लांचा शिष्य बनविण्याशी काय संबंध आहे"
- "Train disciples how to make disciples" → "शिष्यांना प्रशिक्षण द्या कि 20 तासांच्या कोर्स सामग्रीद्वारे शिष्य कसे बनवावे"
- "Disciple making doesn't have to be linear" → "शिष्य बनविणे कसे रेषीय नसणे गरजेचे नाही"

### Discipleship
**Marathi**: शिष्यत्व (shishyatva)
**Transliteration**: shishyatva
**Etymology**: शिष्य (disciple) + त्व (-ness/-hood, abstract noun suffix)
**Usage**: Abstract noun form

**Examples**:
- "Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately" → "बदकाच्या पिल्लू सारिखे शिष्यत्व - त्वरित पुढाकार करताना"
- "Duckling Discipleship" → "बदकाच्या पिल्लू सारखे शिष्यत्व"
- "Discipleship Concepts" → "शिष्यत्व संकल्पना"
- "What is one area of discipleship" → "शिष्यत्वाचे ते एक क्षेत्र कोणते आहे"

### Follow / Follower
**Marathi**: अनुसरण (anusaran) / अनुयायी (anuyayi)
**Transliteration**: anusaran (verb) / anuyayi (noun)
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanwords - अनु (anu) = after/following + सरण (saran) = moving
**Usage**: Standard terms for following and follower

**Examples**:
- "How to Follow Jesus" → "येशू अनुसरण कसे करावे"
- "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "येशूचा अनुयायी काय आहे?"
- "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" → "येशूच्या अनुयायाचे कार्य काय आहेत?"
- "Every follower of Jesus" → "येशूच्या प्रत्येक अनुयायाला"
- "If Jesus intended every one of His followers to obey" → "जर येशूची संकल्पना होती कि त्याच्या प्रत्येक अनुयायांनी"
- "New followers" → "नवीन अनुयायांनी"

**Contextual Notes**:
- अनुसरण (anusaran) is the verbal noun "following/to follow"
- अनुयायी (anuyayi) is the agent noun "one who follows, follower"
- Genitive: अनुयायाचे (anuyayache) - "of the follower"

### Obey / Obedience
**Marathi**: आज्ञापालन (ajnyapalan) / पालन (palan) / आज्ञाधारक (ajnyadharak)
**Transliteration**: ajnyapalan / palan / ajnyadharak
**Etymology**:
- आज्ञा (ajnya) = command/order (Sanskrit)
- पालन (palan) = observance/compliance (Sanskrit)
- आज्ञाधारक (ajnyadharak) = obedient (adjective)

**Examples**:
- "Hearing and Obeying God" → "देवाचे ऐकणे आणि त्याचे पालन करणे"
- "Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God" → "आध्यात्मिक श्वास म्हणजे देवाचे ऐकणे आणि त्याचे पालन करणे होय"
- "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "येशूच्या आज्ञा कशा पाळाव्यात ते शिका"
- "Heard, Obeyed, Shared, Trained" → "ऐकलेले, पालन केलेले, सामायिक केलेले, प्रशिक्षित"
- "Obeyed" → "आज्ञापालन केले"
- "Obey" → "आज्ञापालन करा"
- "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus" → "इतरांना तुमच्या बरोबर येशूचे आज्ञापालन आणि अनुसरण करणे शिकविणे"
- "The Power of Obedient Faith" → "आज्ञाधारक विश्वासाची शक्ती"

**Contextual Notes**:
- आज्ञापालन (ajnyapalan) is the formal term for "obedience"
- पालन (palan) is shorter form meaning "observance, compliance"
- आज्ञाधारक (ajnyadharak) is the adjective "obedient"
- आज्ञा (ajnya) alone means "command" - येशूच्या आज्ञा = "Jesus' commands"

### Hear / Hearing
**Marathi**: ऐकणे (aikane) / ऐकले (aikle)
**Transliteration**: aikane / aikle
**Etymology**: Native Marathi verb root
**Usage**: Standard verb for hearing/listening

**Examples**:
- "Hearing and Obeying God" → "देवाचे ऐकणे आणि त्याचे पालन करणे"
- "We hear from God" → "आपण देवाकडून ऐकतो"
- "Heard" → "ऐकले"
- "Hearing from God regularly" → "देवाचे नियमितपणे ऐकणे"
- "Why is it essential to learn to hear and recognize God's voice?" → "देवाचा आवाज ऐकणे आणि ओळखणे शिकणे का आवश्यक आहे?"

### Train / Training
**Marathi**: प्रशिक्षण (prashikshan) / प्रशिक्षित (prashikshit)
**Transliteration**: prashikshan / prashikshit
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanword from प्र (pra) = forth + शिक्षण (shikshan) = teaching/education
**Usage**: Standard term for training/education

**Examples**:
- "Training" → "प्रशिक्षण"
- "Trainings" → "प्रशिक्षण"
- "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" → "परिपक्व होणाऱ्या शिष्यांसाठी प्रशिक्षण चक्र"
- "Training Cycle" → "प्रशिक्षण चक्र"
- "Trainee" → "प्रशिक्षणार्थी"
- "Trained" → "प्रशिक्षित"
- "Train disciples how to make disciples" → "शिष्यांना प्रशिक्षण द्या कि 20 तासांच्या कोर्स सामग्रीद्वारे शिष्य कसे बनवावे"

## Church Terms

### Church
**Marathi**: चर्च (church)
**Transliteration**: church
**Etymology**: English loanword borrowed directly, reflecting Portuguese colonial influence
**Usage**: Transliterated English term, not translated to native word

**Examples**:
- "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "शिष्य आणि चर्चची साधी व्याख्या"
- "Disciples and the Church" → "शिष्य आणि चर्च"
- "What's a Church?" → "चर्च म्हणजे काय?"
- "Churches Needed" → "चर्च आवश्यक"
- "Churches Reported" → "चर्चने रिपोर्ट केले गेले"
- "Churches" → "चर्चेस" (plural with English -es suffix)
- "My Churches" → "माझे चर्चेस"
- "Church Name" → "चर्चचे नाव"
- "Church Location" → "चर्च स्थान"
- "Parent Church" → "पालक चर्च"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Genitive: चर्चची (churchchi) - "of church" (feminine)
- चर्चचे (churchche) - "church's" (neuter/possessive)
- Plural: चर्चेस (churches) - borrowed English plural form
- Not translated to मंडळी (mandali) which is the traditional term for congregation/assembly

### Simple Church
**Marathi**: साधारण चर्च (sadharan church)
**Transliteration**: sadharan church
**Etymology**: साधारण (sadharan) = simple/ordinary (Sanskrit) + चर्च (church) = English loanword
**Usage**: Technical term for house church/simple church model

**Examples**:
- "2 Simple Churches" → "2 साधारण चर्च"
- "Always report new simple churches that you are starting" → "तुम्ही सुरू करत असलेल्या नवीन साधारण चर्चची नेहमी आमच्याकडे नोंदणी (रिपोर्ट) करा"
- "2 simple churches per 5,000 people" → "प्रत्येकी 5,000 लोकांमागे 2 साधारण चर्च"
- "Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church?" → "काय 3/3 गटाला एक साधारण चर्च मानले जाऊ शकते?"
- "Simple church vision map" → "साधारण चर्च दृष्टी (विजन) नकाशा"
- "Leading a Simple Church" → "साधारण चर्चचे नेतृत्व करणे"

### Community
**Marathi**: समुदाय (samuday)
**Transliteration**: samuday
**Etymology**: Sanskrit loanword - सम् (sam) = together + उदय (uday) = rising
**Usage**: Standard term for community/society

**Examples**:
- "Community" → "समुदाय"
- "Join the Zúme community" → "Zúme समुदायात सामील व्हा"
- "Join the Community" → "समुदायात सामील व्हा"

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
**Marathi**: सुवार्ता (suvarta)
**Transliteration**: suvarta
**Etymology**: Sanskrit-derived: सु (su) = good + वार्ता (varta) = news/tidings
**Usage**: Standard Christian term for "gospel" meaning "good news"

**Examples**:
- "The Gospel and How to Share It" → "सुवार्ता आणि ती इतरांना कशी सांगावी"
- "The Gospel" → "सुवार्ता"
- "Share the Gospel" → "सुवार्ता सांगा"
- "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" → "3-वर्तुळे सुवार्ता सादरीकरण"
- "Sharing the Gospel" → "सुवार्ता सांगणे"
- "Practice Sharing the Gospel" → "सुवार्ता सांगण्याचा सराव करा"
- "Take turns telling the Gospel to each other" → "एक-एक करून एकमेकांना सुवार्ता सांगा"
- "One way to share God's good news" → "देवाची सुवार्ता सांगण्याचा एक मार्ग"

**Contextual Notes**:
- Also appears as शुभवर्तमान (shubhvartaman) in one instance: "३ मंडळे शुभवर्तमान सादरीकरण" (3 Circles Gospel Presentation) - more formal/literary term
- सुवार्ता is the preferred, more common form

### Bible
**Marathi**: बायबल (bible)
**Transliteration**: bible
**Etymology**: Direct English loanword
**Usage**: Transliterated term, not translated

**Examples**:
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" → "S.O.A.P.S. बायबल अध्ययन"
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading" → "S.O.A.P.S. बायबल अध्ययन"
- "Bible Reading" → "बायबल वाचन"
- "Personal Bible Study" → "वैयक्तिक बायबल अभ्यास"
- "The Bible tells us" → "बायबल आपल्याला सांगते"

**Contextual Notes**:
- वाचन (vachan) = reading
- अध्ययन (adhyayan) = study
- अभ्यास (abhyas) = study/practice

### Scripture
**Marathi**: पवित्र शास्त्र (pavitra shastra)
**Transliteration**: pavitra shastra
**Etymology**: पवित्र (pavitra) = holy/sacred + शास्त्र (shastra) = scripture/treatise (both Sanskrit)
**Usage**: Formal term for sacred scripture

**Examples**:
- "Scripture" → "पवित्र शास्त्र"
- "True to scripture" → "पवित्र शास्त्रातील वचनानुसार जी सत्य आहे"

### God's Story
**Marathi**: देवाची गोष्ट (devachi gosht) / देवाची कहाणी (devachi kahani)
**Transliteration**: devachi gosht / devachi kahani
**Etymology**: Native Marathi words for "story/narrative"
**Usage**: Used interchangeably for sharing the gospel narrative

**Examples**:
- "Sharing God's Story" → "देवाची गोष्ट सांगणे"
- "God's story" → "देवाची कहाणी"
- "There's no one best way to tell God's story (also called the gospel)" → "परमेश्वराची कहाणी (ज्याला सुवार्ता देखील म्हणतात) सांगण्याचा एकच उत्तम मार्ग नाही"
- "Sharing God's story" → "देवाची कहाणी सांगणे"

**Contextual Notes**:
- गोष्ट (gosht) = story (more colloquial)
- कहाणी (kahani) = story/narrative (Urdu loanword, slightly more formal)
- Both used interchangeably in translation

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism
**Marathi**: बाप्तिस्मा (baptisma)
**Transliteration**: baptisma
**Etymology**: Greek loanword via Portuguese/English
**Usage**: Standard Christian terminology, transliterated

**Examples**:
- "Baptism and How To Do It" → "बाप्तिस्मा आणि तो कसा द्यावा"
- "Baptism" → "बाप्तिस्मा"

**Contextual Notes**:
- The phrase "तो कसा द्यावा" (how to give it) indicates baptism as an act/sacrament administered
- Standard term across Marathi Christian traditions

### The Lord's Supper
**Marathi**: प्रभू भोजन (prabhu bhojan)
**Transliteration**: prabhu bhojan
**Etymology**: प्रभू (prabhu) = Lord (Sanskrit) + भोजन (bhojan) = meal/food (Sanskrit)
**Usage**: Formal term for communion/eucharist

**Examples**:
- "The Lord's Supper" → "प्रभू भोजन"
- "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" → "प्रभु भोजन आणि त्याचे नेतृत्व कसे करावे"
- "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "प्रभू भोजनाचा सराव करा"

## Translation Patterns and Strategy

### Overall Translation Philosophy

The Marathi Zúme translation demonstrates a **hybrid approach** that balances:

1. **Sanskrit Religious Register**: Heavy use of Sanskrit loanwords for spiritual and religious concepts (शिष्य, आध्यात्मिक, पवित्र, प्रार्थना, विश्वास)

2. **English Technical Terms**: Direct borrowing or transliteration for:
   - Church/organizational terms (चर्च, कोचिंग, विजन)
   - Names (येशू, बायबल)
   - Modern methodology terms (3/3, SOAPS, HOST)

3. **Native Marathi Core**: Everyday verbs and concepts (ऐकणे, सांगणे, वाढ, गट)

4. **Explanatory Parentheticals**: English terms often included in parentheses for clarity with technical terms

### Key Translation Strategies

#### 1. Compound Construction Pattern
Many spiritual concepts use **Sanskrit loanword + Marathi grammar**:
- शिष्य बनवणे (shishya banvane) = disciple + making
- देवाचे ऐकणे (devache aikane) = God's + hearing
- प्रार्थनेतील वाटचाल (prarthanetil vatchal) = in-prayer walking

#### 2. Dual Terminology (Native + Loanword)
Technical terms often presented as:
- साधारण चर्च (sadharan church) = simple + church
- पिढीजन्य नकाशा (जनरेशनल मॅपिंग) = generational mapping + (English in parentheses)
- दृष्टी (विजन) = vision + (English)
- कारभारीपण (रिलेशनल स्टुअर्डशिप) = stewardship + (English explanation)

#### 3. English Loanwords Retained
Certain terms borrowed directly with Devanagari spelling:
- चर्च (church)
- बायबल (bible)
- कोचिंग (coaching)
- विजन (vision)
- मिटिंग (meeting)

#### 4. Grammatical Features

**Case Endings**:
- Genitive: देवाचे, येशूचे, चर्चची (possessive)
- Locative: प्रार्थनेत (in prayer), पिढीमध्ये (in generation)
- Oblique: देवाकडून (from God), आत्म्यास (to the Spirit)

**Verb Forms**:
- Infinitive: करणे (to do), सांगणे (to say), बनवणे (to make)
- Past participle: ऐकलेले, केलेले (heard, done)
- Imperative: करा, सांगा (do, tell)

**Compound Verbs**:
- सामायिक करा (share + do)
- प्रशिक्षित करा (train + do)

#### 5. Cultural Adaptation

**"Gospel"** → सुवार्ता (suvarta):
- Not translated as "good news" literally
- Uses established Christian terminology
- सु (good) + वार्ता (news/tidings)
- Parallel to Hindi शुभसंदेश (shubh sandesh)

**"Church"** → चर्च (church):
- NOT translated to मंडळी (mandali = assembly/congregation)
- Direct borrowing reflects Portuguese colonial influence
- Shows preference for internationally recognized term

**"Disciple"** → शिष्य (shishya):
- Leverages traditional guru-shishya parampara (teacher-student tradition)
- Carries deep cultural resonance in Indian spirituality
- More natural than using "student" (विद्यार्थी)

#### 6. Multiplication Terminology

**Unique Feature**: "Multiply/multiplication" translated as:
- गुणात्मक वाढ (gunatmak vadh) = "multiplicative/qualitative growth"
- NOT using गुणाकार (gunakar) which is mathematical multiplication
- Emphasizes quality and organic growth pattern
- Sophisticated theological term choice

### Linguistic Observations

1. **Script Consistency**: Pure Devanagari throughout, even for English loanwords

2. **Honorifics**: Respectful forms used:
   - तुम्ही (tumhi) = formal "you" (not तू/tu)
   - प्रभू (prabhu) = respectful "Lord"

3. **Verb Preferences**:
   - सांगणे (sangane) preferred over कथा सांगणे for "share/tell"
   - पालन करणे (palan karne) for "obey" rather than मानणे (manane)

4. **Pluralization**:
   - English pattern for loanwords: चर्चेस (churches)
   - Marathi pattern for native words: शिष्यांसाठी (disciples)

5. **Descriptive Translation**:
   - "Vision casting" → दर्शन सांगणे (telling the vision)
   - "Prayer walking" → प्रार्थनेतील वाटचाल (walking in prayer)

### Target Audience Considerations

The translation appears designed for:
- **Urban, educated Marathi speakers** (English loanwords suggest familiarity)
- **Christian community** (use of established Christian terminology)
- **Movement practitioners** (technical terms like 3/3, HOST, MAWL retained)

The hybrid approach allows:
- **Accessibility** through familiar Sanskrit religious vocabulary
- **Precision** through English technical terms
- **Cultural resonance** through traditional Indian spiritual concepts

## Usage Recommendations for Translators

### 1. Maintain Established Terminology
**Consistency is critical**. Use the established translations for core terms:
- देव (God), येशू (Jesus), पवित्र आत्मा (Holy Spirit)
- शिष्य (disciple), चर्च (church), सुवार्ता (gospel)
- प्रार्थना (prayer), बाप्तिस्मा (baptism)

### 2. Follow Compound Construction Patterns
When creating new terms, follow the pattern:
- **Sanskrit base + Marathi verb**: शिष्य बनवणे (disciple-making)
- **Noun + possessive + gerund**: देवाचे ऐकणे (God's hearing)

### 3. Retain English for Technical Terms
Keep English loanwords for:
- Methodology acronyms (SOAPS, HOST, MAWL)
- Technical meeting structures (3/3 Group)
- Modern organizational terms (coaching, vision)

### 4. Use Parenthetical Clarification
For new technical terms:
- Provide Marathi translation + (English in parentheses)
- Example: दृष्टी (विजन), पिढीजन्य नकाशा (जनरेशनल मॅपिंग)

### 5. Prefer Sanskrit Register for Spiritual Concepts
Draw from Sanskrit vocabulary for:
- Spiritual qualities: आध्यात्मिक, पवित्र, विश्वास
- Religious actions: प्रार्थना, पालन, साक्ष
- Abstract concepts: शिष्यत्व, नेतृत्व, कारभारीपण

### 6. Use Respectful Forms
Maintain formal register:
- तुम्ही (you-formal) not तू (you-informal)
- प्रभू (Lord) for divine titles
- Honorific case endings appropriate to context

### 7. Grammatical Consistency
Follow established case patterns:
- **Genitive**: देवाचे, येशूचे (possessive)
- **Locative**: प्रार्थनेत (in prayer)
- **Oblique**: देवाकडून (from God)

### 8. Translation Test Questions
Before finalizing new translations, ask:
1. Does it use established Christian Marathi terminology?
2. Does it resonate with traditional Indian spiritual concepts?
3. Is it clear to urban, educated Marathi speakers?
4. Does it maintain theological precision?
5. Is it consistent with existing Zúme terminology?

### 9. Cultural Sensitivity
Be aware of:
- शिष्य carries guru-shishya tradition weight (positive)
- चर्च vs. मंडळी choice signals theological positioning
- Sanskrit loanwords convey spiritual authority
- English terms signal modern, movement-oriented approach

### 10. Avoid These Common Mistakes
- Don't use mathematical गुणाकार for spiritual "multiplication"
- Don't translate "church" to मंडळी (inconsistent with established term)
- Don't use informal pronouns (तू) for spiritual contexts
- Don't create new terms for concepts that have established Marathi Christian vocabulary
- Don't omit parenthetical English for technical terms
