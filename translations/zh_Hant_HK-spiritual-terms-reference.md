# Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference Guide

## Overview

This guide documents how key spiritual and ministry terms are translated from English to Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) in the Zume training materials. It serves as a reference for translators and coaches to maintain consistency and understand the theological and cultural choices made in the Hong Kong Chinese translation.

**Language:** Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) (繁體中文（香港）)
**Language Code:** zh_Hant_HK
**Script:** Traditional Chinese characters (繁體字)
**Romanization System:** Jyutping (Cantonese romanization) with occasional Pinyin references
**Source File:** zume-zh_Hant_HK.po

---

## Translation Philosophy

The Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) translation demonstrates a consistent approach that:

1. **Uses Traditional Chinese characters** - Hong Kong uses traditional characters (繁體字), not the simplified characters (简体字) used in Mainland China
2. **Reflects Hong Kong Cantonese Christian terminology** - While written in standard Chinese, terminology reflects Hong Kong Protestant vocabulary with British colonial Christian influence
3. **Balances classical and modern vocabulary** - Core theological terms use classical Chinese-based vocabulary while practical ministry terms use contemporary expressions
4. **Uses quotation marks for emphasis** - Frequently uses 「」(Chinese quotation marks) to emphasize key concepts and tools
5. **Maintains respectful language for deity** - Consistent use of respectful forms when referring to God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit
6. **Incorporates both 神 and 上帝** - Uses both 神 (Shen/San) and 上帝 (Shangdi/Soeng Dai) for God, showing Hong Kong's ecumenical Protestant heritage

## Core Deity Terms

### God

**Traditional Chinese:** 神 (Shen) / 上帝 (Shangdi)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** san4 / soeng6 dai3

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** shén / shàngdì

**Literal Meaning:**
- 神 = deity, spirit, divine being
- 上帝 = supreme emperor, heavenly lord

**Register:** Formal/sacred

**Cultural Notes:**
- Hong Kong uses BOTH 神 and 上帝 interchangeably, reflecting diverse Protestant denominational backgrounds
- 神 is shorter and more common in casual Christian speech
- 上帝 is more formal and emphasizes God's sovereignty
- Mainland China (zh_CN) tends to prefer 上帝 in official Protestant contexts
- Taiwan (zh_TW) also uses both but may prefer 神 in evangelical contexts
- Hong Kong's British colonial Christian heritage influences this dual usage

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| God Uses Ordinary People | 神使用「平凡的人」| Title - 神 as active agent |
| Hearing and Obeying God | 聆聽並順服神 | Spiritual discipline |
| Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God | 作為門徒就是要聆聽神的話並遵行神的吩咐 | Discipleship definition |
| What do you learn about God? | 你對神又有什麼新的認識？ | Reflection question |
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together | 祈求上帝的聖靈帶領你們共度時光 | Prayer - uses 上帝 |
| God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact | 神使用「平凡的人」藉着微不足道的小事造成巨大的影響 | Key vision statement |
| Why is it essential to learn to hear and recognize God's voice? | 為什麼我們必須學習聆聽﹑辨識神的聲音？ | Training question |
| Have you ever stopped to think about where God's Kingdom... isn't? | 你可曾靜下來思想那些神的國度尚未臨到的地方嗎？ | Vision casting - 神的國度 |
| Pray and thank God that He is faithful | 禱告並感謝神，讓祂信實地在我們身上完成祂美好的工作 | Prayer instruction |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 神的 (san4 dik1 / shén de) = God's, of God (possessive)
- 神使用 (san4 si2 jung6 / shén shǐyòng) = God uses (active verb)
- 神的話 (san4 dik1 waa6 / shén de huà) = God's Word
- 神的聲音 (san4 dik1 sing1 yam1 / shén de shēngyīn) = God's voice
- 上帝的聖靈 (soeng6 dai3 dik1 sing3 ling4 / shàngdì de shènglíng) = God's Holy Spirit

**Hong Kong vs Mainland China:**
- HK uses both 神 and 上帝 flexibly
- Mainland prefers 上帝 in formal contexts, 神 in evangelical settings
- Taiwan similar to HK but may lean toward 神 in charismatic/evangelical churches

### Jesus

**Traditional Chinese:** 耶穌 (Yesu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** je4 sou1

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** yēsū

**Literal Meaning:** Phonetic transliteration of "Jesus" (from Greek Ἰησοῦς)

**Register:** Sacred name (transliteration)

**Cultural Notes:**
- Same character form across all Chinese-speaking regions (耶穌)
- Simplified Chinese uses 耶稣
- Sometimes appears as 耶穌基督 (Yesu Jidu / Jesus Christ)
- In Hong Kong, often simply 耶穌 without honorific suffix in written text
- Spoken Cantonese may add 主 (zyu2 / Lord) before or after: 主耶穌

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| How to Follow Jesus | 如何跟隨耶穌 | Training title |
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」是什麼？ | Question with quotation marks |
| What are the activities of a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」會做什麼？ | Question format |
| Learn how to obey Jesus' commands | 學習如何透過去和留來遵守耶穌的命令 | Obedience teaching |
| How do I obey Jesus and help others become followers with me? | 我要如何順服耶穌並幫助他人和我一起成為耶穌的跟隨者？ | Personal application |
| Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you | 教導別人和你一起順服耶穌並跟隨他 | Making disciples |
| Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus | 與「您認識的人」談論耶穌 | Evangelism context |
| Speaking to people YOU DON'T KNOW about Jesus | 與「您不認識的人」談論耶穌 | Evangelism outreach |
| What if many friends, family and others start following Jesus with me? | 如果許多朋友、家人和其他人開始和我一起跟隨耶穌，我應該怎麼做？ | Church planting question |
| Becoming more like Jesus... Together | 讓我們一齊⋯⋯越來越像耶穌 | Sanctification vision |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 耶穌的 (je4 sou1 dik1 / yēsū de) = Jesus', of Jesus (possessive)
- 跟隨耶穌 (gan1 ceoi4 je4 sou1 / gēnsuí yēsū) = to follow Jesus
- 順服耶穌 (seon6 fuk6 je4 sou1 / shùnfú yēsū) = to obey Jesus
- 耶穌的命令 (je4 sou1 dik1 ming6 ling6 / yēsū de mìnglìng) = Jesus' commands
- 像耶穌 (zoeng6 je4 sou1 / xiàng yēsū) = like Jesus, Christ-like

**Cantonese Cultural Note:**
- In spoken Cantonese, Christians often say 主耶穌 (zyu2 je4 sou1 / Lord Jesus)
- Written materials often use just 耶穌 to match standard Chinese writing conventions
- This translation follows standard written Chinese rather than Cantonese colloquial speech

### Holy Spirit

**Traditional Chinese:** 聖靈 (Shengling)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** sing3 ling4

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** shènglíng

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy Spirit" (聖 = holy/sacred, 靈 = spirit/soul)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Cultural Notes:**
- Universal term across all Chinese Christian traditions
- Same in traditional and simplified characters
- Sometimes appears as 上帝的聖靈 (God's Holy Spirit) or 神的聖靈
- In theological contexts, may see 保惠師 (Baohuishi / Comforter) as synonym
- Very stable term with over 150 years of usage in Chinese Christianity

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together | 祈求上帝的聖靈帶領你們共度時光 | Prayer instruction |
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time, together | 祈求上帝的聖靈帶領你們一起度過這段時光 | Prayer (variant) |
| Invite His Holy Spirit to lead your time | 邀請祂的聖靈帶領您一起度過這段時光 | Prayer invitation |
| Jesus said: "But when the Holy Spirit comes upon you..." | 耶穌說︰「但聖靈降臨在你們身上﹐你們就必得着能力⋯」 | Scripture quote |
| Pray for the Holy Spirit to continue guiding and helping you | 求聖靈繼續引導幫助你們 | Ongoing prayer |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 聖靈 is used without any particle when subject: 聖靈降臨 (Holy Spirit descends)
- 的聖靈 when possessive: 上帝的聖靈 (God's Holy Spirit)
- 求聖靈 (kau4 sing3 ling4 / qiú shènglíng) = ask/pray to the Holy Spirit
- 聖靈帶領 (sing3 ling4 daai3 ling5 / shènglíng dàilǐng) = Holy Spirit leads

### Lord

**Traditional Chinese:** 主 (Zhu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** zyu2

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** zhǔ

**Literal Meaning:** Lord, master, owner

**Register:** Respectful/sacred

**Cultural Notes:**
- Classical Chinese term for lord/master
- Used for both God the Father and Jesus Christ
- Often appears in compounds: 主的晚餐 (Lord's Supper)
- May appear as 主耶穌 (Lord Jesus) in devotional contexts
- Very respectful term with imperial/sovereign connotations

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It | 聖餐：如何領「主的晚餐」| Training title |
| Celebrate the Lord's Supper | 慶祝主的聖餐 | Practice instruction |
| Ask the Lord who He wants you to train | 問上帝祂希望你訓練誰 | Uses 上帝 instead of 主 |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 主的 (zyu2 dik1 / zhǔ de) = Lord's, of the Lord
- 主耶穌 (zyu2 je4 sou1 / zhǔ yēsū) = Lord Jesus

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple

**Traditional Chinese:** 門徒 (Mentu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** mun4 tou4

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** méntú

**Literal Meaning:** "Disciple, follower, student" (門 = gate/school/sect, 徒 = follower/adherent)

**Register:** Formal/classical

**Cultural Notes:**
- Classical Chinese term with both religious and secular usage
- Same term used for Buddhist, Daoist, and Confucian disciples historically
- In Christian context, specifically refers to followers of Jesus
- Very stable across all Chinese Christian traditions
- Same characters in traditional and simplified Chinese

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 「門徒」與「教會」的簡單定義 | Basic teaching title |
| Disciples and the Church | 「門徒」與「教會」| Section heading |
| Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God | 作為門徒就是要聆聽神的話並遵行神的吩咐 | Definition |
| What's a Disciple? | 何謂「門徒」？ | Question format |
| Being A Disciple | 成為門徒 | Training section |
| Disciple | 門徒 | Single term |
| Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples | 「培訓循環」︰培育門徒邁向成熟 | Tool name |

**Compound Forms:**
- 門徒訓練 (mun4 tou4 fan3 lin6 / méntú xùnliàn) = disciple training
- 成為門徒 (sing4 wai4 mun4 tou4 / chéngwéi méntú) = becoming a disciple
- 建立門徒 (gin3 laap6 mun4 tou4 / jiànlì méntú) = making disciples
- 培育門徒 (pui4 juk6 mun4 tou4 / péiyù méntú) = nurturing disciples
- 門徒的 (mun4 tou4 dik1 / méntú de) = of disciples

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 作為門徒 (zok3 wai4 mun4 tou4 / zuòwéi méntú) = as a disciple
- 耶穌的門徒 = Jesus' disciple(s)

### Making Disciples / Disciple-Making

**Traditional Chinese:** 建立門徒 (Jianli Mentu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** gin3 laap6 mun4 tou4

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** jiànlì méntú

**Literal Meaning:** "Build/establish disciples" (建立 = to establish/build, 門徒 = disciples)

**Register:** Active/practical

**Cultural Notes:**
- 建立 implies intentional construction and establishment
- More formal than 造就 (to nurture/edify)
- Reflects the Great Commission mandate
- Alternative translations include 使萬民作門徒 (make all nations into disciples)

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Making Disciples | 建立門徒 | Section title |
| Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making | 學習小鴨與建立門徒之間有何關係 | Teaching introduction |
| Simple Definition of being a disciple, making a disciple | 何謂「成為門徒」、「建立門徒」| Foundational teaching |

**Related Terms:**
- 成為門徒 (sing4 wai4 mun4 tou4 / chéngwéi méntú) = becoming a disciple
- 建立 (gin3 laap6 / jiànlì) = to establish, to build

### Discipleship

**Traditional Chinese:** 門徒訓練 (Mentu Xunlian)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** mun4 tou4 fan3 lin6

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** méntú xùnliàn

**Literal Meaning:** "Disciple training" (門徒 = disciples, 訓練 = training)

**Register:** Formal/educational

**Cultural Notes:**
- More concrete than abstract concept of "discipleship"
- Emphasizes practical training aspect
- Reflects Hong Kong's emphasis on practical application
- Sometimes appears as 門訓 (shortened form)

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Discipleship Concepts | 門徒訓練概念 | Category title |

**Compound Forms:**
- 門訓 (mun4 fan3 / ménxùn) = discipleship (shortened form)
- 小鴨門訓 = duckling discipleship (specific method)

## Church Terms

### Church

**Traditional Chinese:** 教會 (Jiaohui)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** gaau3 wui6

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** jiàohuì

**Literal Meaning:** "Teaching gathering" or "religious assembly" (教 = teaching/religion, 會 = gathering/meeting/assembly)

**Register:** Formal/standard

**Cultural Notes:**
- Standard term for church across all Chinese Christian contexts
- Same in traditional and simplified Chinese
- Can refer to both the building and the congregation
- Used for local churches, denominational churches, and the universal Church
- In Hong Kong, distinguishes between 教會 (church) and 禮拜堂 (chapel/church building)

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 「門徒」與「教會」的簡單定義 | Basic teaching |
| Disciples and the Church | 「門徒」與「教會」| Section title |
| What's a Church? | 何謂「教會」？ | Question format |
| My Churches | 我的教會 | Personal churches planted |
| Church Name | 教會名稱 | Administrative field |
| Church Location | 教會地點 | Geographic information |
| Parent Church | 母會 | Church planting terminology |
| Church | 教會 | Simple reference |
| Churches Needed | 需要教會 | Vision metric |
| Churches Reported | 已報教會 | Progress tracking |
| Map of Zúme Churches | 「天國酵母」教會的地圖 | Visualization tool |
| Always Part of Two Churches | 「兩個教會並進」| Strategy principle |

**Compound Forms:**
- 簡約教會 (gaan2 joek3 gaau3 wui6 / jiǎnyuē jiàohuì) = simple church
- 母會 (mou5 wui6 / mǔhuì) = mother/parent church
- 我的教會 (ngo5 dik1 gaau3 wui6 / wǒ de jiàohuì) = my church(es)
- 教會的 (gaau3 wui6 dik1 / jiàohuì de) = of the church

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 教會 can be singular or plural depending on context
- 兩個教會 (loeng5 go3 gaau3 wui6 / liǎng gè jiàohuì) = two churches

### Simple Church

**Traditional Chinese:** 簡約教會 (Jianyue Jiaohui)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** gaan2 joek3 gaau3 wui6

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** jiǎnyuē jiàohuì

**Literal Meaning:** "Simple/streamlined church" (簡約 = simple/concise, 教會 = church)

**Register:** Descriptive/technical

**Cultural Notes:**
- 簡約 implies intentional simplicity and streamlining
- Distinguished from 簡單 (simple, easy) which has a more basic meaning
- Reflects Hong Kong house church and missional church movements
- Emphasizes essence over form

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Leading a Simple Church | 帶領簡約教會 | Training section |
| Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church? | 「三分三小組」聚會能不能當成簡約教會來看待？| Discussion question |
| 2 Simple Churches | 兩個簡約教會 | Vision metric |

**Related Terms:**
- 帶領 (daai3 ling5 / dàilǐng) = to lead
- 三分三小組 (saam1 fan1 saam1 siu2 zou2 / sānfēnsān xiǎozǔ) = 3/3 Group

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel

**Traditional Chinese:** 福音 (Fuyin)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** fuk1 yam1

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** fúyīn

**Literal Meaning:** "Good news" or "blessed message" (福 = blessing/good fortune, 音 = sound/news/message)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Cultural Notes:**
- Ancient Chinese Buddhist translation term adopted by Christians
- Originally used for Buddhist "good news" in Chinese translations from Sanskrit
- Universally accepted across all Chinese Christian traditions for 150+ years
- Same in traditional and simplified Chinese
- Very stable and well-established term

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| The Gospel and How to Share It | 「神的故事」︰如何分享福音 | Training title (note: "God's Story" as title) |
| The Gospel | 「神的故事」| Section reference (uses "God's Story" branding) |
| Share the Gospel | 「神的故事」| Action item |
| Take turns telling the Gospel to each other | 輪流互相傳講福音 | Practice activity |
| Sharing the Gospel | 分享福音 | Action noun |
| Practice Sharing the Gospel | 練習分享福音 | Training exercise |
| 3-Circles Gospel Presentation | 「三圈佈道法」| Method name |
| Pray and thank God for inviting us to share the gospel | 禱告並感謝上帝邀請我們與他人分享福音 | Prayer instruction |

**Compound Forms:**
- 分享福音 (fan1 hoeng2 fuk1 yam1 / fēnxiǎng fúyīn) = share the gospel
- 傳講福音 (cyun4 gong2 fuk1 yam1 / chuánjiǎng fúyīn) = proclaim the gospel
- 三圈佈道法 (saam1 hyun1 bou3 dou6 faat3 / sānquān bùdàofǎ) = 3-Circles Gospel method
- 福音的好消息 (fuk1 yam1 dik1 hou2 siu1 sik1 / fúyīn de hǎo xiāoxī) = good news of the gospel
- 神的故事 (san4 dik1 gu3 si6 / shén de gùshì) = God's Story (branding for gospel)

**Cultural Notes:**
- This translation often uses 神的故事 ("God's Story") as the branded term for the gospel message
- This is a contextualization choice to make the concept more narrative and accessible
- The term 福音 is still used when referring to the gospel generally

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 福音 can function as noun: 這是福音 (This is the gospel)
- With action verbs: 分享福音 (share gospel), 傳講福音 (preach gospel)

### Bible / Scripture

**Traditional Chinese:** 聖經 (Shengjing)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** sing3 ging1

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** shèngjīng

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy Scriptures/Classic" (聖 = holy/sacred, 經 = classic text/scripture)

**Register:** Formal/sacred

**Cultural Notes:**
- Universal term for the Bible across all Chinese Christian traditions
- 經 (jing) is the same character used for Confucian classics (經典)
- This gives the Bible the status of "sacred classic" in Chinese culture
- Same in traditional and simplified Chinese
- Extremely stable term with 150+ years of usage

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study | 「肥皂讀經法」| Study method (creative naming) |
| S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading | 「肥皂讀經法」| Reading method |
| Scripture | 查考聖經 | Study practice ("examining scripture") |
| Bible Reading | 讀聖經 | Regular practice |
| Personal Bible Study | 個人的聖經學習 | Individual study |
| The Bible tells us that every follower of Jesus... | 聖經告訴我們，每一位耶穌的跟隨者⋯ | Teaching authority |

**Compound Forms:**
- 讀聖經 (duk6 sing3 ging1 / dú shèngjīng) = read the Bible
- 查考聖經 (caa4 haau2 sing3 ging1 / cháokǎo shèngjīng) = examine/study Scripture
- 聖經學習 (sing3 ging1 hok6 zaap6 / shèngjīng xuéxí) = Bible study/learning
- 聖經告訴我們 (sing3 ging1 gou3 sou3 ngo5 mun4 / shèngjīng gàosù wǒmen) = the Bible tells us
- 肥皂讀經法 (fei4 zou6 duk6 ging1 faat3 / féizào dújīng fǎ) = SOAPS Bible reading (creative translation: "soap Bible reading method")

**Cultural Notes:**
- 肥皂 literally means "soap" - this is a creative Chinese rendering of the SOAPS acronym
- 查考 (examine/investigate) is more formal than 讀 (read) and implies deeper study

### Word (of God)

**Traditional Chinese:** 神的話 (Shen de Hua) / 話語 (Huayu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** san4 dik1 waa6 / waa6 jyu5

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** shén de huà / huàyǔ

**Literal Meaning:** "God's words/speech" (神 = God, 的 = possessive, 話 = words/speech)

**Register:** Respectful/theological

**Cultural Notes:**
- 神的話 is more common and direct
- 話語 is more formal and literary
- Can refer to both Scripture and direct revelation
- Distinct from 聖經 (Bible) as written text

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Hearing and Obeying God | 聆聽並順服神 | Can include hearing God's Word |
| Being a disciple means we hear from God | 作為門徒就是要聆聽神的話並遵行神的吩咐 | Uses 神的話 explicitly |
| A tool for daily Bible study that helps you obey God's Word | 一種每日研讀聖經的方法，讓您更多明白、順服、分享神的話 | Practical application |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 神的話 (san4 dik1 waa6 / shén de huà) = God's words/Word
- 聆聽神的話 (ling4 ting1 san4 dik1 waa6 / língtīng shén de huà) = listen to God's Word
- 順服神的話 (seon6 fuk6 san4 dik1 waa6 / shùnfú shén de huà) = obey God's Word

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism

**Traditional Chinese:** 洗禮 (Xili) / 給人施洗 (Gei Ren Shixi)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** sai2 lai5 / kap1 jan4 si1 sai2

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** xǐlǐ / gěi rén shīxǐ

**Literal Meaning:**
- 洗禮 = "washing ceremony" (洗 = to wash, 禮 = ceremony/rite)
- 給人施洗 = "give people baptism" (給 = to give, 人 = people, 施洗 = to baptize)

**Register:** Formal/liturgical

**Cultural Notes:**
- 洗禮 is the universal Chinese Christian term for baptism
- This translation creatively uses 給人施洗 (baptizing people) to emphasize the action
- Same term used across Catholic and Protestant traditions
- 施洗 literally means "to administer washing"
- John the Baptist is 施洗約翰 (Shixi Yuehan / John the Baptizer)

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Baptism and How To Do It | 洗禮：如何「給人施洗」| Training title |

- 屬靈世代 (suk6 ling4 sai3 doi6 / shǔlíng shìdài) = spiritual generations
- 屬靈的洞察力 (suk6 ling4 dik1 dung6 caat3 lik6 / shǔlíng de dòngchálì) = spiritual insight

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 屬靈 functions as adjective before nouns
- 屬靈的 (suk6 ling4 dik1 / shǔlíng de) when 的 is needed grammatically

### Follow / Follower

**Traditional Chinese:** 跟隨 (Gensui) / 跟隨者 (Gensuizhe) / 跟隨他 (Gensui Ta)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** gan1 ceoi4 / gan1 ceoi4 ze2 / gan1 ceoi4 taa1

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** gēnsuí / gēnsuízhě / gēnsuí tā

**Literal Meaning:** "Follow after" (跟 = follow, 隨 = accompany/go along with)

**Register:** Standard/active

**Cultural Notes:**
- Common verb in both Christian and secular contexts
- Emphasizes active following and companionship
- 者 suffix creates noun (one who follows)
- Same in traditional and simplified Chinese

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese (HK) | Context |
|---------|----------|---------|
| How to Follow Jesus | 如何跟隨耶穌 | Training title |
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」是什麼？ | Definition question |
| What are the activities of a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」會做什麼？ | Behavioral question |
| Follower of Jesus | 耶穌的跟隨者 | Identity term |
| The Bible tells us that every follower of Jesus... | 聖經告訴我們，每一位耶穌的跟隨者⋯ | Teaching introduction |

**Compound Forms:**
- 跟隨耶穌 (gan1 ceoi4 je4 sou1 / gēnsuí yēsū) = follow Jesus
- 耶穌的跟隨者 (je4 sou1 dik1 gan1 ceoi4 ze2 / yēsū de gēnsuízhě) = follower(s) of Jesus
- 跟隨他 (gan1 ceoi4 taa1 / gēnsuí tā) = follow him

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 跟隨 is verb
- 跟隨者 is noun (one who follows)
- 的跟隨者 requires possessive marker 的

### Blessing / Bless

**Traditional Chinese:** 祝福 (Zhufu)

**Jyutping (Cantonese):** zuk1 fuk1

**Pinyin (Mandarin):** zhùfú

**Literal Meaning:** "Wish blessing" (祝 = wish/pray for, 福 = blessing/fortune)

**Register:** Formal/auspicious

**Cultural Notes:**
- Common in both Christian and Chinese cultural contexts

- Can be verb (to bless) or noun (blessing)

## Comparative Notes: Hong Kong vs Mainland vs Taiwan

### God: 神 vs 上帝

**Hong Kong (zh_Hant_HK):** Uses BOTH 神 and 上帝 flexibly

## Usage Guidelines for Translators

### When Translating to Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong):

1. **Use traditional characters** (繁體字) not simplified
2. **Flexibility with 神/上帝** - both are acceptable and reflect Hong Kong usage
3. **Standard written Chinese** - avoid colloquial Cantonese in written materials
4. **Quotation marks** - use 「」for emphasis on tool names and concepts
5. **Creative tool names** - maintain memorable Chinese names like 天國酵母
6. **Biblical terminology** - use established Protestant vocabulary
7. **Respectful language** - maintain proper register for deity references

### Consistency Checks:

- Disciple: Always 門徒
- Church: Always 教會
- Gospel: 福音 for the concept, but can use 神的故事 for Zúme branding
- Bible: Always 聖經
- Prayer: Always 禱告 (or 祈禱 in some contexts)
- Jesus: Always 耶穌
- Holy Spirit: Always 聖靈
- Kingdom: 天國 / 神的國度 / 神國 (all acceptable)
- Spiritual: Always 屬靈
