# Persian (Farsi) Translation Reference Guide (fa_IR)

**Source File:** `po_files/zume-fa_IR.po`
**Purpose:** Reference guide for maintaining consistent spiritual terminology in Persian translations
**Date Analyzed:** 2026-01-16

---

## Overview

This document catalogs key spiritual terms and their Persian (Farsi) translations as used in the Zume training materials. The translation strategy employs **sophisticated Muslim-background contextualization** for Iranian Persian speakers, using Islamic-familiar vocabulary (like "Isa" for Jesus, "Injil" for Gospel) while maintaining Christian distinctiveness where necessary (baptism, Trinity, church). The approach emphasizes practical disciple-making movement methodology suited for house church contexts in restrictive environments.

---

## Core Deity Terms

### Jesus
- **Persian:** عیسی
- **Transliteration:** Isa
- **CRITICAL NOTE:** Uses **عیسی (Isa)** - the Quranic/Islamic form of Jesus - NOT **یسوع (Yasu)** which is the Christian-specific form. This is a strategic contextualization choice for Muslim-background audiences, as "Isa" is familiar and non-threatening from the Quran.
- **Examples:**
  - "How to Follow Jesus" → "چگونه عیسی را دنبال کنیم" (Chegune Isa ra donbal konim)
  - "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "پیروان عیسی چیست؟" (Peyravane Isa chist?)
  - "Becoming more like Jesus... Together" → "بیشتر به شباهت عیسی درآیید... با یکدیگر" (Bishtar be shabahate Isa darayid... ba yekdigar)
  - "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "یاد بگیرید که از فرامین عیسی اطاعت کنید" (Yad begirid ke az faramane Isa eta'at konid)
  - "Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus" → "با مردم صحبت می کنید ، در مورد عیسی می دانید" (Ba mardom sohbat mikonid, dar morede Isa midanid)

### Christ / Messiah
- **Persian:** مسیح (Masih) - MOSTLY AVOIDED
- **Transliteration:** Masih
- **CRITICAL NOTE:** The term "Christ" or "Messiah" (مسیح - Masih) does **not appear prominently** in the translations. This is significant - the material uses "Isa" (Jesus) almost exclusively without the title "Masih" (Messiah/Christ).
- **Limited Usage:**
  - "Body of Christ" → "بدن مسیح" (Badane Masih) - appears once in fixed theological phrase
  - "After you came to faith in Christ" → "بعد از اینکه به مسیح ایمان آوردید" (Ba'd az inke be Masih imān āvardid)
- **Strategic Note:** This appears to be deliberate contextualization. In Muslim contexts, "Isa al-Masih" would be more explicitly Christian, while just "Isa" is neutral and familiar from the Quran.

### God
- **Persian:** خدا
- **Transliteration:** Khoda
- **CRITICAL NOTE:** Uses **خدا (Khoda)** - the Persian native word for God - NOT **الله (Allah)** which is the Arabic loanword used in the Quran and Islamic contexts. This is a significant cultural choice:
  - "Khoda" is more culturally Persian and less explicitly Islamic
  - "Allah" would signal a more Islamic religious context
  - This choice suggests a Christian-distinct yet culturally accessible approach
- **Examples:**
  - "God Uses Ordinary People" → "خدا از مردم عادی استفاده می کند" (Khoda az mardome adi estefade mikonad)
  - "Hearing and Obeying God" → "شنیدن و اطاعت از خدا" (Shenidan va eta'at az Khoda)
  - "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "شاگرد بودن یعنی اینکه از خدا می شنویم و از خدا اطاعت می کنیم" (Shagerd budan ya'ni inke az Khoda mishenavam va az Khoda eta'at mikonim)
  - "What do you learn about God?" → "درباره خدا چه یاد می گیرید؟" (Darbare Khoda che yad migirid?)
  - "God's voice" → "صدای خدا" (sedaye Khoda)

### Lord
- **Persian:** خداوند
- **Transliteration:** Khodavand
- **Notes:** Compound of Khoda (God) + vand (suffix indicating mastery/lordship). More formal and reverential than simple "Khoda."
- **Examples:**
  - "The Lord's Supper" → "شام خداوند" (Shame Khodavand)
  - "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" → "شام خداوند و چگونه آن را رهبری کنیم" (Shame Khodavand va chegune an ra rahbari konim)
  - "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "شام خداوند را برگزار کنید" (Shame Khodavand ra bargozar konid)
- **Pattern:** Appears primarily in the fixed phrase "Lord's Supper." Otherwise, simple "Khoda" is preferred.

### Holy Spirit
- **Persian:** روح‎القدس / روح القدس
- **Transliteration:** Ruh-ul-Qods / Ruh al-Qods
- **Notes:** Arabic loanword compound. Recognizable from Quranic context (though the Quran uses "Ruh al-Qudus" for Gabriel/Spirit). This is Christian-specific Trinitarian terminology in Persian.
- **Examples:**
  - "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together" → "از روح‎القدس بخواهید این زمانی را که با یکدیگر دارید، رهبری کند" (Az Ruh-ul-Qods bekhahid in zamani ra ke ba yekdigar darid, rahbari konad)
  - Trinitarian formula: "Father and Son and Holy Spirit" → "پدر و پسر و روح القدس" (Pedar va Pesar va Ruh-ul-Qods)
  - "از روح‎القدس بخواهید تا زمانی را که با یکدیگر دارید، رهبری کند" (Az Ruh-ul-Qods bekhahid ta zamani ra ke ba yekdigar darid, rahbari konad)

### Spirit / Spiritual
- **Persian:**
  - روح (Ruh) - spirit (noun)
  - روحانی (Ruhani) - spiritual (Arabic-derived adjective)
  - معنوی (Ma'navi) - spiritual (Persian-derived adjective)
- **Transliteration:** Ruh / Ruhani / Ma'navi
- **Notes:** Both روحانی (ruhani - Arabic) and معنوی (ma'navi - Persian) are used interchangeably for "spiritual," showing lexical flexibility.
- **Examples:**
  - "Spiritual Breathing" → "تنفس روحانی" (Tanafose Ruhani) - using Arabic-derived
  - "Spiritual Economy" → "اقتصاد روحانی" (Eqtesade Ruhani)
  - "Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God" → "تنفس معنوی شنیدن و اطاعت خداست" (Tanafose Ma'navi shenidan va eta'ate Khodast) - using Persian-derived
  - "spiritual family" → "خانواده‎ی روحانی" (khanevadeye ruhani)
  - "Spiritual Practices" → "تمرین‎های روحانی" (tamrinhaye ruhani)
  - "Keys to Spiritual Growth" → "کلیدهای رشد روحانی" (klidhaye roshde ruhani)

### Father (God the Father)
- **Persian:** پدر
- **Transliteration:** Pedar
- **Notes:** Simple Persian word for "father" without elaboration or avoidance, appearing in Trinitarian formula
- **Examples:**
  - "Father and Son and Holy Spirit" → "پدر و پسر و روح القدس" (Pedar va Pesar va Ruh-ul-Qods)
  - "Go and make disciples... baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit" → "بروید و شاگردان همه ملل شوید ، آنها را به اسم پدر و پسر و روح القدس تکمیل کنید" (Beravid va shagerdane hame melal shavid, anha ra be esme Pedar va Pesar va Ruh-ul-Qods takmil konid)

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
- **Persian:** شاگرد (singular), شاگردان (plural)
- **Transliteration:** Shagerd (singular), Shagerdan (plural)
- **Notes:** Common Persian word for student/learner/apprentice, not religiously charged. Makes the concept accessible and non-threatening.
- **Examples:**
  - "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "تعریف ساده از شاگرد و کلیسا" (Ta'rife sade az shagerd va kelisa)
  - "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "شاگرد بودن یعنی اینکه از خدا می شنویم و از خدا اطاعت می کنیم" (Shagerd budan ya'ni inke az Khoda mishenavam va az Khoda eta'at mikonim)
  - "What's a Disciple?" → "شاگرد چیست؟" (Shagerd chist?)
  - "Disciples and the Church" → "شاگردان و کلیسا" (Shagerdan va kelisa)
  - "Ask yourself, what does my disciple need" → "از خودتان بپرسید، شاگرد من به چه چیزی نیاز دارد" (Az khodetan beporsid, shagerde man be che chizi niaz darad)

### Disciple-making / Discipleship
- **Persian:** شاگردسازی / شاگرد سازی / ساختن شاگردان
- **Transliteration:** Shagerd-sazi / Sakhtane shagerdan
- **Notes:** "Shagerd-sazi" means disciple-making, disciple-construction. Verb form is "sakhtane shagerdan" (making disciples).
- **Examples:**
  - "Making Disciples" → "شاگردسازی" (Shagerd-sazi)
  - "Duckling Discipleship" → "شاگرد سازی به روش جوجه اردک" (Shagerd-sazi be raveshe juje ordak)
  - "Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making" → "بیاموزید که جوجه اردک ها چه ارتباطی با ساختن شاگردان دارند" (Biamuzid ke juje ordakha che ertebati ba sakhtane shagerdan darand)
  - "Discipleship Concepts" → "مفاهم شاگردسازی" (Mafaheme shagerd-sazi)

### Follower
- **Persian:** پیرو (singular), پیروان (plural)
- **Transliteration:** Peyrow (singular), Peyravān (plural)
- **Notes:** Neutral term for follower, used in both religious and secular contexts
- **Examples:**
  - "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "پیروان عیسی چیست؟" (Peyravāne Isa chist?)
  - "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" → "فعالیت های یک پیروان عیسی چیست؟" (Fa'aliyathaye yek peyravāne Isa chist?)
  - "followers of Jesus" → "پیروان عیسی" (peyravāne Isa)
  - "everyday followers" → "پیروان روزمره" (peyravāne ruzmarre)

### Follow / Following
- **Persian:** پیروی کردن / دنبال کردن
- **Transliteration:** Peyravi kardan (formal/discipleship) / Donbāl kardan (general)
- **Notes:** Two forms - "peyravi kardan" is more formal (to follow/adhere to), "donbāl kardan" is more general (to follow)
- **Examples:**
  - "How to Follow Jesus" → "چگونه عیسی را دنبال کنیم" (Chegune Isa ra donbāl konim)
  - "follow Jesus" → "از عیسی پیروی کنید" (az Isa peyravi konid)
  - "Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you" → "به دیگران بیاموزید که از شما پیروی کنند و از عیسی پیروی کنند" (Be digaran biamuzid ke az shoma peyravi konand va az Isa peyravi konand)

### Believer
- **Persian:** ایماندار (singular), ایمانداران (plural)
- **Transliteration:** Imandār
- **Notes:** Literally "one who has faith" (iman + dar suffix). "Iman" is Islamic concept, making "imandār" recognizable but contextually neutral.
- **Examples:**
  - "Unbeliever" → "غیرایماندار" (gheyr-imandār)
  - "Share the 'Training Cycle' with a believer on your list of 100" → "«چرخه‎ی تعلیم» را با یک ایماندار در فهرست ۱۰۰ نفری خود در میان بگذارید" (Charkheye ta'lim ra ba yek imandār dar fehrest-e 100 nafari khod dar mian begozarid)
  - "at least five believers you know" → "حداقل پنج ایمانداری را که می‎شناسید" (hadaqal panj imandāri ra ke mishenasid)

### Obey / Obedience
- **Persian:** اطاعت کردن (verb), اطاعت (noun)
- **Transliteration:** Eta'at kardan / Eta'at
- **Notes:** Arabic loanword, common in both religious and secular Persian (obedience to authority, parents, etc.). Always uses "اطاعت از" (eta'at az - obedience to/from).
- **Examples:**
  - "Hearing and Obeying God" → "شنیدن و اطاعت از خدا" (Shenidan va eta'at az Khoda)
  - "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "یاد بگیرید که از فرامین عیسی اطاعت کنید" (Yad begirid ke az faramāne Isa eta'at konid)
  - "Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God" → "تنفس معنوی شنیدن و اطاعت خداست" (Tanafose ma'navi shenidan va eta'ate Khodast)
  - "The Power of Obedient Faith" → "قدرت ایمان مطیع" (Qodrate imāne muti')

## Church Terms

### Church
- **Persian:** کلیسا (singular), کلیساها (plural)
- **Transliteration:** Kelisa (singular), Kelisaha (plural)
- **Notes:** Standard Persian word for church (from Greek "ekklesia" via Syriac). Clearly Christian-specific. The material does NOT use alternative terms like "jama'at" (congregation/assembly) which might be more neutral.
- **Examples:**
  - "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "تعریف ساده از شاگرد و کلیسا" (Ta'rife sade az shagerd va kelisa)
  - "What's a Church?" → "کلیسا چیست؟" (Kelisa chist?)
  - "Disciples and the Church" → "شاگردان و کلیسا" (Shagerdan va kelisa)
  - "When you think of a church, what comes to mind?" → "وقتی به کلیسا فکر می کنید، چه چیزی به ذهن تان می آید؟" (Vaqti be kelisa fekr mikonid, che chizi be zehnetān mi-ayad?)
  - "Church Name" → "نام کلیسا" (Nāme kelisa)
  - "My Churches" → "کلیساهای من" (Kelisahaye man)

### Simple Church
- **Persian:** کلیسای کوچک / کلیساهای ساده
- **Transliteration:** Kelisaye kuchak / Kelisahaye sade
- **Notes:** Uses "kuchak" (small/little) rather than "sade" (simple) in most cases, though both appear. Emphasizes size/intimacy over simplicity.
- **Examples:**
  - "2 Simple Churches" → "2 کلیساهای ساده" (2 kelisahaye sade)
  - "simple churches per 5,000 people" → "کلیسای کوچک به ازای هر 5000 نفر" (kelisaye kuchak be ezaye har 5000 nafar)
  - "Always report new simple churches that you are starting" → "همواره کلیساهای کوچک جدیدی را که آغاز می‎کنید، گزارش دهید" (Hamvare kelisahaye kuchake jadidi ra ke aghāz mikonid, gozāresh dehid)
  - "Leading a Simple Church" → "رهبری یک کلیسای کوچک" (Rahbariye yek kelisaye kuchak)
  - "Simple church vision map" → "نقشه دید کلیسای کوچک" (Nagsheye dide kelisaye kuchak)

### Body of Christ
- **Persian:** بدن مسیح
- **Transliteration:** Badane Masih
- **Notes:** Limited usage - appears in fixed theological phrase
- **Examples:**
  - "pray and thank God for the body of Christ" → "دعا کنید و خدا را به‎خاطر بدن مسیح شکر کنید" (Do'a konid va Khoda ra be khātere badane Masih shokr konid)

### Congregation / Assembly
- **Persian:** NOT FOUND
- **Notes:** Material does not use "congregation" or "assembly" as separate concepts from "church." In some Muslim-contextualized materials, "jama'at" (جماعت) is used instead of "kelisa" to avoid Christian terminology. This translation deliberately uses "kelisa" (church), showing a Christian-distinct approach.

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
- **Persian:** انجیل
- **Transliteration:** Injil
- **Notes:** Arabic loanword (from Greek "euangelion"). This is the Quranic/Islamic term for the Gospel, recognized by Muslims as one of the revealed books (though believed corrupted). Makes it familiar and non-threatening while being specifically Christian in this context.
- **Examples:**
  - "The Gospel and How to Share It" → "انجیل و چگونگی به اشتراک گذاشتن آن" (Injil va chegungiye be eshterāk gozāshtane ān)
  - "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" → "ارائه‎ی انجیل با استفاده از سه دایره" (Arāyeye Injil ba estefade az se dāyere)
  - "Share the Gospel" → "انجیل را اعلام کنید" (Injil ra e'lām konid)
  - "Sharing the Gospel" → "اعلام انجیل" (E'lāme Injil)
  - "Practice Sharing the Gospel" → "اعلام انجیل را تمرین کنید" (E'lāme Injil ra tamrin konid)

### Good News
- **Persian:** خبر خوش / خبرهای خوب
- **Transliteration:** Khabare khosh / Khabarhaye khub
- **Notes:** Native Persian vocabulary, not religious terminology. "Khosh" and "khub" (both meaning "good") used interchangeably. Material uses both "Injil" (Gospel) and "khabare khosh" (good news) somewhat interchangeably.
- **Examples:**
  - "God's good news" → "خبرهای خوب خداوند" (khabarhaye khube Khodavand)
  - "good news of the kingdom" → "خبر خوش پادشاهی خدا" (khabare khoshe pādshāhiye Khoda)

### Bible
- **Persian:** کتاب‎مقدس
- **Transliteration:** Ketābe Moqaddas
- **Notes:** Literally "Holy Book." Standard Christian term for Bible in Persian. Muslims would recognize this as referring to Christian scriptures (distinct from Quran).
- **Examples:**
  - "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" → "مطالعۀ کتاب‎مقدس S.O.A.P.S." (Motāle'eye Ketābe Moqaddas S.O.A.P.S.)
  - "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading" → "مطالعه کتاب مقدس به روش ک.م.ک.د.ا" (Motāle'eye Ketābe Moqaddas be raveshe K.M.K.D.A.)
  - "Bible Reading" → "خواندن کتاب مقدس" (Khāndane Ketābe Moqaddas)
  - "Personal Bible Study" → "مطالعه‎ی کتاب مقدس به‎صورت شخصی" (Motāle'eye Ketābe Moqaddas be surate shakhsi)

### Scripture
- **Persian:** کلام خدا
- **Transliteration:** Kalāme Khoda
- **Notes:** Literally "Word of God." "Kalām" is Arabic (word/speech), significant theological term in Islamic theology referring to Quran as God's eternal speech.
- **Examples:**
  - "Scripture" → "کلام خدا" (Kalāme Khoda)

### Word / Word of God
- **Persian:** کلام خدا
- **Transliteration:** Kalāme Khoda
- **Notes:** Same term as "Scripture" above
- **Examples:**
  - Multiple references to studying, obeying, and sharing "کلام خدا" (Kalāme Khoda)

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism / Baptize
- **Persian:** تعمید / غسل تعمید
- **Transliteration:** Ta'mid / Ghosle Ta'mid
- **Notes:**
  - Standard Christian term in Persian, derived from Syriac/Arabic
  - "Ghosle ta'mid" (غسل تعمید) = baptismal washing, where "ghosl" (غسل) means ritual washing (concept familiar from Islamic ablution)
  - This is EXPLICITLY Christian terminology and HIGHLY SENSITIVE in Muslim contexts
  - **BOLD INCLUSION** in Iranian context where conversion is illegal
- **Examples:**
  - "Baptism and How To Do It" → "غسل تعمید و نحوه انجام این کار" (Ghosle ta'mid va nahveye anjāme in kār)
  - "Baptism" → "تعمید" (Ta'mid)
  - "Have you ever baptized someone?" → "آیا تا بحال کسی را تعمید داده اید؟" (Ayā tā be hāl kasi ra ta'mid dāde-id?)
  - "IMPORTANT REMINDER – Have you been baptized?" → "یادآوری مهم – آیا شما تعمید گرفته اید؟" (Yādāvariye mohem - ayā shoma ta'mid gerefte-id?)
  - "baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit" → "آنها را به نام پدر و پسر و روح القدس تعمید دهید" (ānhā ra be nāme Pedar va Pesar va Ruh-ul-Qods ta'mid dehid)
- **Verb forms:**
  - تعمید دادن (ta'mid dādan) - to baptize (give baptism)
  - تعمید گرفتن (ta'mid gereftan) - to be baptized (receive baptism)

### Prayer / Pray
- **Persian:** دعا (noun), دعا کردن (verb)
- **Transliteration:** Do'ā / Do'ā kardan
- **Notes:** Arabic loanword used by both Muslims and Christians. Neutral and accessible. Muslims use "namāz" for ritual prayer, while "do'ā" is personal supplication - making this term suitable for both contexts.
- **Examples:**
  - "How to Spend an Hour in Prayer" → "چگونه یک ساعت را در دعا صرف کنیم" (Chegune yek sā'at ra dar do'ā sarf konim)
  - "Prayer Walking" → "دعای پیاده" (Do'āye piyāde)
  - "The BLESS Prayer Pattern" → "الگوی دعای BLESS" (Olguye do'āye BLESS)
  - "Prayer Cycle" → "چرخه دعا" (Charkheye do'ā)
  - "Pray and thank God" → "دعا کنید و خدا را شکر کنید" (Do'ā konid va Khoda ra shokr konid)

### Communion / Lord's Supper
- **Persian:** شام خداوند
- **Transliteration:** Shāme Khodavand
- **Notes:** Direct translation "Lord's Supper" avoiding Catholic/liturgical terminology like "Communion"
- **Examples:**
  - "The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It" → "شام خداوند و چگونه آن را رهبری کنیم" (Shāme Khodavand va chegune ān ra rahbari konim)
  - "The Lord's Supper" → "شام خداوند" (Shāme Khodavand)
  - "Celebrate the Lord's Supper" → "شام خداوند را برگزار کنید" (Shāme Khodavand ra bargozār konid)

### Worship
- **Persian:** NOT FOUND as standalone term
- **Notes:** Material focuses on practical discipleship actions rather than abstract worship theology

### Testimony
- **Persian:** شهادت
- **Transliteration:** Shahādat
- **IMPORTANT NOTE:** "Shahādat" (شهادت) is a powerful word in Persian/Islamic context:
  - In Islam, "shahadat" is the confession of faith ("there is no god but Allah...")
  - It also means "martyrdom" (shahid = martyr/witness)
  - Here used for Christian "testimony" about Jesus' impact on one's life
  - Strategic use of familiar Islamic terminology for Christian purposes
- **Examples:**
  - "Prepare Your 3-Minute Testimony" → "شهادت 3 دقیقه‌ای خود را آماده کنید" (Shahādāte 3 daqiqe-i khod ra āmāde konid)
  - "3-Minute Testimony" → "شهادت 3 دقیقه ای" (Shahādāte 3 daqiqe-i)
  - "Three-Minute Testimony" → "شهادت سه دقیقه‎ای" (Shahādāte se daqiqe-i)
  - "Practice sharing your testimony" → "اعلام شهادت خود را تمرین کنید" (E'lāme shahādāte khod ra tamrin konid)
  - "Share Your Testimony" → "شهادت خود را اعلام کنید" (Shahādāte khod ra e'lām konid)

### Witness (verb and noun)
- **Persian:** شاهد (noun), شهادت دادن (verb)
- **Transliteration:** Shāhed / Shahādat dādan
- **Notes:** From same root as "shahādat" (testimony). "Shāhed" means witness in both legal and religious senses.
- **Examples:**

## Key Translation Principles

### 1. Sophisticated Muslim-Background Contextualization
**Strategic choices for Muslim-majority Iran:**
- Uses **"Isa" (عیسی)** - Quranic name for Jesus (NOT "Yasu")
- Uses **"Khoda" (خدا)** - Persian native for God (NOT "Allah" - Arabic/Islamic)
- Uses **"Injil" (انجیل)** - Quranic term for Gospel (recognizable, non-threatening)
- **Mostly avoids "Masih"** (Christ/Messiah) - reduces Christian exclusivity
- Uses **familiar Islamic vocabulary** in Christian context (iman, do'ā, shahādat, eta'at)

### 2. Christian-Distinct Where Necessary
**Bold Christian identification:**
- **"Kelisa" (کلیسا)** - clearly Christian church, not avoided
- **"Ta'mid" (تعمید)** - explicit baptism terminology (sensitive/illegal in Iran)
- **Trinitarian formula** intact (Father, Son, Holy Spirit)
- **"Shāme Khodavand"** (Lord's Supper) - Christian sacrament
- **"Ruh-ul-Qods"** (Holy Spirit) - Trinitarian theology

### 3. Lexical Flexibility
**Persian native vs. Arabic loanwords:**
- **Persian native preferred** where available (Khoda, nasl, royā, roshd)
- **Arabic loanwords** for religious concepts (ruh, imān, barakat, eta'at, ta'lim)
- **Both used interchangeably** for some terms (ruhani/ma'navi, āmuzesh/ta'lim)
- **English loanwords** for modern concepts (mentor)

### 4. Formal Register Throughout
**Respectful educational tone:**
- Uses **formal "shoma"** (you) form consistently
- Professional/educational language, not colloquial
- Appropriate for training materials and unknown audience

### 5. Practical Discipleship Focus
**Action-oriented over doctrinal:**
- Emphasizes **doing** (obey, train, multiply) over **believing** (salvation, justification)
- **Avoids abstract theology** (salvation, grace, repentance as standalone concepts)
- **Movement language** (multiplication, generations, networks)
- **Obedience-based** discipleship (eta'at-centered)

### 6. House Church Ecclesiology
**Simple church model:**
- **"Kelisaye kuchak"** (small/simple church)
- Avoids **traditional clergy titles** (pastor, elder, apostle)
- Uses **trainer/coach/leader/mentor** language
- Suitable for **restrictive environments**

### 7. Missiological Concepts
**Key movement terminology:**
- **"Person of Peace"** (shakhse ahle solh) - receptive person concept
- **Multiplication** (taksir) emphasized over addition
- **Generations** (nasl) thinking - spiritual lineage
- **Networks** (shabake) - organic growth
- **Training** (āmuzesh/ta'lim) as core activity

### 8. Spirit-Led Emphasis
**Holy Spirit leadership:**
- Multiple references to "Ruh-ul-Qods rahbari konad" (Holy Spirit leading)
- Charismatic openness
- Trinitarian when necessary

## Linguistic Notes on Persian

### Script and Writing
- Uses **Arabic/Persian script** (right-to-left)
- **UTF-8 encoding**
- Proper **Persian-specific characters** (پ چ ژ گ)

- NOT **تو (to)** - informal singular
