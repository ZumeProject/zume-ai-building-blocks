## 1. Core Deity Terms

### Jesus | Исус
**Cyrillic:** Исус
**Transliteration:** Isus
**Pronunciation:** ee-SOOS

**Usage Notes:**
- Standard form across all Christian denominations in Bulgaria
- Unchanged in all grammatical cases (indeclinable)
- Orthodox tradition also uses full "Иисус" (Iisus) in liturgical texts, following Church Slavonic

**Common Phrases:**
1. "Follow Jesus" → "Следвай Исус" (Sledvay Isus)
2. "Jesus Christ" → "Исус Христос" (Isus Hristos)
3. "In Jesus' name" → "в името на Исус" (v imeto na Isus)
4. "Follower of Jesus" → "последовател на Исус" (posledovatel na Isus)
5. "Jesus said" → "Исус каза" (Isus kaza)

**Transliteration:** Hristos
**Pronunciation:** hree-STOS

**Common Phrases:**

**Cultural Notes:**
- Bulgaria has deep Orthodox Christian heritage dating to 9th century
- Church Slavonic influence remains strong in liturgical language

## Cultural and Linguistic Notes

### 1. Orthodox Christian Influence

Bulgaria's deep Orthodox Christian heritage (dating to 864 AD under Tsar Boris I) profoundly shapes religious vocabulary:

- **Church Slavonic loanwords**: Many spiritual terms derive from Church Slavonic, the liturgical language: Благодат (grace), Воскресение (resurrection), Евангелие (gospel)
- **Liturgical forms**: Orthodox worship uses archaic forms: "Господи, помилуй" (Lord have mercy), "Отче наш" (Our Father)
- **Sacramental emphasis**: Terms like "Причастие" (communion) carry strong sacramental connotations from Orthodox tradition

### 2. Post-Communist Religious Vocabulary

Communist era (1944-1989) suppressed religious language:

- **Secular alternatives**: Many terms developed secular meanings (служба = service/job, сбор = meeting)
- **Revival of religious terms**: Post-1989, religious vocabulary re-emerged but with generational gaps
- **Modern evangelical terms**: Protestant/Evangelical movements introduced new concepts not traditional in Orthodox Bulgaria

### 3. Slavic Language Patterns

Bulgarian shares features with other Slavic languages:

- **Loss of cases**: Unlike Russian, Bulgarian lost most case system (except vocative)
- **Definite articles**: Postpositive articles unique among Slavic languages (църква → църквата)
- **Verbal aspect**: Perfective/imperfective pairs crucial (вярвам/повярвам, спасявам/спася)

### 4. Church Slavonic vs. Modern Bulgarian

**Church Slavonic** (formal, liturgical):
- Христос воскресе (Christ is risen)
- Отче наш (Our Father)
- Свято Писмо (Holy Scripture)

**Modern Bulgarian** (everyday, contemporary):
- Христос възкръсна (Christ rose)
- Нашият Отец (Our Father - less formal)
- Свещено Писание (Holy Scripture - modern)

### 5. Orthodox vs. Protestant/Evangelical Terminology

**Orthodox preference**:
- Причастие (Communion - sacramental)
- Послушание (Obedience - monastic connotation)
- Свещеник (Priest)

**Evangelical preference**:
- Господна вечеря (Lord's Supper - memorial meal)
- Подчинение (Obedience - relational)
- Пастор (Pastor)

### 6. Transliteration Considerations

Cyrillic to Latin transliteration varies:

- **Scientific**: Bog, Tsărkva, Văzkresenie
- **Popular**: Bog, Tsarkva, Vazkresenie
- **English approximation**: Bog, Tserkva, Vuzkresenie

This document uses **scientific transliteration** for accuracy.
