# Nepali (ne_NP) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference Guide

## Overview

This reference guide documents how key spiritual and ministry terms are translated from English to Nepali in the Zume training materials. It is designed to help translators maintain consistency and understand the cultural and theological reasoning behind term choices.

**Language:** Nepali (ne_NP)
**Script:** Devanagari
**Source File:** zume-ne_NP.po
**Date Created:** 2026-01-16

## Core Deity Terms

### God
**Nepali:** परमेश्वर
**Transliteration:** Parmeshwar
**Literal meaning:** Supreme Lord/Ishwara
**Etymology:** Sanskrit compound: परम (param = supreme) + ईश्वर (ishwar = lord/deity)

**Usage Examples:**
- "God Uses Ordinary People" → "परमेश्वरले साधारण मानिसहरुलाई प्रयोग गर्नुहुन्छ ।"
- "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "एक चेला हुनु भनेको हामीले परमेश्वरबाट सुन्नु र परमेश्वरको आज्ञापालन गर्नु हो ।"
- "God's economy is different" → "परमेश्वरको अर्थतन्त्र संसारको अर्थतन्त्रबाट कसरी फरक छ"
- "Kingdom of God" → "परमेश्वरको राज्य"
- "God's Word" → "परमेश्वरको वचन"

**Translation Notes:**
- Consistently uses परमेश्वर throughout the document
- This is a Sanskrit loanword commonly used by Nepali Christians
- The term bridges Hindu and Christian contexts as it's recognizable to both communities
- Uses honorific verb forms (गर्नुहुन्छ) when referring to God's actions

### Jesus
**Nepali:** येशू
**Transliteration:** Yeshu
**Form:** Transliteration of Hebrew/Greek name

**Usage Examples:**
- "Jesus" (standalone) → "येशू"
- "Jesus Christ" → "येशू ख्रीष्ट"
- "followers of Jesus" → "येशूलाई पछ्याउने व्यक्तिहरु"
- "more like Jesus" → "येशू जस्तै"
- "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "गएर अनि बसेर कसरी येशूको आज्ञापालन गर्ने सिक्नुहोस्"

**Translation Notes:**
- Direct transliteration, standard across Nepali Christian contexts
- Uses possessive forms: येशूको (Yeshu-ko = of Jesus)
- Uses respectful language forms

### Christ
**Nepali:** ख्रीष्ट
**Transliteration:** Khrishta
**Literal meaning:** The Anointed One (transliteration of Greek Christos)

**Usage Examples:**
- "Jesus Christ" → "येशू ख्रीष्ट"
- "relationship with Jesus Christ" → "येशू ख्रीष्टसँगको सम्बन्ध"

**Translation Notes:**
- Almost always appears as part of the compound "येशू ख्रीष्ट" (Jesus Christ)
- Transliteration from Greek via English

### Holy Spirit
**Nepali:** पवित्र आत्मा
**Transliteration:** Pavitra Atma
**Literal meaning:** Holy Spirit/Soul

**Usage Examples:**
- "baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" → "पिता र पुत्र र पवित्र आत्‍माको नाउँमा तिनीहरूलाई बप्‍तिस्‍मा देओ"
- "when the Holy Spirit comes upon you" → "पवित्र आत्‍मा तिमीहरूमा आउनुभएपछि"
- "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together" → "पवित्र आत्मालाई तपाईँको समय अगुवाई गर्नुहोस्"
- "The Last 100 Hours map shows the Holy Spirit moving" → "Last 100 Hours नक्शाले पवित्र आत्माले आफ्ना मानिसहरूलाई... लैजाँदै गरेको देखाउँछ"

**Translation Notes:**
- Compound: पवित्र (pavitra = holy/sacred) + आत्मा (atma = spirit/soul)
- आत्मा (atma) is a Sanskrit loanword familiar to Hindus and Buddhists
- Uses respectful verb forms when describing the Spirit's actions

### Lord
**Nepali:** प्रभु
**Transliteration:** Prabhu
**Literal meaning:** Lord, Master

**Usage Examples:**
- "The Lord's Supper" → "प्रभु भोज"
- "When we are faithful to obey and share what the Lord has shared with us" → (context shows Lord used)

**Translation Notes:**
- प्रभु is a Sanskrit loanword meaning lord/master
- Also used in Hindu contexts (e.g., "Prabhu" as a title for Krishna)
- Creates theological bridge while maintaining Christian meaning

### Father (God the Father)
**Nepali:** पिता
**Transliteration:** Pita
**Literal meaning:** Father

**Usage Examples:**
- "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" → "पिता र पुत्र र पवित्र आत्‍माको नाउँमा"

**Translation Notes:**
- Standard Nepali word for father
- Used in Trinitarian formula

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
**Nepali:** चेला
**Transliteration:** Chela
**Literal meaning:** Student, disciple, follower (of a guru)

**Usage Examples:**
- "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "चेला र मण्डलीको सरल परिभाषा"
- "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "एक चेला हुनु भनेको हामीले परमेश्वरबाट सुन्नु र परमेश्वरको आज्ञापालन गर्नु हो"
- "God makes disciples more like Jesus" → "परमेश्वरले चेलाहरुलाई प्रत्येक दिन येशू जस्तै बनाउनुहुने"
- "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" → "परिपक्व चेलाहरूको लागि तालिम चक्र"
- "multiplying disciples" → "बृधि गर्ने चेलाहरु"
- "trained multiplying disciple" → "प्रशिक्षित गुणन शिष्य"

**Translation Notes:**
- Primary translation is चेला (chela), a familiar Hindu/Buddhist term for a disciple of a guru
- Occasionally uses शिष्य (shishya), another Sanskrit word for disciple/student
- चेला is more common in spoken Nepali, while शिष्य is more formal/literary
- Both terms carry the connotation of a devoted follower who learns from and imitates their teacher
- This cultural parallel makes the concept immediately accessible to Nepali readers

### Discipleship
**Nepali:** चेलापन
**Transliteration:** Chelapan
**Literal meaning:** State/quality of being a disciple

**Usage Examples:**
- "Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately" → "हाँसको चल्ला चेलापन- तुरुन्तै अगुवाई गर्ने"
- "What is one area of discipleship (reading/understanding the Bible, praying...)" → (context references discipleship areas)

**Alternative form:** अगुवापन (aguwapan = leadership)
- "Duckling Discipleship" → "हाँसको चल्ला अगुवापन" (in some contexts)

**Translation Notes:**
- चेलापन is formed by adding -पन (-pan) suffix to चेला, indicating a state or quality
- Sometimes translated contextually as leadership (अगुवापन) when emphasizing the leading aspect
- The concept is sometimes expressed through phrases rather than a single word

### Follower
**Nepali:** पछ्याउने व्यक्ति / पछ्याउने
**Transliteration:** Pachhyaune vyakti / Pachhyaune
**Literal meaning:** Following person / one who follows

**Usage Examples:**
- "follower of Jesus" → "येशूलाई पछ्याउने व्यक्तिहरु"
- "followers of Jesus meet" → "येशूलाई पछ्याउने व्यक्तिहरुसँग भेट्ने"
- "everyday followers more like Jesus" → (referenced in context about making disciples)
- "God uses faithful followers" → (referenced in context)

**Translation Notes:**
- Literally "person who follows" or "following person"
- Often appears as participle form: पछ्याउने (pachhyaune = following/follower)
- Combined with येशूलाई (Yeshu-lai = to Jesus) to mean "followers of Jesus"
- Sometimes interchangeable with चेला (disciple) in context

### Believer
**Nepali:** विश्वासी / बिश्वाशी
**Transliteration:** Vishwasi / Bishwashi
**Literal meaning:** One who believes/has faith

**Usage Examples:**
- "Unbeliever" → "अविश्वासी" / "अबिस्वाशी"
- "believers to begin their own S.O.A.P.S. Bible reading practice" → "बिश्वाशीहरुलाई आफ्नै... बाइबल अध्ययन अभ्यास"
- "another believer you know" → "विश्वासीसँग"
- "ordinary believers" → (referenced in context about empowering believers)
- "body of believers" → (referenced in context)
- "If your family are believers" → (context about practicing Lord's Supper)

**Translation Notes:**
- विश्वासी (vishwasi) is standard spelling
- बिश्वाशी (bishwashi) appears as variant spelling (phonetic variation)
- Prefix अ- (a-) added to create "unbeliever" → अविश्वासी/अबिस्वाशी
- From विश्वास/बिश्वास (vishwas/bishwas = faith, belief)
- Suffix -ी (-i) indicates "one who has/does"

## Church Terms

### Church
**Nepali:** मण्डली
**Transliteration:** Mandali
**Literal meaning:** Assembly, congregation, circle

**Usage Examples:**
- "Disciples and the Church" → "चेलाहरू र मण्डली"
- "Simple Church" → "साधारण मण्डली"
- "3/3 Group is not just a small group but a Simple Church" → "३/३ झुण्ड साधारण झुण्ड मात्रै नभई एउटा साधारण मण्डली हो"
- "multiplying churches" → "बृधि हुने मण्डलीहरु"
- "Churches Needed" → "मण्डलीको नाम"
- "Map of Zúme Churches" → "२ साधारण मण्डलीहरु"
- "2 simple churches per 5,000 people" → "प्रति 5,000 मान्छे 2 साधारण चर्च"

**Translation Notes:**
- Primary term is मण्डली (mandali)
- Occasionally uses चर्च (church) as a direct English loanword transliteration
- मण्डली is a Sanskrit loanword meaning assembly/congregation
- Used in Hindu contexts as well (religious assemblies)
- "Simple Church" consistently translated as साधारण मण्डली (sadharan mandali)
- Plural: मण्डलीहरु (mandali-haru)

### Simple Church
**Nepali:** साधारण मण्डली
**Transliteration:** Sadharan Mandali
**Literal meaning:** Simple/ordinary congregation

**Usage Examples:**
- "Simple Church" → "साधारण मण्डली"
- "Could a 3/3 Group be considered a Simple Church?" → "३/३ झुण्डलाई साधारण मण्डली भन्न मिल्ला ?"
- "What's described in the video as a 'Simple Church'" → "भिडियोमा वणर्न गरिएको साधारण मण्डली"
- "Always report new simple churches" → "तपाईंले सुरु गरिरहनुभएको नयाँ साधारण मण्डलीहरू सधैं रिपोर्ट गर्नुहोस्"
- "2 Simple Churches" → "२ साधारण मण्डलीहरु"

**Translation Notes:**
- Consistently translated as साधारण मण्डली throughout
- साधारण (sadharan) = simple, ordinary, common
- Emphasizes accessibility and simplicity
- Important Zume concept maintaining consistent translation

- Familiar political term that transfers well to spiritual concept

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
**Nepali:** सुसमाचार
**Transliteration:** Susamachar
**Literal meaning:** Good news/tidings

**Usage Examples:**
- "The Gospel and How to Share It" → "सुसमाचार र यसलाई कसरी बाँड्ने"
- "share God's good news" → "परमेश्वरको सुसमाचार बाँड्ने"
- "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" → "३-चक्रहरु सुसमाचार प्रस्तुति"
- "God's story (also called the gospel)" → "परमेश्वरको कथा (जसलाई सुसमाचार पनि भनिन्छ)"
- "tell the gospel" → "सुसमाचार सुनाउने"

**Translation Notes:**
- सुसमाचार is compound: सु (su = good) + समाचार (samachar = news)
- Standard Christian term in Nepali
- Literally translates the Greek "eu-angelion" (good news)
- Well-established in Nepali Christian vocabulary

### Bible
**Nepali:** बाइबल
**Transliteration:** Bible
**Form:** Direct transliteration from English

**Usage Examples:**
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" → "सोप्स (एस.ओ.ए.पि.एस) बाइबल अध्ययन"
- "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Reading" → "एस.ओ.ए.पि.एस बाइबल अध्ययन"
- "The Bible tells us" → "बाइबलले भन्दछ"
- "daily Bible study" → "दैनिक बाइबल अध्ययन"
- "Bible study or Small Group" → (referenced in comparison)
- "Personal Bible Study" → "व्यक्तिगत बाइबल अध्ययन"
- "Bible Reading" → "बाइबल पढाइ"

**Translation Notes:**
- Consistently uses बाइबल (Bible) as transliteration
- No attempt to translate as "Holy Book" or use Sanskrit equivalent
- Standard across Nepali Christian contexts
- Easily recognizable even to non-Christians

### Scripture / Word of God
**Nepali:** वचन
**Transliteration:** Vachan
**Literal meaning:** Word, speech, promise

**Usage Examples:**
- "Scripture" → "वचन"
- "God's Word" → "परमेश्वरको वचन"
- "helps you understand, obey, and share God's Word" → "परमेश्वरको वचन बुझ्न, आज्ञापालन गर्न, र बाँड्न सहायता गर्ने"
- "true to scripture" → (implied in context about telling God's story)
- "He promises in His Word" → (referenced in prayer contexts)

**Translation Notes:**
- वचन (vachan) means word, speech, or promise in Sanskrit/Nepali
- Used for both "Word" (scripture) and specific verses/promises
- Context determines whether it refers to Bible generally or specific passage
- Theological parallel to Greek "logos"

## Spiritual Qualities and Actions

### Obey / Obedience
**Nepali:** आज्ञापालन / आज्ञाकारीता
**Transliteration:** Aagyapalan / Aagyakarita
**Literal meaning:** Command-following / Obedience

**Usage Examples:**
- "we hear from God and we obey God" → "परमेश्वरबाट सुन्नु र परमेश्वरको आज्ञापालन गर्नु"
- "Hearing and Obeying God" → "परमेश्वरलाई सुन्नु र उहाँको आज्ञापालन गर्नु हो"
- "helps you understand, obey, and share God's Word" → "परमेश्वरको वचन बुझ्न, आज्ञापालन गर्न, र बाँड्न"
- "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "कसरी येशूको आज्ञापालन गर्ने सिक्नुहोस्"
- "Obeyed" → "आज्ञापालन गरेको"
- "Heard, Obeyed, Shared, Trained" → "सुनेको, आज्ञाकारीता, बाँडेको, प्रशिक्षित"
- "practice obeying and sharing" → "आज्ञा पालना गर्ने"
- "how 'faithful' are you in terms of obeying and sharing" → (context references obedience)
- "radical, costly obedience to Jesus" → (archived content)

**Translation Notes:**
- आज्ञापालन (aagyapalan) = command + following/observance
- आज्ञाकारीता (aagyakarita) = state of obedience
- आज्ञा (aagya) = command, order
- पालन (palan) = to follow, observe, keep

## Translation Patterns and Strategies

### 1. Loanword Strategy
The translation makes extensive use of Sanskrit loanwords that are familiar to both Hindu and Christian audiences:
- परमेश्वर (Parmeshwar - God)
- प्रभु (Prabhu - Lord)
- आत्मा (Atma - Spirit)
- चेला (Chela - Disciple)
- मण्डली (Mandali - Church)
- प्रार्थना (Prarthana - Prayer)

This creates theological bridges while maintaining Christian distinctives.

### 2. Transliteration for Biblical Terms
Names and certain theological terms are transliterated:
- येशू (Yeshu - Jesus)
- ख्रीष्ट (Khrishta - Christ)
- बप्तिस्मा (Baptisma - Baptism)
- बाइबल (Bible)

### 3. Honorific Language
The translation uses honorific verb forms when referring to God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit:
- गर्नुहुन्छ (garnuhunchha - does/makes - honorific)
- भन्नुभयो (bhannubhayo - said - honorific)
- आउनुभएपछि (aunubhayepachhi - after coming - honorific)

This shows proper respect in Nepali cultural context.

### 4. Compound Terms
Many spiritual concepts are expressed through compound words:
- आत्मिक परिवार (atmik parivar - spiritual family)
- साधारण मण्डली (sadharan mandali - simple church)
- जवाफदेहीता झुण्ड (javafadehita jhund - accountability group)
- प्रभु भोज (Prabhu bhoj - Lord's supper)

### 5. Cultural Adaptation
The translation uses culturally familiar concepts:
- चेला (chela) - disciple, evoking guru-disciple relationships familiar in Hinduism
- भोज (bhoj) - feast, a familiar communal meal concept
- शान्ति (shanti) - peace, universal spiritual concept

### 6. Multiple Translations
Some terms have variant translations:
- "Disciple" → चेला (chela) or शिष्य (shishya)
- "Leader" → नेता (neta) or अगुवा (aguwa)
- "Training" → तालिम (talim) or प्रशिक्षण (prashikshan)
- "Multiply" → बृधि (vridhi) or गुणन (gunan)

The variation often reflects register (formal vs. informal) or emphasis (political vs. spiritual leadership).

## Cultural and Religious Context

### Hindu-Buddhist Influence
Many spiritual terms have Hindu-Buddhist parallels:
- **चेला (chela)** - The guru-chela relationship is central to Hinduism
- **आत्मा (atma)** - Hindu concept of soul/self (Atman)
- **मुक्ति (mukti)** - Hindu/Buddhist liberation (moksha)
- **प्रभु (Prabhu)** - Used for Krishna and other Hindu deities
- **भोज (bhoj)** - Hindu communal feasting tradition (bhandara)
- **शान्ति (shanti)** - Universal peace concept (Om Shanti)

### Translation Philosophy
The translation strategy appears to:
1. **Use familiar religious vocabulary** from Hindu-Buddhist tradition
2. **Fill with Christian meaning** through context and Biblical references
3. **Maintain distinctiveness** through terms like येशू ख्रीष्ट (Jesus Christ)
4. **Emphasize practice** over abstract theology
5. **Use respectful language** honoring Nepali cultural norms

### Contextual Appropriateness
The translation choices show sensitivity to:
- **Caste/hierarchy consciousness** - Using appropriate honorifics
- **Guru tradition** - Leveraging chela-guru relationship model
- **Oral culture** - Emphasis on doing, sharing, practicing
- **Community values** - Family language (परिवार - parivar)
- **Religious pluralism** - Using bridges without compromising distinctives

## Recommendations for Translators

### 1. Consistency in Key Terms
Maintain consistent translation for core concepts:
- Always use परमेश्वर for God
- Always use येशू or येशू ख्रीष्ट for Jesus/Jesus Christ
- Always use चेला for disciple
- Always use साधारण मण्डली for Simple Church
- Always use पवित्र आत्मा for Holy Spirit

### 2. Honorific Forms
Always use honorific verb forms when God, Jesus, or the Holy Spirit is the subject:
- गर्नुहुन्छ (not गर्छ)
- भन्नुभयो (not भन्यो)
- आउनुभयो (not आयो)

### 3. Cultural Bridges
Leverage Hindu-Buddhist vocabulary where appropriate:
- चेला (chela) connects to familiar guru-disciple model
- Use Sanskrit loanwords that resonate with educated Nepali speakers
- Frame Christian concepts in culturally accessible terms

### 4. Action-Oriented Language
Emphasize practice and doing:
- Use अभ्यास (practice) frequently
- Stress आज्ञापालन (obedience) as active following
- Emphasize बाँड्नु (sharing) as core action

### 5. Family Language
Use family metaphors extensively:
- आत्मिक परिवार (spiritual family)
- परिवार (family) for church community
- Leverage Nepali strong family values

### 6. Avoid Over-Theologizing
Keep language simple and accessible:
- Use साधारण (ordinary/simple) to emphasize accessibility
- Prefer common words over technical theological terms
- Focus on practical application

### 7. Register Awareness
Choose appropriate register for context:
- Formal contexts: प्रशिक्षण, शिष्य, नेतृत्व
- Informal contexts: तालिम, चेला, अगुवाई
- Maintain consistency within a document

### 8. Spelling Consistency
Be aware of phonetic variants and choose one:
- विश्वास or बिश्वास (vishwas/bishwas) - prefer विश्वास
- विश्वासी or बिश्वाशी (vishwasi/bishwashi) - prefer विश्वासी
