# Russian (ru_RU) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference Guide

## Overview
This guide documents the translation of key spiritual and ministry terms from English to Russian as used in the Zume training materials. The Russian translations reflect Protestant evangelical terminology rather than Russian Orthodox Church tradition.

**Source File:** `zume-ru_RU.po`
**Language Code:** ru_RU (Russian - Russia)
**Script:** Cyrillic
**Target Audience:** Protestant/Evangelical believers in Russian-speaking regions

---

## Core Spiritual Concepts

### 1. УЧЕНИК (Uchenik) - Disciple

**English:** Disciple
**Russian:** Ученик (uchenik)
**Transliteration:** oo-cheh-NEEK
**Literal Meaning:** Student, learner, follower

#### Grammatical Forms (6 Cases):
- **Nominative:** ученик (the disciple)
- **Genitive:** ученика (of the disciple)
- **Dative:** ученику (to the disciple)
- **Accusative:** ученика (the disciple - direct object)
- **Instrumental:** учеником (by/with the disciple)
- **Prepositional:** об ученике (about the disciple)

#### Plural Forms:
- **Nominative:** ученики (disciples)
- **Genitive:** учеников (of disciples)

#### Usage Examples:
- "Simple Definition of Disciple" → "Простое определение понятий 'ученик'"
- "Making Disciples" → "Приобретение учеников"
- "Being a Disciple" → "Быть учеником"
- "What's a Disciple?" → "Что такое ученик?"
- "Disciples and the Church" → "Ученики и Церковь"
- "Duckling Discipleship" → "Ученичество по-утиному"
- "Multiplying disciples" → "приумножающимися учениками"

#### Related Terms:
- **Ученичество** (uchenichestvo) - discipleship (the process)
- **Приобретение учеников** - Making disciples
- **Умножение учеников** - Multiplying disciples

#### Theological Context:
The term избегает Orthodox terminology (послушник - novice in monastery) and uses standard educational terminology to emphasize the learning/following relationship with Jesus.

**English:** Church
**Russian:** Церковь (tserkov')
**Transliteration:** TSER-koff
**Literal Meaning:** Assembly, congregation

#### Grammatical Forms (6 Cases):
- **Nominative:** церковь (the church)

- **Dative:** церкви (to the church)
- **Accusative:** церковь (the church - direct object)
- **Instrumental:** церковью (by/with the church)
- **Prepositional:** о церкви (about the church)

#### Plural Forms:
- **Nominative:** церкви (churches)
- **Genitive:** церквей (of churches)
- **Accusative:** церкви (churches - direct object)

#### Usage Examples:
- "Simple church" → "Простая церковь"
- "What's a Church?" → "Что такое церковь?"
- "My Churches" → "Мои церкви"
- "Always Part of Two Churches" → "Быть частью двух церквей"

- "Churches Needed" → "Требуются церкви"

#### Theological Context:
**ВАЖНО:** Uses the same word (церковь) as Russian Orthodox Church, but context differentiates:
- **Simple church** (простая церковь) - emphasizes non-institutional gathering
- Avoids **храм** (temple/church building) and **собор** (cathedral)
- Focus on **община** (community/fellowship) aspect

#### Related Terms:
- **Простая церковь** - Simple church (house church model)
- **Церковное** - Church-related (adjective)

## Cultural and Theological Considerations

### 1. Protestant vs. Orthodox Distinctions

**Emphasized in Russian Protestant Context:**
- Personal relationship with Jesus (личные отношения с Иисусом)
- Bible reading and study (чтение и изучение Библии)
- Personal prayer (личная молитва)
- Evangelism and witnessing (евангелизация и свидетельство)
- Simplicity in church structure (простота церковной структуры)

**De-emphasized (Orthodox associations):**
- Liturgical calendar and saints' days
- Veneration of icons (иконопочитание)
- Elaborate ceremonies and vestments
- Hierarchical church structure
- Monastic terminology

### 2. Church Slavonic Loanwords

Some Church Slavonic forms still appear in Protestant Russian:

- **Господь** (Gospod') - Lord (Church Slavonic form, still widely used)
- **Святый** vs. **Святой** (both forms of "Holy" exist)
- **Благословение** (Blagosloveniye) - Blessing (Church Slavonic root)
- **Благодать** (Blagodat') - Grace (Church Slavonic root)

### 3. Borrowings from English

Some English terms are transliterated in Russian Protestant contexts:

- **Тренинг** (Trening) - Training (borrowed)
- **Лидер** (Lider) - Leader (borrowed, alongside Russian вождь/руководитель)
- **Коуч** (Kouch) - Coach (borrowed, but **наставник** preferred in Zume)

## Pronunciation Guide

### Stress Patterns
Russian words have one stressed syllable (marked in caps in transliterations above).

**Key pronunciation notes:**
- **Ц** (ts) - as in "cats"
- **Ч** (ch) - as in "church"
- **Щ** (shch) - as in "fresh cheese"
- **Х** (kh) - as in German "Bach"
- **Ж** (zh) - as in "measure"
- **Ш** (sh) - as in "ship"
- **Ь** (soft sign) - softens preceding consonant
- **Ъ** (hard sign) - separates syllables

### Vowel Reduction
Unstressed vowels are often reduced:
- **О** → sounds like **А** when unstressed
- **Е** → sounds like **И** when unstressed
