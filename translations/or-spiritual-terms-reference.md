# Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference
## Zúme Training Materials

**Language:** Odia (Oriya)
**Script:** Odia script (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଲିପି)
**Region:** Odisha state, India
**Total Entries Analyzed:** 1,346 translation pairs
**Date:** January 2026

---

## Introduction

This reference guide documents how key spiritual and ministry terms are translated from English to Odia in the Zúme training materials. Odia is an Indo-Aryan language spoken primarily in Odisha state, India, with significant Hindu cultural influence, particularly from the Jagannath temple tradition in Puri.

The translation strategy uses a mix of Sanskrit loanwords and native Odia terms to communicate Christian concepts effectively within this cultural context.

## I. Core Deity Terms

### 1. God

**Odia Translation:** ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) / ଈଶ୍ୱର (Ishwar)

**Transliteration:** Ishwara/Ishwar

**Etymology:** Sanskrit loanword, commonly used in Hindu contexts for the supreme deity

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"God Uses Ordinary People"**
   - ଈଶ୍ବର ସାଧାରଣ ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କୁ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରନ୍ତି
   - Ishwara sadhāraṇa lokamāṅkū byabahāra karanti

2. **"Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କ କଥା ଶୁଣିବା ଓ ଆଜ୍ଞାବହ ହେବା
   - Ishwaraṅka kathā śuṇibā o ājñābaha hebā

3. **"Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God"**
   - ଜଣେ ଶିଷ୍ୟ ହେବାର ଅର୍ଥ ହେଉଛି ଆମେ ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କଠାରୁ ଶୁଣିବା ଏବଂ ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କ ଆଜ୍ଞା ମାନିବା
   - Jaṇe śiṣya hebāra artha heuuchi āme Ishwaraṅkaṭhāru śuṇibā ebaṅ Ishwaraṅka ājñā mānibā

4. **"Sharing God's Story"**
   - ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କ କାହାଣୀ ବାଣ୍ଟିବା
   - Ishwaraṅka kahāṇi bāṇṭibā

**Notes:** The term ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) is widely accepted in Christian usage in Odia, despite its Hindu origins. It effectively communicates the concept of the supreme personal God.

### 2. Jesus

**Odia Translation:** ଯୀଶୁ (Yīśu)

**Transliteration:** Yīśu

**Etymology:** Transliteration from English/Greek

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"How to Follow Jesus"**
   - ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କୁ କିପରି ଅନୁସରଣ କରିବା
   - Yīśuṅku kipari anusaraṇa karibā

2. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କର ଅନୁଗାମୀ ମାନେ କ'ଣ?
   - Yīśuṅkara anugāmī māne kaṇa?

3. **"Learn how to obey Jesus' commands by going AND staying"**
   - ଯିବା ଏବଂ ରହିବା ଦ୍ୱାରା ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କ ଆଦେଶକୁ କିପରି ପାଳନ କରିବେ ଶିଖନ୍ତୁ
   - Yibā ebaṅ rahibā dwārā Yīśuṅka ādeśaku kipari pāḷana karibe śikhantu

**Notes:** The name is consistently transliterated as ଯୀଶୁ (Yīśu) with genitive form ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କ (Yīśuṅka).

### 3. Christ

**Odia Translation:** ଖ୍ରୀଷ୍ଟ (Khrīṣṭa)

**Transliteration:** Khrīṣṭa

**Etymology:** Transliteration from Greek Christos

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Body of Christ"**
   - ଖ୍ରୀଷ୍ଟଙ୍କ ଶରୀର
   - Khrīṣṭaṅka śarīra

2. **"Christ's death, burial, and resurrection"**
   - ଖ୍ରୀଷ୍ଟଙ୍କ ମୃତ୍ୟୁ, ସମାଧି ଓ ପୁନରୁତ୍ଥାନ
   - Khrīṣṭaṅka mṛtyu, samādhi o punarutthāna

   - ଖ୍ରୀଷ୍ଟଙ୍କ ମନ
   - Khrīṣṭaṅka mana

**Notes:** Used primarily in theological contexts, often paired with Jesus (Yīśu Khrīṣṭa).

### 4. Holy Spirit

**Odia Translation:** ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା (Pabitrātmā) / ପବିତ୍ରାତ୍ମା (Pabitrātmā)

**Transliteration:** Pabitrātmā

**Etymology:**
- ପବିତ୍ର (pabitra) = holy, sacred (Sanskrit: pavitra)
- ଆତ୍ମା (ātmā) = spirit, soul (Sanskrit: ātman)

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together"**
   - ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କ ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମାଙ୍କୁ ମାଗନ୍ତୁ ଯେ ସେ ଆପଣମାନଙ୍କ ଏକତ୍ରିତ ହୋଇଥିବା ସମୟର ନେତୃତ୍ୱ ନିଅନ୍ତୁ
   - Ishwaraṅka pabitrātmāṅku māgantu ye se āpaṇamāṅka ekatrita hoithibā samayara netṛtwa niāntu

2. **"The Last 100 Hours map shows the Holy Spirit moving his people"**
   - ଶେଷ ୧୦୦ ଘଣ୍ଟାର ମାନଚିତ୍ର ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା ତାଙ୍କ ଲୋକମାନଙ୍କୁ ଯୁମେ ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ସମଗ୍ର ବିଶ୍ୱରେ ଶିଷ୍ୟ କରିବାକୁ ପ୍ରେରିତ କରୁଥିବା ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନ କରେ
   - Śeṣa 100 ghaṇṭāra mānacitra pabitrātmā tāṅka lokamāṅku Yume mādhyamare samagra biśware śiṣya karibāku prerita karuthiba pradarśana kare

3. **"You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you"**
   - ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା ତୁମ୍ଭମାନଙ୍କ ଉପରେ ଅବତୀର୍ଣ୍ଣ ହୁଅନ୍ତେ, ତୁମ୍ଭେମାନେ ଶକ୍ତିପ୍ରାପ୍ତ ହେବ
   - Pabitrātmā tumbhamāṅka upare abatīrṇṇa huante, tumbhemāne śaktipprāpta heba

**Notes:** The term uses Hindu philosophical vocabulary (ātmā) but clearly distinguishes the Christian concept through the modifier "pabitra" (holy).

## II. Discipleship Terms

### 1. Disciple

**Odia Translation:** ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya)

**Transliteration:** Śiṣya

**Etymology:** Sanskrit word meaning student, pupil, disciple (widely used in Hindu guru-disciple tradition)

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"**
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀର ସରଳ ପରିଭାଷା
   - Śiṣya ebaṅ maṇḍalīra saraḷa paribhāṣā

2. **"Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God"**
   - ଜଣେ ଶିଷ୍ୟ ହେବାର ଅର୍ଥ ହେଉଛି ଆମେ ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କଠାରୁ ଶୁଣିବା ଏବଂ ଈଶ୍ବରଙ୍କ ଆଜ୍ଞା ମାନିବା
   - Jaṇe śiṣya hebāra artha heuuchi āme Ishwaraṅkaṭhāru śuṇibā ebaṅ Ishwaraṅka ājñā mānibā

3. **"Make disciples of all nations"**
   - ସମସ୍ତ ଜାତିର ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ... ମୋର ଶିଷ୍ୟ କର
   - Samasta jātira lokaṅku... mora śiṣya kara

4. **"1 trained multiplying disciple per 5,000"**
   - ପ୍ରତି ୫,୦୦୦ ଲୋକରେ ଜଣେ ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷିତ ବହୁଗୁଣିତ ହେଉଥିବା ଶିଷ୍ୟ
   - Prati 5,000 lokare jaṇe praśikṣita baguguṇita heuthibā śiṣya

**Cultural Notes:** The term ଶିଷ୍ୟ (śiṣya) is deeply familiar in Odia culture through the guru-śiṣya (teacher-student) tradition, making it an excellent translation that carries appropriate connotations of learning, obedience, and discipleship.

### 2. Discipleship

**Odia Translation:** ଶିଷ୍ୟତ୍ୱ (Śiṣyatwa)

**Transliteration:** Śiṣyatwa

**Etymology:** ଶିଷ୍ୟ (śiṣya = disciple) + ତ୍ୱ (twa = -ness, state of being)

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately"**
   - ବତକଛୁଆ ଶିଷ୍ୟତ୍ୱ - ତୁରନ୍ତ ନେତୃତ୍ୱ କରିବା
   - Batakachuā śiṣyatwa - turanta netṛtwa karibā

2. **"Discipleship Concepts"**
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟତ୍ୱର ଧାରଣା
   - Śiṣyatwara dhāraṇā

**Notes:** The suffix -twa creates an abstract noun, similar to English "-ship" in "discipleship."

### 3. Follower (of Jesus)

**Odia Translation:** ଅନୁଗାମୀ (Anugāmī) / ଅନୁସରଣକାରୀ (Anusaraṇakārī)

**Transliteration:** Anugāmī / Anusaraṇakārī

**Etymology:**
- ଅନୁ (anu) = after, following
- ଗାମୀ (gāmī) = one who goes
- ଅନୁସରଣ (anusaraṇa) = following + କାରୀ (kārī) = doer

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କର ଅନୁଗାମୀ ମାନେ କ'ଣ?
   - Yīśuṅkara anugāmī māne kaṇa?

2. **"God makes everyday followers more like Jesus"**
   - ଈଶ୍ବର ଦୈନନ୍ଦିନ ଅନୁଗାମୀମାନଙ୍କୁ ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କ ପରି ଅଧିକ କରିବାର ଚାରିଟି ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଉପାୟ
   - Ishwara dainandin anugāmīmāṅku Yīśuṅka pari adhika karibāra cāriṭi mukhya upāya

3. **"Every follower of Jesus will one day be held accountable"**
   - ଯୀଶୁଙ୍କର ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଅନୁଗାମୀ ଦିନେ ନା ଦିନେ... ଉତ୍ତରଦାୟୀ ହେବା
   - Yīśuṅkara pratyeka anugāmī dine nā dine... uttaradāyī hebā

**Notes:** Both terms are used, with ଅନୁଗାମୀ (anugāmī) being more common and concise.

### 4. Make Disciples / Disciple-Making

**Odia Translation:** ଶିଷ୍ୟ କରିବା (Śiṣya karibā) / ଶିଷ୍ୟ ତିଆରି କରିବା (Śiṣya tiāri karibā) / ଶିଷ୍ୟ ନିର୍ମାଣ କରିବା (Śiṣya nirmāṇa karibā)

**Transliteration:** Śiṣya karibā / Śiṣya tiāri karibā / Śiṣya nirmāṇa karibā

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Make disciples of all nations"**
   - ସମସ୍ତ ଜାତିର ଲୋକଙ୍କୁ... ମୋର ଶିଷ୍ୟ କର
   - Samasta jātira lokaṅku... mora śiṣya kara

2. **"Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making"**
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟ ତିଆରି କରିବା ସହ ବତକଛୁଆର ସମ୍ପର୍କ କ'ଣ ଜାଣନ୍ତୁ
   - Śiṣya tiāri karibā saha batakachuāra samparka kaṇa jāṇantu

3. **"Making disciples who multiply"**
   - ବହୁଗୁଣିତ କରୁଥିବା ଶିଷ୍ୟ କରିବା
   - Baguguṇita karuthibe śiṣya karibā

**Notes:** Three variations exist:
- ଶିଷ୍ୟ କରିବା (direct: to disciple)
- ଶିଷ୍ୟ ତିଆରି କରିବା (to make/prepare disciples)
- ଶିଷ୍ୟ ନିର୍ମାଣ କରିବା (to construct/build disciples)

### 5. Training / Train

**Odia Translation:** ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ (Praśikṣaṇa) - noun / ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ ଦେବା (Praśikṣaṇa debā) - verb

**Transliteration:** Praśikṣaṇa / Praśikṣaṇa debā

**Etymology:** Sanskrit-based term widely used in modern Odia for training/education

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Training"**
   - ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ
   - Praśikṣaṇa

2. **"Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples"**
   - ପରିପକ୍ୱ ହେଉଥିବା ଶିଷ୍ୟମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ ଚକ୍ର
   - Paripakwa heuthibā śiṣyamāṅka pāiṅ praśikṣaṇa cakra

3. **"Trained multiplying disciple"**
   - ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷିତ ବହୁଗୁଣିତ ହେଉଥିବା ଶିଷ୍ୟ
   - Praśikṣita baguguṇita heuthibā śiṣya

**Notes:** Modern, widely understood term for training and education.

## III. Church Terms

### 1. Church

**Odia Translation:** ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (Maṇḍalī)

**Transliteration:** Maṇḍalī

**Etymology:** Sanskrit मण्डली (maṇḍalī) meaning assembly, congregation, circle

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"**
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀର ସରଳ ପରିଭାଷା
   - Śiṣya ebaṅ maṇḍalīra saraḷa paribhāṣā

2. **"Disciples and the Church"**
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟ ଏବଂ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ
   - Śiṣya ebaṅ maṇḍalī

3. **"Always Part of Two Churches"**
   - ସର୍ବଦା ଦୁଇଟି ମଣ୍ଡଳୀର ଅଂଶ
   - Sabadā duitī maṇḍalīra aṅśa

4. **"Churches Needed"**
   - ମଣ୍ଡଳୀର ଆବଶ୍ୟକ
   - Maṇḍalīra ābaśyaka

5. **"2 simple churches per 5,000 people"**
   - ପ୍ରତି ୫,୦୦୦ ଲୋକରେ ୨ଟି ସରଳ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ
   - Prati 5,000 lokare 2ṭī saraḷa maṇḍalī

**Notes:** The term ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ is the standard Christian term for church in Odia, though it originally meant any assembly or gathering.

### 2. Simple Church

**Odia Translation:** ସରଳ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (Saraḷa maṇḍalī) / ସାଧାରଣ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (Sādhāraṇa maṇḍalī)

**Transliteration:** Saraḷa maṇḍalī / Sādhāraṇa maṇḍalī

**Etymology:**
- ସରଳ (saraḷa) = simple, straightforward
- ସାଧାରଣ (sādhāraṇa) = simple, common, ordinary

**Usage Examples:**

1. **"Always report new simple churches that you are starting"**
   - ଆପଣ ପ୍ରାରମ୍ଭ କରୁଥିବା ନୂତନ ସରଳ ଚର୍ଚ୍ଚଗୁଡିକ ସର୍ବଦା ରିପୋର୍ଟ କରନ୍ତୁ
   - Āpaṇa prārambha karuthibe nūtana saraḷa carcacaguḍika sabadā ripōrṭa karantu

2. **"2 simple churches per 5,000 people in the United States"**
   - ଯୁକ୍ତରାଷ୍ଟ୍ର ଆମେରିକାରେ ପ୍ରତି ୫,୦୦୦ ଲୋକରେ ୨ଟି ସରଳ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ
   - Yuktarāṣṭra Amerikāre prati 5,000 lokare 2ṭi saraḷa maṇḍalī

**Notes:** Distinguishes house churches or simple gatherings from institutional church buildings.

## XIV. Translation Patterns and Strategies

### 1. Transliteration Strategy

The Odia translation uses direct transliteration for:

**Proper Names:**
- Jesus → ଯୀଶୁ (Yīśu)
- Christ → ଖ୍ରୀଷ୍ଟ (Khrīṣṭa)
- Bible → ବାଇବଲ (Bāibala)
- Baptism → ବାପ୍ତିସ୍ମ (Bāptisma)

**Acronyms:**
- S.O.A.P.S. → ଏସ.ଓ.ଏ.ପି.ଏସ. (Es.o.e.pi.es.)
- BLESS → ବିଏଲ୍‍ଇଏସ୍ଏସ୍‍ (Bie.l.i.es.es.)

### 2. Sanskrit Loanword Strategy

Heavy reliance on Sanskrit-based vocabulary for spiritual/theological concepts:

- ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) - God
- ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya) - disciple
- ଆତ୍ମା (Ātmā) - spirit/soul
- ପ୍ରାର୍ଥନା (Prārthanā) - prayer
- ଶାସ୍ତ୍ର (Śāstra) - scripture
- ଆଧ୍ୟାତ୍ମିକ (Ādhyātmika) - spiritual

**Rationale:** These terms bridge Hindu-Christian understanding while maintaining theological precision.

### 3. Compound Formation

Odia creates compounds for complex Christian concepts:

- ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା (Pabitra + ātmā) = Holy Spirit
- ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (Maṇḍalī) = Church (assembly)
- ସୁସମାଚାର (Su + samācāra) = Gospel (good news)
- ପ୍ରଭୁଭୋଜ (Prabhu + bhoja) = Lord's Supper
- ସ୍ବର୍ଗରାଜ୍ୟ (Swarga + rājya) = Kingdom of Heaven

### 4. Guru-Disciple Framework

The translation leverages the familiar guru-śiṣya (teacher-disciple) cultural framework:

- ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya) = disciple
- ଶିଷ୍ୟତ୍ୱ (Śiṣyatwa) = discipleship
- ନେତା (Netā) = leader
- ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ (Praśikṣaṇa) = training

This creates cultural resonance while maintaining biblical fidelity.

### 5. Modern Administrative Vocabulary

Contemporary organizational terms applied to church structures:

- ଉତ୍ତରଦାୟିତ୍ୱ (Uttaradāyitwa) = accountability
- ପରିଚାଳକତ୍ଵ (Paricāḷakatwa) = stewardship
- ଗୋଷ୍ଠୀ (Goṣṭhī) = group
- ସମୁଦାୟ (Samudāya) = community
- ଆନ୍ଦୋଳନ (Āndoḷana) = movement

### 6. Action-Oriented Language

Emphasis on verbs and action rather than abstract theology:

- ଶୁଣିବା (śuṇibā) = hearing/listening
- ମାନିବା (mānibā) = obeying
- ବାଣ୍ଟିବା (bāṇṭibā) = sharing
- ପାଳନ କରିବା (pāḷana karibā) = practicing/observing
- ଅଭ୍ୟାସ କରିବା (abhyāsa karibā) = practicing

Reflects Zúme's emphasis on practical discipleship.

## XV. Cultural and Linguistic Notes

### 1. Hindu-Christian Bridge Terms

Many terms successfully bridge Hindu and Christian contexts:

**ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) - God**
- Hindu context: Supreme deity, often Shiva
- Christian adaptation: Personal God of the Bible
- Bridge: Both traditions affirm supreme personal deity

**ଆତ୍ମା (Ātmā) - Soul/Spirit**
- Hindu context: Eternal self (Atman)
- Christian adaptation: Human spirit/soul or Holy Spirit (with ପବିତ୍ର pabitra)
- Bridge: Spiritual dimension of human existence

**ପ୍ରାର୍ଥନା (Prārthanā) - Prayer**
- Hindu context: Devotional prayer to deities
- Christian adaptation: Communication with God
- Bridge: Universal concept of addressing the divine

**ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya) - Disciple**
- Hindu context: Student of a guru
- Christian adaptation: Follower/learner of Jesus
- Bridge: Both emphasize obedience, learning, imitation

### 2. Jagannath Temple Cultural Context

Odisha is home to the famous Jagannath Temple in Puri, one of the four dhams (sacred pilgrimage sites) in Hinduism. This influences:

- **Familiarity with religious terminology:** Terms like ଭକ୍ତ (bhakta = devotee), ଆରାଧନା (ārādhanā = worship), ପ୍ରସାଦ (prasāda = blessed food) are deeply understood
- **Temple vs. Gathered Church:** The concept of ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (maṇḍalī = assembly/church) as people rather than building contrasts with temple-centric Hinduism
- **Spiritual authority:** The guru-disciple (ଗୁରୁ-ଶିଷ୍ୟ) relationship provides natural framework for discipleship

### 3. Odia Script Considerations

The Odia script (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଲିପି) is an abugida derived from Brahmic scripts:

- **Conjunct consonants:** Complex ligatures like କ୍ଷ (kṣa), ଜ୍ଞ (jña)
- **Vowel markers:** Diacritics modify consonants (e.g., କ ka, କା kā, କି ki)
- **Anusvara and Chandrabindu:** Nasalization marks (ଂ, ଁ)
- **Visual similarity:** Some letters look similar (ର ra vs ଲ la), requiring careful reading

### 4. Diglossia in Odia

Odia exhibits diglossia (high vs. low registers):

- **Standard Odia (ସଅର):** Used in writing, formal speech, literature
- **Colloquial Odia:** Varies by region, used in daily conversation

The translation uses **Standard Odia**, making it accessible across Odisha while maintaining formal respectability appropriate for spiritual instruction.

### 5. Honorific Forms

Odia uses honorific language for respect:

- **ଆପଣ (āpaṇa):** Formal "you"
- **ତୁମେ (tume):** Informal "you"
- **ତୁମ୍ଭେ (tumbhe):** Respectful "you" (archaic/biblical style)

The translation uses formal honorifics when addressing readers and respectful forms when quoting Jesus.

### 6. Regional Variations

Odisha has dialectal variations:
- **Northern Odia:** Influenced by Bengali
- **Southern Odia:** Influenced by Telugu
- **Western Odia:** More rural, distinct phonology
- **Standard/Puri dialect:** Basis for written Odia

The Zúme translation uses **Standard Odia** based on the Puri-Cuttack dialect, understood statewide.

## XVI. Theological Considerations

### 1. Monotheism vs. Henotheism

**Challenge:** Hindu tradition often interpreted as henotheistic (many gods, one supreme) or polytheistic

**Translation Strategy:**
- Clear use of ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) for the ONE God
- Trinitarian language: ପିତା, ପୁତ୍ର ଓ ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit)
- Avoidance of generic ଦେବତା (debatā = deity/god) in favor of specific ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara)

### 2. Atman vs. Soul

**Challenge:** Hindu concept of ଆତ୍ମା (ātmā) = eternal, unchanging self identical with Brahman differs from Christian anthropology

**Translation Strategy:**
- Uses ଆତ୍ମା (ātmā) for spirit/soul but context clarifies Christian understanding
- Pairs with ହୃଦୟ (hṛdaya = heart) to emphasize personal rather than impersonal concept
- Qualifies with ପବିତ୍ର (pabitra = holy) when referring to Holy Spirit

### 3. Karma vs. Grace

**Challenge:** Hindu karma (ବିଧି bidhi, କର୍ମ karma) emphasizes works; Christianity emphasizes grace

**Translation Strategy:**
- Uses ଅନୁଗ୍ରହ (anugraha) and କୃପା (kṛpā) for grace
- Emphasizes ବିଶ୍ୱାସ (biśwāsa = faith) alongside works
- "Faithfulness" (ବିଶ୍ୱସ୍ତତା biśwasatā) rather than mere knowledge

### 4. Moksha vs. Salvation

**Challenge:** Hindu ମୁକ୍ତି (mukti = liberation from samsara/rebirth) differs from Christian salvation

**Translation Strategy:**
- While ମୁକ୍ତି (mukti) and ପରିତ୍ରାଣ (paritrāṇa) both work, context provides Christian framework
- Emphasis on relationship with ଈଶ୍ବର (God) rather than escape from illusion
- Gospel as ସୁସମାଚାର (good news), not philosophical system

### 5. Avatar vs. Incarnation

**Challenge:** Hindu concept of avatar (divine descent) could be confused with Incarnation

**Translation Strategy:**
- Does not use ଅବତାର (abatāra = avatar)
- Emphasizes Jesus as ପୁତ୍ର (Son) of God, unique relationship
- Resurrection (ପୁନରୁତ୍ଥାନ punarutthāna) distinguishes from mythological narratives

## XVIII. Practical Translation Principles

### Principles Observed in the Odia Translation:

1. **Cultural Accessibility**
   - Uses familiar Sanskrit-based religious vocabulary
   - Leverages guru-śiṣya (teacher-disciple) cultural framework
   - Employs terms that resonate with Odia Hindu background while maintaining biblical fidelity

2. **Clarity Over Literalism**
   - "Person of Peace" → ଶାନ୍ତିର ପାତ୍ର (vessel/worthy person of peace)
   - "Simple church" → ସରଳ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (simple assembly)
   - Contextualizes rather than word-for-word translation

3. **Action-Oriented Language**
   - Emphasizes verbs: hearing, obeying, sharing, training, multiplying
   - Reflects Zúme's focus on practical discipleship over abstract theology
   - Uses gerunds and participles to maintain active voice

4. **Consistency in Key Terms**
   - ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) consistently for God
   - ଯୀଶୁ (Yīśu) consistently for Jesus
   - ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya) consistently for disciple
   - ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ (Maṇḍalī) consistently for church

5. **Honorific Respect**
   - Uses formal ଆପଣ (āpaṇa) for "you" when addressing reader
   - Respectful forms when quoting Jesus
   - Maintains dignity appropriate for spiritual instruction

6. **Modern Vocabulary Integration**
   - ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ (praśikṣaṇa) = training (modern educational term)
   - ଉତ୍ତରଦାୟିତ୍ୱ (uttaradāyitwa) = accountability (modern organizational term)
   - Bridges traditional spiritual and contemporary practical language

7. **Trinitarian Precision**
   - Clear distinction: ପିତା (Father), ପୁତ୍ର (Son), ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା (Holy Spirit)
   - Maintains orthodox Christian theology while using culturally familiar terms

8. **Multiplication Language**
   - ବହୁଗୁଣିତ (baguguṇita) = multiplied (mathematical term)
   - ପିଢ଼ି (piḍhi) = generation
   - Emphasizes reproductive discipleship, not just addition

## XIX. Coaching Notes for Translators

### When Translating Similar Materials into Odia:

1. **Maintain Core Terminology**
   - Use established terms documented in this guide for consistency across Christian materials
   - ଈଶ୍ବର (Ishwara) for God, ଶିଷ୍ୟ (Śiṣya) for disciple, etc.

2. **Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Be aware that many terms (ଆତ୍ମା, ଈଶ୍ବର, ମୁକ୍ତି) carry Hindu theological baggage
   - Contextualize through surrounding phrases and biblical narrative
   - Don't avoid these terms—redeem them through Christian usage

3. **Guru-Disciple Framework**
   - Leverage the familiar ଶିଷ୍ୟ (śiṣya) concept
   - Emphasize relational learning, not just information transfer
   - "Training" (ପ୍ରଶିକ୍ଷଣ) + "discipleship" (ଶିଷ୍ୟତ୍ୱ) = holistic formation

4. **Active Voice Preference**
   - Odia prefers active constructions
   - Use infinitives and gerunds liberally
   - Example: "to disciple" = ଶିଷ୍ୟ କରିବା (śiṣya karibā)

5. **Compound Formation**
   - Odia readily forms compounds for new concepts
   - Follow Sanskrit sandhi rules for euphonic combinations
   - Example: ପବିତ୍ର + ଆତ୍ମା = ପବିତ୍ରଆତ୍ମା (Holy + Spirit = Holy Spirit)

6. **Register Appropriateness**
   - Use Standard Odia (not colloquial) for written materials
   - Formal ଆପଣ (āpaṇa) not informal ତୁମେ (tume)
   - Maintain dignified tone appropriate for spiritual content

7. **Scripture Citation Style**
   - Follow standard Odia Bible translation for direct quotes
   - The Odia Bible typically uses similar Sanskrit-based vocabulary
   - Consistency with established Odia Bible builds credibility

8. **Avoid Over-Sanskritization**
   - While Sanskrit loanwords are appropriate for spiritual concepts, don't make text inaccessible
   - Balance ত্సম (tatsama = Sanskrit loans) with তদ্ভব (tadbhava = Odia derivatives)
   - Use simple, clear language for instructions and explanations

9. **Gender Considerations**
   - Odia has grammatical gender (masculine, feminine, neuter)
   - Use inclusive language where possible
   - ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି (byakti = person) is gender-neutral
   - Be careful with pronouns: ସେ (se) is gender-neutral "he/she"

10. **Testing and Feedback**
    - Test translations with native Odia speakers from Christian background
    - Also test with Hindu-background readers for accessibility
    - Especially important in Odisha's multi-religious context
