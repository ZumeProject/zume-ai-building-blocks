## Core Deity Terms

### Jesus (यीशु)
**Devanagari:** यीशु
**Transliteration:** Yīśu
**Etymology:** Borrowed from English/Greek through Christian missionary influence

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Jesus is our measurement"**
   - यीशु हमारा माप है।
   - *Yīśu hamārā māp hai*

2. **"How to Follow Jesus"**
   - यीशु का अनुसरण कैसे करें
   - *Yīśu kā anusaraṇ kaise kareṁ*

3. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - एक यीशु का चेला (शिष्य) कौन होता है?
   - *Ek Yīśu kā chelā (śiṣya) kaun hotā hai?*

4. **"Learn how to obey Jesus' commands"**
   - यीशु की आज्ञाओं का पालन करें
   - *Yīśu kī ājñāoṁ kā pālan kareṁ*

5. **"Becoming more like Jesus... Together"**
   - यीशु की तरह बनना... एक साथ मिलकर
   - *Yīśu kī tarah bannā... ek sāth milakar*

**Variations:**
- यीशु के अनुयायी (*Yīśu ke anuyāyī*) - follower of Jesus
- यीशु का चेला (*Yīśu kā chelā*) - disciple of Jesus
- यीशु के पीछे चलना (*Yīśu ke pīche chalnā*) - to follow Jesus (lit. "walk behind Jesus")

**Notes:** The name यीशु is universally used across Hindi Christian contexts. It's a transliteration rather than a translation, maintaining phonetic similarity to the original.

### Christ / Messiah (मसीह)
**Devanagari:** मसीह
**Transliteration:** Masīh
**Etymology:** From Arabic/Hebrew مسيح (Masīḥ), meaning "anointed one"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"united with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection"**
   - मसीह की मृत्यु, दफन और पुनरुत्थान के साथ एकजुट
   - *Masīh kī mṛtyu, dafan aur punarutthān ke sāth ekjuṭ*

**Notes:** मसीह is preferred over other possible translations because it carries the theological weight of "anointed one" and is recognized by both Christian and Muslim Hindi speakers. The term is borrowed from Urdu/Arabic, showing the Perso-Arabic influence in Hindi religious vocabulary.

### God (परमेश्वर)
**Devanagari:** परमेश्वर
**Transliteration:** Parameśvar
**Etymology:** Sanskrit compound: परम (param = supreme) + ईश्वर (īśvar = lord/god)
**Literal Meaning:** "Supreme Lord" or "Highest God"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"God Uses Ordinary People"**
   - परमेश्वर आम लोगों को इस्तेमाल करता है
   - *Parameśvar ām logoṁ ko istemāl kartā hai*

2. **"Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - परमेश्वर की सुनना और उसका आज्ञा पालन करना
   - *Parameśvar kī sunnā aur uskā ājñā pālan karnā*

3. **"What do you learn about God?"**
   - परमेश्वर के बारे में आप क्या सीखते हैं?
   - *Parameśvar ke bāre meṁ āp kyā sīkhte haiṁ?*

4. **"God's Kingdom"**
   - परमेश्वर का राज्य
   - *Parameśvar kā rājya*

5. **"Sharing God's Story"**
   - परमेश्वर की कहानी साझा करना
   - *Parameśvar kī kahānī sājhā karnā*

**Phrases:**
- परमेश्वर का परिवार (*Parameśvar kā parivār*) - God's Family
- परमेश्वर की वाणी (*Parameśvar kī vāṇī*) - God's voice
- परमेश्वर की योजनाएँ (*Parameśvar kī yojanāeṁ*) - God's plans

**Cultural Notes:** परमेश्वर is the most common term for the Christian God in Hindi, chosen because it emphasizes the supreme, transcendent nature of God. It's a Sanskrit (tatsama) word, giving it a formal, elevated register suitable for religious contexts. Alternative terms exist (जीसस का पिता, खुदा, ईश्वर) but परमेश्वर is standard in Hindi Christian literature.

### Holy Spirit (पवित्र आत्मा)
**Devanagari:** पवित्र आत्मा
**Transliteration:** Pavitra Ātmā
**Etymology:** पवित्र (pavitra = holy, pure, Sanskrit) + आत्मा (ātmā = spirit/soul, Sanskrit)
**Literal Meaning:** "Holy Spirit" or "Pure Soul"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together"**
   - परमेश्वर के पवित्र आत्मा से आपके साथ मिलकर समय का नेतृत्व करने के लिए कहें
   - *Parameśvar ke pavitra ātmā se āpke sāth milakar samay kā netṛtva karne ke lie kaheṁ*

2. **"Invite His Holy Spirit to lead"**
   - उसके पवित्र आत्मा को आमंत्रित करें आपके समय की मिलजुलकर अगुवाई करने के लिए
   - *Uske pavitra ātmā ko āmanṭrit kareṁ āpke samay kī miljulakar aguvāī karne ke lie*

3. **"The Holy Spirit comes upon you"**
   - पवित्र आत्मा आप पर आता है
   - *Pavitra ātmā āp par ātā hai*

**Related Terms:**
- आत्मा (*ātmā*) - spirit (general)
- आत्मिक (*ātmik*) - spiritual (adjective)
- आध्यात्मिक (*ādhyātmik*) - spiritual (more philosophical/metaphysical)

**Notes:** The term पवित्र आत्मा is a direct Sanskrit calque of "Holy Spirit." आत्मा in Hindu philosophy refers to the eternal soul/self, so this terminology creates a bridge to Hindu religious concepts while maintaining Christian distinctiveness through the adjective पवित्र (holy/pure).

### Spirit / Spiritual (आत्मा / आत्मिक / आध्यात्मिक)

**Spirit (noun):** आत्मा (*ātmā*)

**Spiritual (adjective):** आत्मिक (*ātmik*) or आध्यात्मिक (*ādhyātmik*)

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Spiritual Breathing"**
   - आध्यात्मिक श्वास (आत्मिक सांस)
   - *Ādhyātmik śvās (ātmik sāns)*
   - Note: Both forms shown, with आध्यात्मिक preferred

2. **"Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - आत्मिक साँस लेना परमेश्वर की सुनना और आज्ञा पालन करना है
   - *Ātmik sāns lenā Parameśvar kī sunnā aur ājñā pālan karnā hai*

3. **"Spiritual Economy"**
   - आत्मिक अर्थव्यवस्था
   - *Ātmik arthavyavasthā*

4. **"Keys to Spiritual Growth"**
   - आध्यात्मिक विकास की कुंजियाँ
   - *Ādhyātmik vikās kī kuñjiyāṁ*

5. **"Spiritual Practices"**
   - आध्यात्मिक अभ्यास
   - *Ādhyātmik abhyās*

**Distinction:**
- **आत्मिक** (*ātmik*) - Used for Christian spiritual contexts, more direct and relational
- **आध्यात्मिक** (*ādhyātmik*) - More philosophical/metaphysical, used in both Hindu and Christian contexts

**Notes:** The dual usage reflects a tension in Hindi Christian vocabulary between creating distinctive Christian terminology (आत्मिक) and using broadly recognized spiritual vocabulary (आध्यात्मिक) that resonates with Hindu concepts.

### Lord (प्रभु)
**Devanagari:** प्रभु
**Transliteration:** Prabhu
**Etymology:** Sanskrit, meaning "master," "lord," "sovereign"
**Literal Meaning:** Lord, Master, Supreme Being

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"The Lord's Supper"**
   - प्रभु भोज
   - *Prabhu bhoj*

2. **"The Lord's Supper and How To Lead It"**
   - प्रभु भोज और इसकी अगुवाई कैसे की जाएं
   - *Prabhu bhoj aur iskī aguvāī kaise kī jāeṁ*

3. **"Celebrate the Lord's Supper"**
   - प्रभु भोज लें
   - *Prabhu bhoj leṁ*

**Notes:** प्रभु is a highly respected Sanskrit term commonly used in Hindu contexts for deities (especially Vishnu, Rama, Krishna), making it culturally resonant. In Christian Hindi, it typically refers to Jesus Christ as Lord. The term conveys authority, reverence, and divine sovereignty.

### Father (पिता)
**Devanagari:** पिता
**Transliteration:** Pitā
**Etymology:** Sanskrit, meaning "father"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"**
   - पिता और पुत्र और पवित्र आत्मा के नाम से
   - *Pitā aur putra aur pavitra ātmā ke nām se*

2. **"God the Father" (contextual)**
   - परमेश्वर पिता
   - *Parameśvar pitā*

**Notes:** पिता is the standard Hindi word for "father" and is used both in familial and theological contexts. In Christian usage, it refers to God the Father, the first person of the Trinity.

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple (शिष्य / चेला)
**Primary:** शिष्य (*śiṣya*)
**Secondary:** चेला (*chelā*)
**Transliteration:** Śiṣya / Chelā
**Etymology:**
- शिष्य - Sanskrit, formal term for student/disciple
- चेला - Hindi/Prakrit, colloquial term for disciple/follower

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"**
   - शिष्य और कलीसिया की एक साधारण परिभाषा
   - *Śiṣya aur kalīsiyā kī ek sādhāraṇ paribhāṣā*

2. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - एक यीशु का चेला (शिष्य) कौन होता है?
   - *Ek Yīśu kā chelā (śiṣya) kaun hotā hai?*
   - Note: Both terms used together for clarity

3. **"Being A Disciple"**
   - शिष्य बनना
   - *Śiṣya bannā*

4. **"What's a Disciple?"**
   - शिष्य क्या है?
   - *Śiṣya kyā hai?*

5. **"1 trained multiplying disciple per 5,000"**
   - प्रति 5,000 पर 1 प्रशिक्षित गुणन शिष्य
   - *Prati 5,000 par 1 praśikṣit guṇan śiṣya*

**Plural Forms:**
- शिष्यों (*śiṣyoṁ*) - disciples (formal)
- चेलों (*cheloṁ*) - disciples (colloquial)

**Related Phrases:**
- शिष्य बनाना (*śiṣya banānā*) - to make disciples
- शिष्यों के निर्माण (*śiṣyoṁ ke nirmāṇ*) - making/formation of disciples
- परिपक्व शिष्य (*paripakva śiṣya*) - mature disciple
- गुणात्मक वृद्धि करने वाले शिष्य (*guṇātmak vṛddhi karne vāle śiṣya*) - multiplying disciples

**Cultural Notes:** शिष्य is the preferred formal term, rooted in the guru-śiṣya (teacher-student) tradition of Hinduism and Buddhism. This makes it highly culturally resonant. चेला is more colloquial and commonly used in North India. The dual usage shows sensitivity to both formal theological discourse and everyday speech.

### Discipleship (शिष्यत्व)
**Devanagari:** शिष्यत्व
**Transliteration:** Śiṣyatva
**Etymology:** शिष्य (śiṣya = disciple) + त्व (tva = abstract noun suffix, "ness")
**Literal Meaning:** "Disciple-hood" or "state of being a disciple"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately"**
   - डकलिंग (बत्तख के चूजे) शिष्यत्व - तुरंत अगुवाई करना
   - *Ḍakliṅg (battakh ke chūje) śiṣyatva - turant aguvāī karnā*

2. **"Discipleship Concepts"**
   - शिष्यत्व अवधारणाएँ
   - *Śiṣyatva avdhāraṇāeṁ*

**Related Terms:**
- शिष्य बनाना (*śiṣya banānā*) - disciple-making (verbal form)
- चेलापन (*chelāpan*) - discipleship (colloquial)

**Notes:** The suffix -त्व creates an abstract noun, similar to English "-ness" or "-ship." This is a formal, literary construction.

### Follower (अनुयायी / अनुगामी)
**Primary:** अनुयायी (*anuyāyī*)
**Transliteration:** Anuyāyī
**Etymology:** अनु (anu = after, following) + या (yā = to go) + ई (ī = agent suffix)
**Literal Meaning:** "One who goes after" or "one who follows"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"What is a follower of Jesus?"**
   - एक यीशु का चेला (शिष्य) कौन होता है?
   - *Ek Yīśu kā chelā (śiṣya) kaun hotā hai?*
   - Note: Translated as चेला/शिष्य rather than अनुयायी

2. **"What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?"**
   - यीशु के अनुयायी की गतिविधियाँ क्या हैं?
   - *Yīśu ke anuyāyī kī gatividhiyāṁ kyā haiṁ?*

3. **"You are a follower (disciple) of Jesus"**
   - यीशु के अनुयायी (शिष्य) होने का क्या अर्थ है
   - *Yīśu ke anuyāyī (śiṣya) hone kā kyā arth hai*

**Notes:** अनुयायी is more neutral/political (like "follower of a leader"), while शिष्य/चेला carries deeper spiritual commitment. In the translations, शिष्य is often preferred for "follower of Jesus" to emphasize the discipleship relationship.

**Devanagari:** विश्वासी
**Transliteration:** Viśvāsī
**Etymology:** विश्वास (viśvās = faith, belief) + ई (ī = agent suffix)
**Literal Meaning:** "One who believes" or "believer"

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Share the Training Cycle with a believer on your list"**

**Notes:** विश्वासी is a straightforward construction meaning "one who has faith/belief." It's used less frequently than शिष्य in these materials.

### Obey / Obedience (आज्ञा पालन / आज्ञाकारी)
**Obey (verb):** आज्ञा पालन करना (*ājñā pālan karnā*)
**Obedience (noun):** आज्ञापालन (*ājñāpālan*) or आज्ञाकारिता (*ājñākāritā*)
**Obedient (adjective):** आज्ञाकारी (*ājñākārī*)
**Obeyed (past participle):** आज्ञा पालन किया (*ājñā pālan kiyā*)

**Etymology:** आज्ञा (*ājñā*) = command, order (Sanskrit) + पालन (*pālan*) = observance, following

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - आत्मिक साँस लेना परमेश्वर की सुनना और आज्ञा पालन करना है
   - *Ātmik sāns lenā Parameśvar kī sunnā aur ājñā pālan karnā hai*

2. **"Hearing and Obeying God"**
   - परमेश्वर की सुनना और उसका आज्ञा पालन करना
   - *Parameśvar kī sunnā aur uskā ājñā pālan karnā*

3. **"Learn how to obey Jesus' commands"**
   - यीशु की आज्ञाओं का पालन करें
   - *Yīśu kī ājñāoṁ kā pālan kareṁ*

4. **"Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus"**
   - दूसरों को आज्ञा पालन करना और आपके साथ यीशु के पीछे चलना सिखाएं
   - *Dūsroṁ ko ājñā pālan karnā aur āpke sāth Yīśu ke pīche chalnā sikhāeṁ*

5. **"The Power of Obedient Faith"**
   - आज्ञाकारी विश्वास का सामर्थ्य
   - *Ājñākārī viśvās kā sāmarthya*

6. **"Self-evaluate using Heard, Obeyed, Shared, Trained"**
   - सुना, आज्ञा पालन किया, साझा किया, प्रशिक्षित किया
   - *Sunā, ājñā pālan kiyā, sājhā kiyā, praśikṣit kiyā*

**Command Forms:**
- आज्ञा पालन करें (*ājñā pālan kareṁ*) - Obey! (polite imperative)
- आज्ञा (*ājñā*) - command/commandment

**Related Terms:**
- आज्ञाएं (*ājñāeṁ*) - commandments (plural)
- आज्ञाओं (*ājñāoṁ*) - of the commandments (oblique plural)

**Notes:** This is a compound construction that literally means "command-observance" or "following orders." It has strong resonance in Indian culture where obedience to elders and authorities is highly valued.

### Follow / Following (अनुसरण / पीछे चलना)
**Follow (verb):** अनुसरण करना (*anusaraṇ karnā*) or पीछे चलना (*pīche chalnā*)
**Following (noun/gerund):** अनुसरण (*anusaraṇ*)
**Transliteration:** Anusaraṇ karnā / Pīche chalnā

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"How to Follow Jesus"**
   - यीशु का अनुसरण कैसे करें
   - *Yīśu kā anusaraṇ kaise kareṁ*

2. **"Following Jesus" (idiomatic)**
   - यीशु के पीछे चलना
   - *Yīśu ke pīche chalnā*
   - Literal: "walking behind Jesus"

3. **"A decision to follow Jesus"**
   - यीशु का अनुसरण करने का निर्णय
   - *Yīśu kā anusaraṇ karne kā nirṇay*

**Notes:** Two expressions are used:
- **अनुसरण करना** - More formal, literary Sanskrit term
- **पीछे चलना** - Idiomatic Hindi, literally "to walk behind," very vivid imagery of discipleship

## Church Terms

### Church (चर्च / कलीसिया)
**Primary:** चर्च (*carch*)
**Secondary:** कलीसिया (*kalīsiyā*)
**Transliteration:** Carch / Kalīsiyā
**Etymology:**
- चर्च - From English "church"
- कलीसिया - From Greek ἐκκλησία (ekklēsia) via Portuguese/Latin

**Contextual Examples:**

1. **"Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"**
   - शिष्य और कलीसिया की एक साधारण परिभाषा
   - *Śiṣya aur kalīsiyā kī ek sādhāraṇ paribhāṣā*
   - Note: Uses कलीसिया in formal definition

2. **"Disciples and the Church"**
   - शिष्य और चर्च
   - *Śiṣya aur carch*
   - Note: Uses चर्च in general usage

3. **"What's a Church?"**
   - चर्च क्या है?
   - *Carch kyā hai?*

4. **"Leading a Simple Church"**
   - एक साधारण चर्च का नेतृत्व करना
   - *Ek sādhāraṇ carch kā netṛtva karnā*

5. **"2 simple churches per 5,000 people"**
   - प्रति 5,000 लोगों पर 2 साधारण चर्च
   - *Prati 5,000 logoṁ par 2 sādhāraṇ carch*

6. **"Always Part of Two Churches"**
   - सदैव दो कलीसियाओं का हिस्सा
   - *Sadaiv do kalīsiyāoṁ kā hissā*
   - Note: Uses कलीसिया for theological concept

**Plural Forms:**
- चर्च (*carch*) - churches (treated as collective)
- चर्चों (*carchoṁ*) - churches (oblique plural)
- कलीसियाएं (*kalīsiyāeṁ*) - churches (plural)
- कलीसियाओं (*kalīsiyāoṁ*) - churches (oblique plural)

**Related Phrases:**
- साधारण चर्च (*sādhāraṇ carch*) - simple church
- सरल चर्च (*saral carch*) - simple church (alternate)
- मेरे चर्च (*mere carch*) - my churches
- चर्च का नाम (*carch kā nām*) - church name
- मूल चर्च (*mūl carch*) - parent church

**Cultural Notes:** The dual vocabulary reflects translation history:
- **चर्च** is the most common term in everyday Hindi, borrowed directly from English
- **कलीसिया** is more theological/biblical, used in formal religious contexts and Bible translations
Both terms are used interchangeably in the materials, with चर्च slightly more common.

### Simple Church (साधारण चर्च / सरल चर्च)
**Primary:** साधारण चर्च (*sādhāraṇ carch*)
**Alternate:** सरल चर्च (*saral carch*)

## Linguistic and Cultural Notes

### Indo-Aryan Language Patterns

Hindi is an Indo-Aryan language descended from Sanskrit through Prakrit and Apabhramsha stages. Key patterns visible in these translations:

1. **Postpositions**: Hindi uses postpositions rather than prepositions
   - "In the name of" → के नाम से (*ke nām se*)
   - "With your group" → अपने समूह के साथ (*apne samūh ke sāth*)

2. **SOV Word Order**: Subject-Object-Verb, unlike English SVO
   - "Jesus loves us" → यीशु हमसे प्रेम करता है (*Yīśu hamse prem kartā hai*, lit. "Jesus us-from love does")

3. **Agglutinative Verbal System**: Complex verb forms built with auxiliaries
   - "Will be baptizing" → बपतिस्मा देते रहेंगे (*baptismā dete raheṅge*)

4. **Gender Agreement**: Verbs, adjectives, and some postpositions agree with gender
   - Masculine: शिष्य बनता है (*śiṣya bantā hai* - disciple becomes)
   - Feminine: शिष्या बनती है (*śiṣyā bantī hai* - female disciple becomes)

### Hindu Cultural Context

**Terms Bridging Hindu and Christian Contexts:**

1. **गुरु-शिष्य परम्परा (Guru-Disciple Tradition)**

   - सेवा (service/worship) fundamental to Hindu bhakti tradition
   - प्रेम (spiritual love) used in Bhakti movement and Christianity

   - वचन (word/promise) used in both traditions

   - परमेश्वर (Supreme God) emphasizes monotheism

**Strategy:** The translations create **continuity with Hindu spiritual vocabulary** while establishing Christian distinctives through specific combinations (e.g., पवित्र आत्मा for Holy Spirit, not just आत्मा).

### Devanagari Script Usage

**Devanagari Characteristics in Translation:**

1. **Phonetic Accuracy**: Devanagari allows precise representation of Sanskrit sounds
   - प्र (pra) vs. प (pa) - aspiration differences
   - Vowel marks: ा ि ी ु ू े ै ो ौ

2. **Consonant Clusters**: Sanskrit words maintain clusters
   - प्रशिक्षण (praśikṣaṇ) - training
   - स्थान (sthān) - place

3. **Nukta for Borrowed Sounds**: Dot below for non-Sanskrit sounds
   - क़ (qa) vs क (ka) - not heavily used in these translations

4. **Halant (्) for Consonant Ending**: Rarely appears in modern Hindi
   - Usually adds inherent 'a' sound

**Transliteration Note:** These materials would likely use Roman script for English borrowings in practice (Church, Training, Coach), though Devanagari forms exist.

### Honorific and Politeness Systems

Hindi has complex honorific systems shown in these translations:

**Verb Forms (by respect level):**

1. **Intimate** (तू - tū): Not used in materials

**Pattern:** The materials consistently use **respectful forms**, creating a tone of invitation rather than command, appropriate for adult learners and spiritual formation.
