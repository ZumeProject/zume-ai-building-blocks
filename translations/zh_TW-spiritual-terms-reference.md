# Traditional Chinese - Taiwan (zh_TW) Spiritual Terms Translation Reference Guide

## Overview

This guide documents how key spiritual and ministry terms are translated from English to Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) in the Zume training materials. It serves as a reference for translators and coaches to maintain consistency and understand the theological and cultural choices made in the Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) translation.

**Language:** Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)
**Region:** Taiwan (台灣)
**Language Code:** zh_TW
**Script:** Traditional Chinese characters (繁體字)
**Source File:** zume-zh_TW.po

---

## Translation Philosophy

The Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) translation demonstrates a consistent approach that:

1. **Uses Traditional Chinese characters** - Taiwan uses traditional character forms (繁體字) rather than simplified characters, reflecting the cultural and educational heritage of the region
2. **Employs standard Protestant Christian terminology** - The translation uses well-established terms from Taiwan's Presbyterian missionary tradition dating back to the 19th century
3. **Balances formal and accessible language** - Uses respectful register appropriate for teaching materials while remaining approachable for everyday believers
4. **Maintains theological precision** - Core theological concepts use established Chinese Christian vocabulary recognized across denominations
5. **Reflects local Christian culture** - Taiwan's Christian terminology has been heavily influenced by Presbyterian missionaries and the Union Version Bible translation
6. **Considers Buddhist/Taoist cultural overlay** - Term choices are sensitive to Taiwan's multi-religious context where Buddhist and Taoist vocabulary can overlap with Christian concepts

## Core Deity Terms

### God

**Traditional Chinese:** 神 (Shén) / 上帝 (Shàngdì)

**Pinyin:** Shén / Shàng-dì

**Literal Meaning:**
- 神 = "spirit, deity, divine being"
- 上帝 = "supreme emperor, sovereign Lord" (上 = above/supreme, 帝 = emperor/sovereign)

**Register:** Formal/respectful

**Cultural Context:**
- Both 神 and 上帝 appear in the translation, showing flexibility in Taiwan's Christian usage
- 上帝 was favored by early Protestant missionaries as it avoided Buddhist/Taoist associations of 神
- Modern Taiwan Christians comfortably use both terms
- The Union Version Bible (和合本) uses both terms contextually
- 上帝 carries more monotheistic specificity, while 神 is more general

**Notes:**
- 神 is used more frequently in compounds and phrases
- 上帝 appears in more formal theological contexts
- Both terms command respect and are capitalized in theological writing
- Unlike Korean, Chinese doesn't add honorific suffixes to deity names

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| God Uses Ordinary People | 神使用平凡人 | Shén shǐyòng píngfán rén | Title - God as active agent |
| Hearing and Obeying God | 聆聽並順服神 | Língtīng bìng shùnfú Shén | Spiritual discipline |
| What do you learn about God? | 你對神又有什麼新的認識？ | Nǐ duì Shén yòu yǒu shénme xīn de rènshi? | Question form |
| God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact | 上帝使用普通人做簡單的事情來產生巨大的影響 | Shàngdì shǐyòng pǔtōng rén zuò jiǎndān de shìqíng lái chǎnshēng jùdà de yǐngxiǎng | Full sentence |
| Sharing God's Story | 分享神的故事 | Fēnxiǎng Shén de gùshi | Evangelism |
| God's Kingdom | 神的國度 / 神國 | Shén de guódù / Shénguó | Kingdom concept |
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together | 祈求上帝的聖靈帶領你們共度時光 | Qíqiú Shàngdì de Shènglíng dàilǐng nǐmen gòngdù shíguāng | Prayer invocation |
| God makes plans | 神制定計劃 | Shén zhìdìng jìhuà | God's sovereignty |
| Thank God that He loves the lost | 感謝神，祂愛那些迷失的人 | Gǎnxiè Shén, Tā ài nàxiē míshī de rén | Thanksgiving |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Possessive form: 神的 (Shén de) = "God's" / "of God"
- 神 and 上帝 are interchangeable in most contexts in Taiwan usage
- Often paired with 祂 (Tā) - honorific pronoun for deity (distinct from 他 for humans)

### Jesus

**Traditional Chinese:** 耶穌 (Yēsū)

**Pinyin:** Yē-sū

**Literal Meaning:** Phonetic transliteration of "Jesus" (no inherent meaning in Chinese characters)

**Register:** Formal/standard

**Cultural Context:**
- Standard transliteration established by 19th-century missionaries
- Universally recognized across all Chinese-speaking Christian communities
- The same transliteration is used in Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Singapore
- No honorific suffix is added in Chinese (unlike Korean 예수님)

**Notes:**
- Sometimes appears in compound form 耶穌基督 (Yēsū Jīdū = Jesus Christ)
- The name alone carries inherent respect in Christian contexts
- Used consistently without variation

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| How to Follow Jesus | 如何跟隨耶穌 | Rúhé gēnsuí Yēsū | Discipleship title |
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」是什麼？ | "Yēsū de gēnsuí zhě" shì shénme? | Definition question |
| Learn how to obey Jesus' commands | 透過去和留來學習如何遵守耶穌的命令 | Tòuguò qù hé liú lái xuéxí rúhé zūnshǒu Yēsū de mìnglìng | Obedience teaching |
| Speaking to people YOU KNOW about Jesus | 和「你認識的人」談論耶穌 | Hé "nǐ rènshi de rén" tánlùn Yēsū | Evangelism context |
| Teaching others to obey and follow Jesus with you | 教導別人和你一起順服耶穌並跟隨他 | Jiàodǎo biérén hé nǐ yīqǐ shùnfú Yēsū bìng gēnsuí tā | Disciple-making |
| Becoming more like Jesus... Together | 我們一起越來越像耶穌 | Wǒmen yīqǐ yuè lái yuè xiàng Yēsū | Spiritual transformation |
| How Jesus has impacted your life | 耶穌如何改變了你的生命 | Yēsū rúhé gǎibiàn le nǐ de shēngmìng | Testimony |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Possessive: 耶穌的 (Yēsū de) = "Jesus'" or "of Jesus"
- Often followed by object markers in verb phrases
- Can be used with or without possessive particle depending on context

### Holy Spirit

**Traditional Chinese:** 聖靈 (Shènglíng)

**Pinyin:** Shèng-líng

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy Spirit" (聖 = holy/sacred, 靈 = spirit/spiritual entity)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Cultural Context:**
- Standard term across all Chinese Christian traditions
- 聖 (holy) is also used in Buddhist contexts, but the compound 聖靈 is distinctly Christian
- The term 靈 connects to traditional Chinese concepts of spiritual beings but is contextualized in Christian theology
- Universally understood across denominations

**Notes:**
- Sometimes appears as 上帝的聖靈 (Shàngdì de Shènglíng = "God's Holy Spirit")
- Also appears in longer form: 聖父、聖子、聖靈 (Holy Father, Holy Son, Holy Spirit - Trinity)
- No honorific suffix added in Chinese

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together | 祈求上帝的聖靈帶領你們共度時光 | Qíqiú Shàngdì de Shènglíng dàilǐng nǐmen gòngdù shíguāng | Prayer for guidance |
| In the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit | 奉聖父、聖子、聖靈的名 | Fèng Shèngfù, Shèngzǐ, Shènglíng de míng | Baptismal formula |
| Invite His Holy Spirit to lead | 邀請祂的聖靈帶領 | Yāoqǐng Tā de Shènglíng dàilǐng | Invocation |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Often paired with verbs of leading, guiding, empowering
- Used with honorific pronoun 祂 when referring to the Spirit's actions
- Can be used as subject or object in sentences

### Lord

**Traditional Chinese:** 主 (Zhǔ)

**Pinyin:** Zhǔ

**Literal Meaning:** "Lord, master, owner, sovereign"

**Register:** Formal/respectful

**Cultural Context:**
- Ancient Chinese term for sovereign ruler, now contextualized for Christian use
- Used both independently and in compounds
- Can refer to God the Father or Jesus Christ depending on context
- Common in traditional Chinese culture (feudal lord, master) but given Christian meaning

**Notes:**
- Often appears in the phrase 跟隨主 (gēnsuí Zhǔ = "follow the Lord")
- Used in theological compounds like 主的聖餐 (the Lord's Supper)
- Sometimes used interchangeably with 神 or 耶穌 depending on context

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Follow the Lord | 跟隨主 | Gēnsuí Zhǔ | Discipleship |
| The Lord's Supper | 主的聖餐 | Zhǔ de shèngcān | Communion |
| Obey the Lord's commands | 遵守主的命令 | Zūnshǒu Zhǔ de mìnglìng | Obedience |

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple

**Traditional Chinese:** 門徒 (Méntú)

**Pinyin:** Mén-tú

**Literal Meaning:** "Gate/door follower" or "student of the way" (門 = gate/door/school/discipline, 徒 = follower/student)

**Register:** Formal/classical

**Cultural Context:**
- Ancient Chinese term predating Christianity, used for students of Confucius and other masters
- Carries strong cultural resonance of master-apprentice relationship
- The character 門 (gate) implies entering into a particular school or tradition
- Perfectly suited for Christian discipleship concept due to pre-existing cultural framework
- Used in both secular (martial arts, traditional crafts) and religious contexts

**Notes:**
- Very stable and well-established term with deep cultural roots
- Same term used across all Chinese translations of the Bible
- Implies not just learning but also life commitment and character formation
- The term naturally conveys the holistic relationship between Jesus and His followers

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Disciple | 門徒 | Méntú | Basic term |
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 門徒與教會的簡單定義 | Méntú yǔ jiàohuì de jiǎndān dìngyì | Teaching title |
| Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God | 作為跟隨主的門徒就是要聆聽神的話且遵行神的吩咐 | Zuòwéi gēnsuí Zhǔ de méntú jiùshì yào língtīng Shén de huà qiě zūnxíng Shén de fēnfù | Definition |
| Train disciples how to make disciples | 以20小時的課程內容訓練門徒如何使他們成為門徒 | Yǐ 20 xiǎoshí de kèchéng nèiróng xùnliàn méntú rúhé shǐ tāmen chéngwéi méntú | Training purpose |
| Disciple training | 門徒訓練 | Méntú xùnliàn | Training concept |
| I want to make disciples who multiply | 我想要帶出有倍增力的門徒 | Wǒ xiǎng yào dài chū yǒu bèizēng lì de méntú | Vision statement |

**Compound Forms:**
- 門徒訓練 (méntú xùnliàn) = disciple training
- 培養門徒 (péiyǎng méntú) = cultivating/raising disciples
- 帶領門徒 (dàilǐng méntú) = leading disciples
- 成為門徒 (chéngwéi méntú) = becoming a disciple

**Grammatical Notes:**
- Functions as a noun
- Often appears in possessive constructions (我的門徒 = "my disciple")
- Can be used as subject or object

### Disciple-Making / Making Disciples

**Traditional Chinese:** 使人成為門徒 (Shǐ rén chéngwéi méntú) / 帶出門徒 (Dài chū méntú)

**Pinyin:** Shǐ rén chéng-wéi mén-tú / Dài chū mén-tú

**Literal Meaning:**
- 使人成為門徒 = "cause people to become disciples"
- 帶出門徒 = "bring forth disciples" / "raise up disciples"

**Register:** Active/practical

**Cultural Context:**
- Taiwan Christians commonly use more dynamic, active phrases for disciple-making
- 帶出 (dài chū) is particularly vivid, implying leading someone along a path until they emerge as something new
- Reflects the relational, process-oriented nature of Asian discipleship

**Notes:**
- The Great Commission phrase "make disciples" can be translated several ways
- 使...成為 (shǐ...chéngwéi) is a causative construction
- 帶出 (dài chū) emphasizes the mentor's active role in development

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Make disciples | 使人成為門徒 | Shǐ rén chéngwéi méntú | Great Commission |
| Train disciples how to make disciples | 訓練門徒如何使他們成為門徒 | Xùnliàn méntú rúhé shǐ tāmen chéngwéi méntú | Training objective |
| I want to make disciples who multiply | 我想要帶出有倍增力的門徒 | Wǒ xiǎng yào dài chū yǒu bèizēng lì de méntú | Vision |

### Follower (of Jesus)

**Traditional Chinese:** 跟隨者 (Gēnsuí zhě)

**Pinyin:** Gēn-suí zhě

**Literal Meaning:** "One who follows" (跟隨 = to follow, 者 = person/one who)

**Register:** Formal/descriptive

**Notes:**
- More general term than 門徒 (disciple)
- 跟隨 (gēnsuí) means "to follow closely, to pursue"
- The suffix 者 (zhě) turns the verb into a noun indicating "one who does X"
- Often used to describe believers in a more accessible way

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| What is a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」是什麼？ | "Yēsū de gēnsuí zhě" shì shénme? | Definition question |
| What are the activities of a follower of Jesus? | 「耶穌的跟隨者」會做什麼？ | "Yēsū de gēnsuí zhě" huì zuò shénme? | Practical question |
| A group of Jesus followers | 一群耶穌的跟隨者 | Yī qún Yēsū de gēnsuí zhě | Group description |

## Church Terms

### Church

**Traditional Chinese:** 教會 (Jiàohuì)

**Pinyin:** Jiào-huì

**Literal Meaning:** "Teaching gathering" (教 = teach/religion, 會 = meeting/gathering/assembly)

**Register:** Formal/standard

**Cultural Context:**
- Standard Protestant term for church in all Chinese-speaking regions
- The compound emphasizes the church as a gathering for teaching and learning
- Catholics use the same term, though they may also use 聖會 (holy assembly) in some contexts
- Can refer to both the building and the congregation
- Same term used for both local churches and the universal Church

**Notes:**
- Very stable term established in the 19th century
- Used across all denominations
- Emphasizes the didactic and communal nature of the church
- The character 教 links to 教導 (teaching) and 教育 (education)

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |
|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|
| Church | 教會 | Jiàohuì | General term |
| Simple Definition of Disciple and Church | 門徒與教會的簡單定義 | Méntú yǔ jiàohuì de jiǎndān dìngyì | Teaching title |
| What is a church? | 教會是什麼？ | Jiàohuì shì shénme? | Definition |
| When you think of a church, what comes to mind? | 當你想到教會時，你會想到什麼？ | Dāng nǐ xiǎngdào jiàohuì shí, nǐ huì xiǎngdào shénme? | Reflection question |
| Leading a Simple Church | 帶領簡單教會 | Dàilǐng jiǎndān jiàohuì | House church context |

**Compound Forms:**
- 簡單教會 (jiǎndān jiàohuì) = simple church
- 家庭教會 (jiātíng jiàohuì) = house church / home church
- 微型教會 (wēixíng jiàohuì) = micro church

### Simple Church

**Traditional Chinese:** 簡單教會 (Jiǎndān jiàohuì) / 三合一小組 (Sān hé yī xiǎozú)

**Pinyin:** Jiǎn-dān jiào-huì / Sān hé yī xiǎo-zú

**Literal Meaning:**
- 簡單教會 = "simple church"
- 三合一小組 = "three-in-one small group" (referring to the 3/3 Group pattern)

**Register:** Descriptive/accessible

**Cultural Context:**
- 簡單教會 directly translates "simple church"
- 三合一小組 is uniquely used in Zume materials to describe the 3/3 Group pattern
- Both terms are used to describe informal, organic church expressions
- Taiwan has a strong house church movement, making these terms culturally relevant

**Notes:**
- 簡單 (jiǎndān) means simple, uncomplicated, straightforward
- 三合一 (sān hé yī) literally "three combined into one" - refers to the three-part structure
- 小組 (xiǎozú) means small group

**Examples:**

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel

**Traditional Chinese:** 福音 (Fúyīn)

**Pinyin:** Fú-yīn

**Literal Meaning:** "Good news" or "blessed sound/message" (福 = blessing/good fortune, 音 = sound/news/message)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Cultural Context:**
- Standard biblical term established in early Chinese Bible translations
- The character 福 is deeply rooted in Chinese culture (seen in New Year decorations, etc.)
- Combining 福 (blessing) with 音 (sound/message) creates a uniquely Christian term
- Well-established with over 150 years of usage in Chinese Christianity
- Same term used across all Chinese Bible translations

**Notes:**
- Universally understood across Chinese-speaking Christian communities
- Can refer to both the gospel message and the Gospel books (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John)
- The term carries positive cultural connotations even for non-Christians

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |

| Share the Gospel | 分享福音 | Fēnxiǎng fúyīn | Evangelism action |
| Take turns telling the Gospel to each other | 輪流互相傳講福音 | Lúnliú hùxiāng chuánjiǎng fúyīn | Practice activity |
| Sharing the Gospel | 分享福音 | Fēnxiǎng fúyīn | Action noun |
| Practice Sharing the Gospel | 練習分享福音 | Liànxí fēnxiǎng fúyīn | Training exercise |
| 3-Circles Gospel Presentation | 三圈福音演講 / 3 圈福音簡報 | Sān quān fúyīn yǎnjiǎng / 3 quān fúyīn jiǎnbào | Method name |
| God's Story (from creation to judgment) | 神的故事 / 神的救恩故事 | Shén de gùshi / Shén de jiù'ēn gùshi | Gospel narrative |

**Grammatical Notes:**
- 分享福音 (fēnxiǎng fúyīn) = share the gospel
- 傳講福音 (chuánjiǎng fúyīn) = proclaim the gospel
- 傳福音 (chuán fúyīn) = evangelize / spread the gospel

### Bible / Scripture

**Traditional Chinese:** 聖經 (Shèngjīng)

**Pinyin:** Shèng-jīng

**Literal Meaning:** "Holy scriptures/classic" (聖 = holy/sacred, 經 = classic text/scripture/sutra)

**Register:** Formal/theological

**Cultural Context:**
- Universal term for the Bible in Chinese
- The character 經 (jīng) is also used for Buddhist sutras and Confucian classics
- The prefix 聖 (holy) distinguishes Christian scripture from other sacred texts
- Very stable and well-established term
- Used identically across Taiwan, Mainland China, Hong Kong, Singapore

**Notes:**
- Can refer to the entire Bible or to specific scripture passages
- Used across all denominations without variation
- The compound emphasizes both the sacred nature and textual authority of scripture

**Examples:**

|---------|---------------------|--------|---------|

| Scripture | 查詢聖經 | Cháxún shèngjīng | Scripture reading |
| Bible Reading | 讀聖經 | Dú shèngjīng | Regular practice |
| Personal Bible Study | 個人聖經學習 | Gèrén shèngjīng xuéxí | Individual study |
| A tool for daily Bible reading | 日常學習聖經的工具 | Rìcháng xuéxí shèngjīng de gōngjù | Study tool |

**Compound Forms:**
- 讀經 (dú jīng) = Bible reading (shortened form)
- 查經 (chá jīng) = Bible study (examining scripture)
- 聖經學習 (shèngjīng xuéxí) = Bible learning/study
- 讀經法 (dú jīng fǎ) = Bible reading method

**Notes on S.O.A.P.S.:**
- Translated as 香皂讀經法 (xiāngzào dú jīng fǎ) = "Soap Bible Reading Method"
- 香皂 (xiāngzào) = fragrant soap (playful translation preserving the acronym concept)

### Word (of God)

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism

**Traditional Chinese:** 給人施洗 (Gěi rén shīxǐ) / 受洗 (Shòuxǐ) / 洗禮 (Xǐlǐ)

**Pinyin:** Gěi rén shī-xǐ / Shòu-xǐ / Xǐ-lǐ

**Literal Meaning:**
- 給人施洗 = "give baptism to people" (active - baptizing others)
- 受洗 = "receive washing" (passive - being baptized)
- 洗禮 = "washing ceremony/rite" (noun form)

**Register:** Formal/liturgical

**Cultural Context:**
- Protestant churches in Taiwan use 洗禮 (xǐlǐ) for the sacrament
- Catholics use 領洗 (lǐngxǐ = "receive washing") or 聖洗 (shèngxǐ = "holy washing")
- The Zume materials emphasize the active form 給人施洗 (baptizing others) reflecting the Great Commission
- Taiwan Presbyterians have practiced baptism since the 19th century

**Notes:**
- The active/passive distinction is important:
  - 施洗 (shīxǐ) = to baptize (someone else)
  - 受洗 (shòuxǐ) = to be baptized (oneself)
- 給人施洗 literally "give to people the washing" - emphasizes the action of baptizing

**Examples:**

| English | Traditional Chinese | Pinyin | Context |

## Cultural and Contextual Notes

### Taiwan Christian Context

**Historical Background:**
- Christianity arrived in Taiwan primarily through Presbyterian missionaries in the 19th century (1865 onwards)
- Presbyterian missionary George Leslie Mackay and others established churches, schools, and hospitals
- Taiwan's Christian terminology reflects this Presbyterian heritage
- The Chinese Union Version Bible (和合本, 1919) is the standard Bible translation used

**Traditional vs. Simplified Characters:**
- Taiwan uses Traditional Chinese characters (繁體字)
- Mainland China uses Simplified Chinese characters (简体字)
- Hong Kong also uses Traditional characters but with some regional variations
- The spiritual terminology is largely the same, but characters differ

**Religious Landscape:**
- Taiwan is predominantly Buddhist/Taoist (around 35-40% identify as such)
- Christians make up about 5-6% of the population
- Folk religion and ancestor worship remain influential
- Christian terminology must navigate this multi-religious context

**Language Characteristics:**
- Taiwan Mandarin has some pronunciation and vocabulary differences from Mainland Mandarin
- Many Taiwanese also speak Taiwanese Hokkien (閩南語)
- Older church members may be more familiar with Taiwanese Hokkien Christian terms
- The Zume materials use standard Mandarin that all Taiwan Christians can understand

### Translation Choices and Theological Implications

**God Terminology:**
- The use of both 神 (Shén) and 上帝 (Shàngdì) reflects Taiwan Protestant flexibility
- 上帝 was preferred by early missionaries to avoid Buddhist/Taoist associations
- Modern usage shows comfort with both terms
- Context determines which is more appropriate

**Baptism Terminology:**
- Emphasis on 給人施洗 (baptizing others) rather than just 受洗 (being baptized)
- Reflects Zume's focus on empowering every believer to baptize
- Challenges traditional clergy-centered baptism practices

**Disciple-Making Language:**
- Multiple active verbs for making disciples: 使...成為, 帶出, 培養
- Taiwan usage prefers dynamic, action-oriented language
- Reflects cultural value of mentorship and apprenticeship

**Obedience Terms:**
- Three different terms for obedience (順服, 遵守, 遵行) show nuance
- Reflects comprehensive understanding of obedience: heart attitude, keeping commands, and practical action
- Taiwan Christian culture values both internal and external obedience

### Comparison with Other Chinese Regions

**Mainland China (zh_CN):**
- Uses simplified characters
- Some terminology differences due to different missionary influences and political history
- 上帝 is more common in official church contexts
- Generally mutually intelligible with Taiwan usage

**Hong Kong (zh_Hant_HK):**
- Uses traditional characters like Taiwan
- Some British colonial influence on terminology
- Generally very similar to Taiwan usage
- May use some Cantonese-influenced terms in spoken contexts

### Cultural Sensitivity Notes

**Buddhist/Taoist Overlay:**
- Terms like 福 (blessing), 靈 (spirit), 經 (scripture) exist in Buddhist/Taoist contexts
- Christian usage contextualizes these culturally familiar concepts
- The prefix 聖 (holy) often distinguishes Christian terms from general religious usage
- Translators must be aware of potential confusion or syncretism

**Family and Community Values:**
- Taiwan culture highly values family relationships (家庭, 世代, 關係)
- Christian community is often described using family language
- The concept of 屬靈家庭 (spiritual family) resonates deeply
- Multi-generational thinking (世世代代) is culturally natural

**Honor and Respect:**
- Taiwan culture emphasizes respect for authority and elders
- Christian leadership terms reflect servant leadership while maintaining respect
- The balance between humility and authority is culturally important
