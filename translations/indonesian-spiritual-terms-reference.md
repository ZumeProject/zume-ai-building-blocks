## Overview

This document catalogs key spiritual terms and their Indonesian translations as used in the Zume training materials. The translation strategy uses Islamic-friendly terminology that bridges Christian and Islamic contexts, making the content accessible to Indonesian speakers from diverse religious backgrounds.

## Core Deity Terms

### Jesus
- **Indonesian:** "Isa" or "Isa Almasih"
- **Notes:**
  - Uses "Isa" (the Islamic/Arabic form) rather than "Yesus" (more common Indonesian Christian form)
  - Often appears with honorific "AS" (Alaihissalam - peace be upon him)
  - Full title: "Isa Almasih AS"
- **Examples:**
  - "Learn how to obey Jesus' commands" → "Pelajari bagaimana menaati perintah Isa"
  - "How to Follow Jesus" → "Bagaimana Mengikuti Isa Almasih AS"
  - "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "Apa itu pengikut Isa Almasih?"
  - "Becoming more like Jesus... Together" → "Menjadi lebih seperti Isa... Bersama-sama"

### Christ
- **Indonesian:** "Almasih" (Messiah)
- **Notes:** Not used standalone, but combined with "Isa" as "Isa Almasih"
- **Pattern:** Always appears as part of the full title "Isa Almasih"

### God
- **Indonesian:** "Allah"
- **Notes:** Consistently uses "Allah" (common in both Islamic and Indonesian Christian contexts)
- **Examples:**
  - "God Uses Ordinary People" → "Allah Memakai Orang-orang Biasa"
  - "Hearing and Obeying God" → "Mendengar dan Menaati Allah"
  - "Sharing God's Story" → "Membagikan Kisah Allah"
  - "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "Menjadi serorang murid berarti kita mendengar dan menaati Allah"

### Holy Spirit
- **Indonesian:** "Roh Suci Allah" (literally: Holy Spirit of God)
- **Notes:** Always includes "Allah" (of God) with "Roh Suci"
- **Examples:**
  - "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together" → "Minta Roh Suci Allah untuk memimpin waktu kalian bersama"

### Spirit
- **Indonesian:** "Roh" (spirit - noun) or "Rohani" (spiritual - adjective)
- **Examples:**
  - "Spiritual Breathing" → "Pernapasan Rohani"
  - "Spiritual Economy" → "Kebutuhan Ekonomi Rohani"

### Lord
- **Indonesian:** "Allah"
- **Notes:** Uses "Allah" rather than "Tuhan" (Lord/Master) in this context
- **Examples:**
  - "The Lord's Supper" → "Perjamuan Allah"

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
- **Indonesian:** "Murid" (student/learner)
- **Plural forms:** "murid-murid" or "para murid"
- **Examples:**
  - "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "Pengertian Sederhana dari Murid dan Jemaah"
  - "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples" → "Siklus Pelatihan untuk Mendewasakan para Murid"
  - "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God" → "Menjadi serorang murid berarti kita mendengar dan menaati Allah"

### Disciple-making / Discipleship
- **Indonesian:** "Pemuridan" (noun form) or "menjadikan-pengikut" (verb phrase)
- **Notes:** Two variations are used interchangeably
- **Examples:**
  - "Duckling Discipleship" → "Pemuridan Anak Bebek"
  - "Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making" → "Pelajari apa hubungan anak bebek dengan pemuridan" (also: "menjadikan-pengikut")
  - "Train disciples how to make disciples" → "Melatih murid bagaimana membuat murid"

### Follower
- **Indonesian:** "Pengikut"
- **Examples:**
  - "What is a follower of Jesus?" → "Apa itu pengikut Isa Almasih?"
  - "What are the activities of a follower of Jesus?" → "Apa saja kegiatan-kegiatan pengikut Isa Almasih?"

## Church Terms

### Church
- **Indonesian:** "Jemaah" or "Jemaah Allah"
- **Notes:**
  - Uses "Jemaah" (Islamic term for congregation/assembly) rather than "Gereja" (church building)
  - Sometimes specified as "Jemaah Allah" (God's congregation)
- **Examples:**
  - "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church" → "Pengertian Sederhana dari Murid dan Jemaah"
  - "Disciples and the Church" → "Murid-murid dan Jemaah"
  - "Always Part of Two Churches" → "Selalu Menjadi Bagian dari Dua Jemaah"
  - "Churches Needed" → "Jemaah Allah Dibutuhkan"
  - "Churches Reported" → "Jemaah Allah Dilaporkan"

### Simple Church
- **Indonesian:** "Jemaah sederhana" or "Jemaah Allah sederhana"
- **Examples:**
  - "Always report new simple churches" → "Selalu laporkan jemaah Allah sederhana baru"
  - "2 simple churches per 5,000 people" → "2 jemaah sederhana per 5.000 orang"

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
- **Indonesian:** "Injil" or "Kabar Baik"
- **Notes:**
  - Two forms used interchangeably
  - "Injil" is more formal/religious
  - "Kabar Baik" is literal translation of "Good News"
- **Examples:**
  - "The Gospel and How to Share It" → "Kabar Baik dan Bagaimana Cara Membagikannya"
  - "3-Circles Gospel Presentation" → "Presentasi Injil 3 Lingkaran"
  - "The Gospel" → "Injil"
  - "Share the Gospel" → "Memberitakan Injil"

### Bible
- **Indonesian:** "Alkitab" or "Kitab Suci"
- **Notes:** Both terms used interchangeably (Kitab Suci = Holy Scripture)
- **Examples:**
  - "Personal Bible Study" → "Pelajaran Alkitab Pribadi"
  - "Bible Reading" → "Membaca Kitab Suci"
  - "S.O.A.P.S. Bible Study" → "Penelaahan Kitab Suci S.O.A.P.S"

### Scripture
- **Indonesian:** "Kitab Suci" (Holy Scripture)
- **Pattern:** Consistently uses "Kitab Suci"

## Key Translation Principles

### 1. Islamic-Friendly Terminology
The translation intentionally uses terminology that bridges Christian and Islamic contexts:
- **"Isa"** instead of "Yesus" for Jesus
- **"Allah"** for God (universal in Indonesian)
- **"Jemaah"** instead of "Gereja" for church
- **"Permandian"** instead of "Baptisan" for baptism

### 2. Honorific Usage
- **"AS" (Alaihissalam)** - Islamic honorific meaning "peace be upon him" is used with "Isa Almasih AS"

### 3. Consistency in Core Terms
Key terms are used consistently throughout the translation:
- **Murid** for disciple
- **Allah** for God
- **Injil** or **Kabar Baik** for gospel
- **Jemaah** for church

### 4. Dual Translations for Flexibility
Some terms have two acceptable translations:
- **Gospel:** "Injil" (formal) or "Kabar Baik" (descriptive)
- **Bible:** "Alkitab" or "Kitab Suci"
- **Disciple-making:** "Pemuridan" or "menjadikan-pengikut"

### 5. Practical Ministry Focus
The vocabulary emphasizes practical concepts:
- **Pelatihan** (training)
- **Pelipatgandaan** (multiplication)
- **Kepemimpinan** (leadership)
- Rather than abstract theological terminology

## Translation Strategy Summary

This Indonesian translation appears designed to be **accessible to speakers from both Christian and Muslim backgrounds** by:

1. Using **shared religious vocabulary** where possible
2. Employing **Islamic-respectful terms** for Jesus and church contexts
3. Focusing on **practical discipleship concepts** rather than denominational theology
4. Maintaining **consistency** in core terminology
5. Allowing **flexibility** with dual translations where culturally appropriate

When coaching further translation work, maintain this strategy to ensure consistency with the existing translation and preserve its cross-cultural accessibility.

## Recommended Usage Guidelines

### DO:
- Use "Isa" or "Isa Almasih AS" for Jesus
- Use "Allah" for God
- Use "Jemaah" or "Jemaah Allah" for church
- Use "Murid" for disciple
- Use "Injil" or "Kabar Baik" for gospel
- Use "Permandian" for baptism
- Maintain the Islamic-friendly terminology consistently

### DON'T:
- Switch to "Yesus" or "Gereja" (more exclusively Christian terms)
- Introduce theological terms not already present in the translation
- Use "Tuhan" for Lord when "Allah" is the established pattern
- Over-theologize simple, practical concepts
