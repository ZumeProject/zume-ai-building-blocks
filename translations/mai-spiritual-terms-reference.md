## Core Deity Terms

### God
**Translation**: परमेश्वर (Parameśvar)
**Transliteration**: Parmeshvar
**Etymology**: Sanskrit compound: परम (param = supreme) + ईश्वर (īśvar = lord, god)
**Type**: Sanskrit loanword - standard term in Indic languages

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "God Uses Ordinary People"
   **Maithili**: परमेश्वर साधारण लोकक प्रयोग करैत छथि
   _"Parmeshvar sadharan lokok prayog karait chhathi"_

2. **English**: "God makes plans, and He expects us to make plans, too."
   **Maithili**: परमेश्वर योजना बनबैत छथि, आ ओ अपेक्षा करैत छथि जे हम सभ सेहो योजना बनाबी।
   _Plans and expectations in God's work_

3. **English**: "See how God uses ordinary people doing simple things to make a big impact."
   **Maithili**: देखब जे परमेश्वर कोना साधारण काज करय वाला साधारण लोक के उपयोग कs बड़का प्रभाव डालय छथिन्ह
   _Demonstrative use showing God's methodology_

4. **English**: "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God."
   **Maithili**: शिष्य होयबाक मतलब अछि जे हम परमेश्वर सँ सुनैत छी आ हुनकर आज्ञा मानैत छी।
   _Relational aspect of hearing from God_

5. **English**: "Spiritual Breathing is Hearing and Obeying God"
   **Maithili**: आध्यात्मिक श्वास लबाक मतलब अछि परमेश्वर के सुनब आ मानब।
   _Spiritual practice related to God_

**Grammatical Notes**:
- Consistently uses honorific verb forms (छथि/chhathi) when referring to God
- Often paired with possessive "हुनकर" (hunkar = His) using honorific forms
- The term परमेश्वर is used universally without variation

### Jesus / Christ
**Translation**: यीशु (Yīśu)
**Transliteration**: Yishu
**Etymology**: Transliteration from English/Greek via Hindi
**Type**: Standard Christian terminology

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Learn four key ways God makes everyday followers more like Jesus."
   **Maithili**: अहाँ चारि प्रमुख तरीका जानब जेहिसँ परमेश्वर दैनिक अनुयायी सभ के यीशु जेकाँ बनबैत छथि।
   _"Making followers like Jesus"_

2. **English**: "Jesus said, 'Therefore go and make disciples...'"
   **Maithili**: यीशु कहलथिन, एहि लेल अहाँ सभ आब जा कऽ सभ जातिक लोक केँ हमर शिष्‍य बनाउ और ओकरा सभ केँ पिता, पुत्र आ पवित्र आत्‍माक नाम सँ बपतिस्‍मा दिऔक।
   _Great Commission quote_

3. **English**: "Learn how to share your testimony in three minutes, sharing how Jesus has impacted your life."
   **Maithili**: ई सीखू जे तीन मिनट मे अपन गवाही कोना बाँटि सकैत छी, ई साझा क' क' जे यीशु अहाँक जीवन केँ कोना प्रभावित केने छथि।
   _Personal testimony about Jesus_

4. **English**: "Learn a simple pattern for making not just one follower of Jesus, but an entire spiritual family"
   **Maithili**: यीशु केरऽ खाली एगो अनुयायी नै बल्कि पूरा आध्यात्मिक परिवार बनाबै के एगो सरल पैटर्न सीखऽ जे आबै वाला पीढ़ी लेली बढ़ी जाय छै।
   _Building spiritual families through Jesus_

5. **English**: "A simple way to celebrate our close and continuing relationship with Jesus."
   **Maithili**: ई हमर यीशु सँ घनिष्ठ संबंध आ सतत संबंध केँ मनाबय के सरल तरीका अछि।
   _Ongoing relationship with Jesus_

**Grammatical Notes**:
- Uses honorific verb forms (कहलथिन/kahalthini = said [honorific])
- No separate term for "Christ" appears in the translations
- Consistently uses यीशु without variation

### Holy Spirit
**Translation**: पवित्र आत्मा (Pavitra Ātmā)
**Transliteration**: Pavitra Atma
**Etymology**: Sanskrit: पवित्र (pavitra = holy, pure) + आत्मा (ātmā = spirit, soul)
**Type**: Sanskrit compound - standard Christian terminology

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Baptize them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"
   **Maithili**: पिता, पुत्र आ पवित्र आत्‍माक नाम सँ बपतिस्‍मा दिऔक
   _Trinity formula_

2. **English**: "Ask God's Holy Spirit to lead your time together."
   **Maithili**: प्रार्थना करू जे परमेश्वरक पवित्र आत्मा अहाँक सभक संगत समय के नेतृत्व करू।
   _Prayer for guidance_

3. **English**: "Start with prayer. Spiritual understanding and transformation is not possible without the Holy Spirit."
   **Maithili**: प्रार्थना सँ शुरू करू। आत्मिक समझदारी आ रूपांतरण पवित्र आत्मा केँ बिना संभव नै अछि।
   _Necessity of the Holy Spirit_

4. **English**: "Thank God and ask His Holy Spirit to lead your time."
   **Maithili**: परमेश्वर केँ धन्यवाद दिअ आ हुनकर पवित्र आत्मा सँ आग्रह करू जे ओ अहाँक समय के नेतृत्व करू।
   _Gratitude and request for guidance_

5. **English**: "The Holy Spirit is leading His people through Zume"
   **Maithili**: पवित्र आत्मा अपनऽ लोगऽ क॑ दुनिया भर म॑ ज़ुमे के माध्यम स॑ शिष्य बनाबै के तरफ ले जाय रहलऽ छै।
   _Active leading by the Spirit_

**Grammatical Notes**:
- पवित्र आत्मा used consistently without variation
- Associated with leadership, guidance, and transformation
- Grammatically treated with honorific forms

### Trinity References

**Father (पिता - Pitā)**:
- Standard term for "father" used in Trinitarian formula
- Example: पिता, पुत्र आ पवित्र आत्‍माक नाम सँ (in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit)

**Son (पुत्र - Putra)**:
- Standard Sanskrit-derived term for "son"
- Used specifically in Trinity context
- Example: Same Trinitarian formula as above

**Note**: These terms appear primarily in the Great Commission baptismal formula and are not extensively used elsewhere in the translation.

## Discipleship Terms

### Disciple
**Translation**: शिष्य (Śiṣya)
**Transliteration**: Shishya
**Etymology**: Sanskrit शिष्य (śiṣya = student, learner, disciple)
**Type**: Classical Sanskrit term - widely used in Hindu, Buddhist, and Christian contexts

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Disciples and the Church"
   **Maithili**: शिष्य आ कलीसिया
   _Basic pairing with church_

2. **English**: "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"
   **Maithili**: शिष्य एवं चर्च की सरल परिभाषा
   _Defining disciples_

3. **English**: "Discover the essence of being a disciple, making disciples, and what is the church."
   **Maithili**: शिष्य बनय के सार के खोज करू, शिष्य बनाबय के, आओर कलीसिया की अछि.
   _Essence of discipleship_

4. **English**: "Being a disciple means we hear from God and we obey God."
   **Maithili**: शिष्य होयबाक मतलब अछि जे हम परमेश्वर सँ सुनैत छी आ हुनकर आज्ञा मानैत छी।
   _Definition: hearing and obeying_

5. **English**: "Training Cycle for Maturing Disciples"
   **Maithili**: परिपक्व शिष्य के लिये प्रशिक्षण चक्र
   _Mature disciples_

6. **English**: "1 trained multiplying disciple per 5,000 in the United States"
   **Maithili**: 1 प्रशिक्षित गुणा शिष्य प्रति 5,000 अमेरिका मे
   _Multiplying disciples metric_

**Grammatical Notes**:
- शिष्य is used consistently for both singular and plural contexts
- Forms compound concepts: "multiplying disciple" = गुणा शिष्य
- Associated with verbs: बनय (become), बनाबय (make), होयबाक (being)

### Discipleship
**Translation**: शिष्यत्व (Śiṣyatva)
**Transliteration**: Shishyatva
**Etymology**: Sanskrit शिष्य (disciple) + त्व (-tva = -ness, abstract noun suffix)
**Type**: Sanskrit abstract noun formation

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Duckling Discipleship – Leading Immediately"
   **Maithili**: बत्तख शिष्यत्व – तुरन्त नेतृत्व करब
   _Immediate discipleship model_

2. **English**: "Learn what ducklings have to do with disciple-making."
   **Maithili**: बतकछौड़ा सभक शिष्य बनबामे की संबंध अछि, से जानू।
   _Disciple-making process_

3. **English**: "Discipleship Concepts"
   **Maithili**: शिष्यत्व के सिद्धांत
   _Foundational concepts_

**Grammatical Notes**:
- शिष्यत्व specifically refers to the state/quality of being a disciple
- Distinguished from शिष्य (the person) and शिष्य बनाबय (the action of making disciples)

### Follower
**Translation**: अनुयायी (Anuyāyī)
**Transliteration**: Anuyayi
**Etymology**: Sanskrit अनु (anu = after, following) + या (yā = to go) + ई (-ī = agent suffix)
**Type**: Sanskrit-derived term meaning "one who follows"

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Learn four key ways God makes everyday followers more like Jesus."
   **Maithili**: अहाँ चारि प्रमुख तरीका जानब जेहिसँ परमेश्वर दैनिक अनुयायी सभ के यीशु जेकाँ बनबैत छथि।
   _Everyday followers_

2. **English**: "Making not just one follower of Jesus"
   **Maithili**: यीशु केरऽ खाली एगो अनुयायी नै
   _Single follower_

3. **English**: "3/3 Group is a way for followers of Jesus to meet"
   **Maithili**: 3/3 समूह यीशु के अनुयायी के लेलऽ एगो तरीका छै
   _Followers gathering_

4. **English**: "The Bible tells us that every follower of Jesus will one day be held accountable"
   **Maithili**: बाइबिल हमरा सभ केँ कहैत अछि जे यीशुक हरेक अनुयायी एक दिन हमरा सभक काज आ कहब आ सोचबाक लेल जवाबदेह ठहराओल जायत।
   _Accountability of followers_

**Usage Pattern**:
- अनुयायी is used interchangeably with शिष्य in many contexts
- अनुयायी emphasizes the "following" aspect
- शिष्य emphasizes the "learning/training" aspect
- Both terms carry legitimate theological weight

## Church Terms

### Church
**Translation**: चर्च (Carc) / कलीसिया (Kalīsiyā)
**Transliteration**: Church / Kalisia
**Type**: Two terms used with distinct connotations

**Etymology**:
- **चर्च** (Carc): Direct phonetic borrowing from English "church"
- **कलीसिया** (Kalīsiyā): Greek ἐκκλησία (ekklēsía) via Hindi

**Usage Pattern**:
The translation employs BOTH terms with interesting distinctions:

**चर्च (Church)** - Used for:
- Institutional/organizational references
- Counting and metrics
- Physical or organizational entities

**कलीसिया (Kalisia)** - Used for:
- Theological/spiritual concepts
- Biblical definitions
- Spiritual family/community sense

**Examples of चर्च (Church)**:
1. **English**: "Churches Needed"
   **Maithili**: चर्च सभक आवश्यकता

2. **English**: "Churches Reported"
   **Maithili**: रिपोर्ट कएल गेल चर्च

3. **English**: "Churches"
   **Maithili**: चर्च सभ

4. **English**: "Church Name"
   **Maithili**: चर्च के नाम

5. **English**: "Church Location"
   **Maithili**: चर्च के स्थान

6. **English**: "2 simple churches per 5,000 people in the United States"
   **Maithili**: संयुक्त राज्य मे प्रत्येक 5,000 लोक पर 2 सरल चर्च।

7. **English**: "Always report new simple churches that you are starting."
   **Maithili**: हमेशा नव साधारण चर्च के रिपोर्ट करू जे अहाँ शुरू क रहल छी।

**Examples of कलीसिया (Kalisia)**:
1. **English**: "Disciples and the Church"
   **Maithili**: शिष्य आ कलीसिया

2. **English**: "Simple Definition of Disciple and Church"
   **Maithili**: शिष्य एवं चर्च की सरल परिभाषा
   _(Note: This uses चर्च in the title but discusses कलीसिया concept)_

3. **English**: "Discover what is the church."
   **Maithili**: शिष्य बनय के सार के खोज करू, शिष्य बनाबय के, आओर कलीसिया की अछि.
   _(Defining the essence of church)_

4. **English**: "Always Part of Two Churches"
   **Maithili**: हमेशा दुइटा कलीसिया केर हिस्सा बनू।
   _(Spiritual membership concept)_

5. **English**: "Learn how multiplying churches remain connected"
   **Maithili**: सीखू जे कोना गुणा करय वाला कलीसिया जुड़ल रहैत अछि आओर एकटा विस्तारित, आध्यात्मिक परिवार के रूप मे एक संग जीवन जीबैत अछि.
   _(Spiritual family and multiplication)_

6. **English**: "3/3 Group is not just a small group but a simple church"
   **Maithili**: एहि तरहेँ 3/3 ग्रुप मात्र छोट समूह नहि अपितु एकटा साधारण चर्च अछि |
   _(Defining what constitutes church)_

**Translation Strategy Note**:
This bilingual approach allows the translators to:
- Use चर्च for practical, organizational, and quantifiable aspects
- Reserve कलीसिया for theological, spiritual, and relational dimensions
- Maintain both Western and Indian Christian terminology
- Connect with different audience backgrounds

## Gospel and Scripture Terms

### Gospel
**Translation**: सुसमाचार (Susamācār)
**Transliteration**: Susamachar
**Etymology**: Sanskrit सु (su = good) + समाचार (samācār = news, message)
**Type**: Sanskrit compound - standard Christian term meaning "good news"

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "The Gospel and How to Share It"
   **Maithili**: सुसमाचार आ एकरा कोना बाँटल जाय

2. **English**: "Learn the way to share God's Gospel from the beginning of humanity all the way to the end of this age."
   **Maithili**: मानवता केरऽ शुरुआत स॑ ल॑ क॑ ई युग केरऽ अंत तक परमेश्वर केरऽ सुसमाचार साझा करै के तरीका सीखऽ।

3. **English**: "3-Circles Gospel Presentation"
   **Maithili**: तीन वृत्त सुसमाचार प्रस्तुति

4. **English**: "The 3 Circles Gospel Presentation is a way to share the Gospel through a simple picture"
   **Maithili**: 3-गो वृत्त सुसमाचार प्रस्तुति एक एहन तरीका छी, जाहिसँ सुसमाचार केँ एक सरल चित्रक माध्यम सँ कहल जाइत अछि

5. **English**: "Share the Gospel"
   **Maithili**: सुसमाचार साझा करू

**Grammatical Notes**:
- सुसमाचार used consistently without variation
- Often paired with verbs: साझा करू (share), बाँटल (distribute), कहल (tell)
- Associated with visual presentation methods (3 Circles)

### Bible / Scripture
**Translation**: बाइबिल (Bāibil) / धर्मशास्त्र (Dharmaśāstra)
**Transliteration**: Baibil / Dharmashastra
**Type**: Dual terminology approach

**बाइबिल (Baibil)**:
- Direct transliteration from English
- More commonly used in the translation
- Familiar to Christian communities

**धर्मशास्त्र (Dharmashastra)**:
- Sanskrit: धर्म (dharma = religion, righteousness) + शास्त्र (śāstra = scripture, treatise)
- More formal, traditional term
- Connects to Hindu concept of sacred texts but Christianized

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Bible Reading"
   **Maithili**: बाइबिल पाठ

2. **English**: "Scripture"
   **Maithili**: धर्मशास्त्र (बाइबल) के अध्ययन
   _(Note: Provides both terms with बाइबिल in parentheses)_

3. **English**: "A tool for daily Bible study that helps you understand, obey, and share God's Word."
   **Maithili**: दैनिक बाइबिल अध्ययन के एक साधन जे अहाँ के परमेश्वर के वचन के बुझबाक, पालन करबाक आ बाँटबाक मदद करैत अछि।

4. **English**: "Use Matthew 6:9-13 to work through the SOAPS Bible study pattern personally."
   **Maithili**: मत्ती 6:9-13 के उपयोग क SOAPS बाइबिल अध्ययन पैटर्न के माध्यम स व्यक्तिगत रूप स काज करू।

5. **English**: "Areas of discipleship (such as Bible reading/understanding)"
   **Maithili**: शिष्यत्वक कोन क्षेत्र (जइसे — बाइबिल पढ़नाई/समझनाई, प्रार्थना करनाई, परमेश्वरक कथा साझा करनाई आदि)

6. **English**: "Encourage them to start their daily S.O.A.P.S. Bible reading practice"
   **Maithili**: हुनका अपन S.O.A.P.S. बाइबिल पठन अभ्यास प्रतिदिन शुरू करबाक लेल प्रेरित करू

**Usage Pattern**:
- बाइबिल is the primary term used throughout
- धर्मशास्त्र appears in more formal contexts
- Often paired with अध्ययन (study) and पाठ (reading)

## Sacraments and Practices

### Baptism
**Translation**: बपतिस्मा (Baptismā) / बपतिस्‍मा (alternate spelling)
**Transliteration**: Baptisma
**Etymology**: Greek βάπτισμα (baptisma) via Hindi/English
**Type**: Standard Christian transliteration

**Examples in Context**:
1. **English**: "Baptism and How To Do It"
   **Maithili**: बपतिस्मा आ कोना करब

2. **English**: "Baptism"
   **Maithili**: बपतिस्‍मा

## Translation Patterns and Analysis

### Overall Translation Strategy

The Maithili translation of Zume materials demonstrates a **sophisticated bilingual approach** that balances several priorities:

#### 1. **Language Mixing Strategy**
The translation employs strategic code-mixing between:
- **Classical Sanskrit terms** (for theological/spiritual concepts)
- **Modern Hindi loanwords** (for contemporary concepts)
- **English transliterations** (for technical terms, brand names, and international consistency)
- **Native Maithili grammar and syntax** (verb forms, particles, connectors)

**Examples**:
- चर्च (English "church") + कलीसिया (Greek ekklesia via Hindi)
- प्रशिक्षण (Sanskrit "training") + कोचिंग (English "coaching")
- Model, Assist, Watch, Leave retained in English with Maithili explanations

#### 2. **Dual Terminology for Key Concepts**
Several important concepts maintain TWO terms with distinct nuances:

| English | Term 1 | Usage | Term 2 | Usage |
|---------|--------|-------|--------|-------|
| Church | चर्च (Carc) | Organizational, countable | कलीसिया (Kalisia) | Theological, spiritual |
| Spiritual | आध्यात्मिक (Adhyatmik) | Formal, complete | आत्मिक (Atmik) | Shortened, colloquial |
| Bible | बाइबिल (Baibil) | Common usage | धर्मशास्त्र (Dharmashastra) | Formal scripture |
| Share | साझा करब (Sajha) | Communal sharing | बाँटब (Bantab) | Active distribution |
| Obey | आज्ञा मानब (Aagya mana) | Accept command | पालन करब (Palan karab) | Follow/observe |

This dual terminology allows the translation to:
- Connect with different audience backgrounds (Hindu vs. Christian terminology)
- Distinguish between abstract concepts and concrete practices
- Maintain both local and international Christian vocabulary

#### 3. **Honorific System**
The translation consistently uses **Maithili honorific grammar** when referring to God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit:

**Regular vs. Honorific Verb Forms**:
- Regular: करैत अछि (karait achhi = is doing)
- Honorific: करैत छथि (karait chhathi = is doing [honorific])

**Examples**:
- परमेश्वर बनबैत **छथि** (God makes [honorific])
- यीशु कहलथिन (Jesus said [honorific past tense])
- ओ अपेक्षा करैत **छथि** (He expects [honorific])

This grammatical respect mirrors the honorific systems in Hindi and shows cultural appropriateness.

#### 4. **Compound Formation Patterns**

**Sanskrit-Based Compounds**:
The translation freely creates compounds following Sanskrit rules:
- आध्यात्मिक + परिवार = आध्यात्मिक परिवार (spiritual family)
- गुणा + शिष्य = गुणा शिष्य (multiplying disciple)
- पीढ़ी + गत = पीढ़ीगत (generational)
- समकक्ष + मेंटरिंग = समकक्ष मेंटरिंग (peer mentoring)

**Descriptive Compounds**:
- प्रार्थना यात्रा (prayer + journey = prayer walking)
- दृष्टि मानचित्रण (vision + mapping = vision mapping)
- प्रशिक्षण चक्र (training + cycle = training cycle)

#### 5. **Abstract Noun Formation**

**Using -त्व (-tva) Suffix** (Sanskrit abstract noun maker):
- शिष्य (disciple) → शिष्यत्व (discipleship)
- नेता (leader) → नेतृत्व (leadership)
- उत्तर-दाय (answerable) → उत्तरदायित्व (accountability)

**Using -ता (-tā) Suffix**:
- उपभोक् (consumer) → उपभोक्ता (consumer [noun])
- उत्पाद (product) → उत्पादक (producer)

#### 6. **Verb Construction Patterns**

**Light Verb Construction** (common in Hindi/Maithili):
Base verb + करब (karab = to do):
- साझा करब (share-do = to share)
- प्रार्थना करब (prayer-do = to pray)
- अभ्यास करब (practice-do = to practice)
- पालन करब (observance-do = to obey)

**Command/Imperative Forms**:
- करू (karu = do!)
- देखू (dekhu = see!/watch!)
- सीखू (sikhu = learn!)
- मानू (manu = obey!)

#### 7. **Theological Term Selection**

**Preference for Pan-Indian Religious Vocabulary**:
The translation favors terms that are:
- Recognizable across Hindu, Buddhist, and Christian contexts
- Sanskrit-based (prestigious, formal)
- Already established in Hindi Christian usage

**Examples**:
- परमेश्वर (Parmeshvar = God) - used across religions
- शिष्य (Shishya = disciple) - used in Hindu, Buddhist, Christian contexts
- आत्मा (Atma = soul/spirit) - universal Indic concept
- धर्म (Dharma) - embedded in धर्मशास्त्र (Scripture)

This approach:
- Makes the message accessible to non-Christians
- Provides theological bridges for evangelism
- Respects the religious culture of the region

#### 8. **Retention of English Terms**

**Categories of English Retention**:

**A. Brand Names/Proper Nouns**:
- Zume (ज़ुमे)
- SOAPS

**B. Technical Terms for International Consistency**:
- Model, Assist, Watch, Leave (MAWL method)
- 3/3 Group
- Network

**C. Modern Concepts Without Easy Sanskrit Equivalents**:
- App (ऐप)
- Coaching (कोचिंग)
- Vision casting (विजन कास्टिंग)

**Translation Philosophy**:
English terms are retained when:
1. They're part of a branded methodology (MAWL, SOAPS)
2. They're more recognizable than translations (coaching)
3. International consistency is valued (3/3 Group)
4. The concepts are modern/technical (app)

### Linguistic Features Specific to Maithili

#### 1. **Postpositions** (instead of prepositions):
- **सँ** (sã) = from, by, with
  - परमेश्वर **सँ** सुनब (hear FROM God)
  - यीशु **सँ** संबंध (relationship WITH Jesus)

- **केँ** (kẽ) = to, for (object marker)
  - शिष्य **केँ** बनाउ (make disciples)
  - परमेश्वर **केँ** सुनब (hear to-God = hear God)

- **के** (ke) = of, possessive
  - परमेश्वर **के** वचन (God's word)
  - यीशु **के** अनुयायी (follower of Jesus)

- **मे** (me) = in, within
  - प्रार्थना **मे** बिताबब (spend IN prayer)
  - समूह **मे** (in the group)

#### 2. **Verb Inflections**:
The translation preserves Maithili verb endings distinct from Hindi:

**Present Habitual**:
- करैत छी (karait chhi = we do)
- सुनैत छी (sunait chhi = we hear)
- मानैत छी (manait chhi = we obey)

**Infinitive**:
- करब (karab = to do)
- सुनब (sunab = to hear)
- मानब (manab = to obey)

**Past Tense**:
- कएलहुँ (kaelhũ = I did)
- देलहुँ (delhũ = I gave)

**Command/Imperative**:
- करू (karũ = do!)
- सुनू (sunũ = hear!)
- देखू (dekhũ = see!)

#### 3. **Copula Variations**:
Maithili uses distinctive copulas:
- अछि (achhi = is/are)
- छथि (chhathi = is/are [honorific])
- छी (chhi = am/are)
- छै (chhai = is [informal])

- **Already-believing** participants

**4. Cultural Adaptation**:
Avoiding terms that:
- Carry heavy **cultural baggage** (sin, hell)

The materials prioritize:
- **Observable actions** (obey, share, train)

- Over abstract theological vocabulary

- Accessible to non-theological audiences

- Relational rather than propositional

- Assumes theological foundation from elsewhere
